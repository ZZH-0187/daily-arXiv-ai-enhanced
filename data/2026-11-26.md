*Louis Henderson,Vladimir Zhdankin*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 数值模拟研究表明，碰撞less等离子体湍流系统存在强烈的蝴蝶效应，即使单个粒子的微小扰动也会在几个动力学时间内导致系统统计相关性丧失。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 蝴蝶效应是混沌系统的核心特征，但此前尚未在碰撞less等离子体湍流中得到详细表征。本研究旨在通过数值模拟探究这类等离子体中微小初始扰动如何引发系统级不稳定，影响湍流等离子体系统的可预测性。

Method: 采用粒子网格（PIC）模拟研究了驱动湍流系统。通过对比基准模拟与施加局部初始扰动（甚至可达单粒子水平）的模拟对，分析扰动的时间演化。建立了结合空间扩散与指数放大的分析模型来描述早期演化，并在不同等离子体β参数和温度条件下进行参数扫描。

Result: 局部扰动会径向传播至整个模拟区域，造成模拟对间差异的指数放大，最终导致电场、电流密度等场量完全失去统计相关性。参数研究表明，扰动大小主要影响早期演化，对最终的解相关状态影响有限。即使微小的数值误差也会在几个动力学时间内使模拟结果变得不可分辨。

Conclusion: 碰撞less等离子体湍流中存在极其强烈且稳健的蝴蝶效应，这对湍流等离子体系统的可预测性提出了重要挑战。系统的长期行为对初始条件极为敏感，微小的数值误差都可能导致模拟结果失去物理意义。

Abstract: The butterfly effect is a fundamental process in chaotic systems, but it has not previously been characterized in detail for a collisionless turbulent plasma. In this work, we apply particle-in-cell (PIC) simulations of driven turbulence to study the emergence of the butterfly effect from a small initial perturbation to the simulated particles. In particular, we compare pairs of (baseline and perturbed) simulations that differ only by a localized initial perturbation (down to the single-particle level). We find that the effect of the localized perturbation spreads radially throughout the PIC simulation, causing a difference between each simulation pair that is exponentially amplified over time within the affected region. The fields, including the electric current density, eventually become statistically decorrelated. We construct an analytical model combining spatial expansion with exponential amplification to approximate the early-time evolution. Varying the initial perturbation primarily affects the early-time evolution, while having little effect on the final, decorrelated state. We describe parameter scans with varying plasma beta and temperature (covering sub-relativistic and ultra-relativistic regimes). Our results show that remarkably small perturbations will grow to decorrelate each pair of simulations within a few dynamical times; the imposed perturbation can no longer be resolved once it falls below the numerical precision floor set by the machine precision. These results confirm that the butterfly effect is very strong and robust in a collisionless plasma, with implications for the predictability of turbulent plasma systems.

### [2] [Global self-organisation via driven interchange turbulence in a hard-core Z-pinch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33618)
*Jonathan Squire,Christopher Hill,Laoisa Huckle*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该论文通过MHD模拟研究了悬浮偶极装置中的等离子体湍流自组织特性。研究发现等离子体和磁场会共同形成连续湍流、有限β值的交换边缘状态，压力密度剖面具有准通用性，但随着β值增加磁场剖面会从真空样衰减变为内部最小值形态。高β状态下会出现m=1不稳定性破坏约束。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统磁约束装置通常抑制湍流输运，但悬浮偶极装置却利用湍流，让等离子体通过自组织过程集中到强场区域。研究旨在了解这种装置中的湍流自组织机制及其对聚变条件的影响。

Method: 使用可压缩理想磁流体动力学（MHD）进行全局模拟，自洽演化磁场，研究驱动硬核心Z箍缩系统。通过二维和三维模拟，分析不同驱动条件和边界条件下的等离子体行为。

Result: 等离子体和磁场形成连续湍流、有限β交换边缘状态；压力密度剖面在不同驱动条件下保持准通用性；随着β增加，磁场剖面从真空样衰减转变为具有内部最小值；高β状态下会出现类似气球模的m=1不稳定性，破坏核心约束。

Conclusion: 悬浮偶极装置中的湍流自组织过程会产生鲁棒的等离子体剖面，设计者对其控制有限，核心与线圈的最终间距由湍流边界层确定。虽然三维波动下仍保持稳定性，但高β状态会受到m=1不稳定性破坏，这为未来设计提供重要参考。

Abstract: Whereas most magnetic-confinement concepts seek to suppress turbulent transport, the levitated dipole exploits it, with plasma turbulently self-organising to concentrate into the stronger-field region near the levitating coil. In a sufficiently large device, this can create the conditions needed for fusion, despite stiff and vigorous transport. We study this process in global simulations of a driven hard-core Z-pinch using compressible ideal magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) with self-consistent evolution of the magnetic field. The results reveal how plasma and field jointly settle onto a continuously turbulent, finite-beta interchange-marginal family. Across a wide drive scan in two-dimensional simulations, quasi-universal pressure and density profiles result, with the magnetic-field profile changing from a decreasing vacuum-like form to one with an interior minimum at increasing beta. These profiles are robust to large changes to the heat and particle sources (including moving both to the outside of the device) as well as to different exhaust and boundary prescriptions. A designer consequently has little direct control over the selected profiles, including the final separation between the pressure maximum and coil, which is set by a turbulent boundary layer. We show that such results persist in the presence of three-dimensional fluctuations at modest beta, but that high-beta states become unstable to a field-parallel m=1 instability that is the analogue of the ballooning instability in a true dipole; this evacuates the core, destroying confinement. Based on a near-marginal expansion and a geometrical remapping, we argue that many features of our results should apply to any shearless closed-field-line system.
