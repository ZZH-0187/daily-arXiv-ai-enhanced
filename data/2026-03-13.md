Abstract: Vlasov-Poisson-Fokker-Planck (VPFP) simulations of large-amplitude electron plasma waves, where the bounce frequency is much larger than the collision frequency, $ω_B \gg ν_\text{ee}$, show that the evolution of these waves exhibits three phases; I.
