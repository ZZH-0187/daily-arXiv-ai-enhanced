### [1] [TeV Electron Beams from Plasma Acceleration via Regenerative Cascading](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07979)
*Chaojie Zhang,Chan Joshi*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 提出通过再生级联的等离子体尾波场加速新方案，利用前级加速的束流作为下一级的驱动束，实现能量倍增而非简单相加，从45 GeV驱动束获得1.1 TeV电子束。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 尽管等离子体加速器能维持比传统射频加速器高数个数量级的加速梯度，但要达到TeV能量通常需要数十级级联，而每级都要求亚微米级对准、飞秒级同步和精密的束流匹配，这些技术挑战限制了实际应用。

Method: 提出再生级联等离子体尾波场加速方案：每级自注入新鲜的尾随电子束，被加速的尾随束成为下一级的驱动束。该方法具有自动对准、同步和匹配的优点，且每级重置尾随束亮度。

Result: 通过粒子网格模拟显示，在亚公里级两级等离子体加速器中，从45 GeV、100 nC驱动束产生1.1 TeV电子束，具有~0.3%均方根能散和0.12 nC电荷。低能散通过后耗尽驱动束演化尾波中的动态束载实现内置能量整流。

Conclusion: 再生级联方案为等离子体加速器提供了一条实现高能、高亮度电子束的有效路径，通过简化复杂的对准、同步和匹配要求，为未来紧凑型TeV级粒子加速器开辟了可能性。

Abstract: Plasma accelerators sustain gradients orders of magnitude higher than conventional radiofrequency machines, but most proposed paths to TeV energies still require tens of stages, each demanding sub-micrometer alignment, femtosecond synchronization, and precise matching of the accelerating trailing bunch. Here we introduce plasma wakefield acceleration via regenerative cascading, in which each stage self-injects a fresh trailing electron bunch and the accelerated trailing bunch becomes the driver for the next stage. This approach has several advantages: energy multiplication instead of addition; automatic alignment, synchronization, and matching of the trailing bunch to the wake; and trailing bunch brightness reset in each stage. Particle-in-cell simulations show the generation of a 1.1 TeV electron beam with ~0.3% rms energy spread and 0.12 nC charge from a two-stage, sub-kilometer plasma accelerator driven by a 45 GeV, 100 nC beam. The low energy spread is achieved via dynamic beam loading in the evolving wake of the post-depletion driver that acts as a built-in energy dechirper.

### [2] [Multi-GeV Electron Combs from a Plasma Wakefield Accelerator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08069)
*Chaojie Zhang,Douglas Storey,Alexander Knetsch,Brendan D. O'Shea,Robert Ariniello,Sébastien Corde,Thamine N. Dalichaouch,Claudio Emma,Ole G. Finnerud,Spencer Gessner,Claire Hansel,Valentina Lee,Carl A. Lindstrøm,Michael Litos,Nathan Majernik,Kenneth A. Marsh,Warren B. Mori,Mark J. Hogan,Chan Joshi*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 该论文展示了一种新型等离子体加速技术，通过在自激发等离子体尾波中进行离子化注入，产生由十多个微束组成、同时在能量和时间上分离的多GeV电子梳


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 等离子体加速器已能产生GeV级电子束，但尚未充分利用其在飞秒或更短时间尺度上原位塑造束流相空间的独特能力。本文旨在探索和展示这种能力，创造具有定制能量-时间结构的电子束

Method: 利用驱动束在其自激发等离子体尾波中的周期性收缩效应，通过嵌入的氦原子连续离子化注入多个微束，同时利用逐渐变化的等离子体密度将每个束团映射到不同的尾波相位，将17厘米区域内捕获的电子压缩成微米级长度的梳状结构

Result: 成功产生了包含十多个微束的多GeV电子梳，单个微束具有百分比级别的能量分布，能量间隔可达10%，每个微束包含几皮库仑电荷。通过束加载分析和粒子-细胞模拟证实，微束持续时间低于飞秒，束团间距为几飞秒

Conclusion: 这项工作首次展示了等离子体加速器中飞秒尺度的原位相空间塑造能力，为实现具有定制能量-时间结构的电子束开辟了道路，为未来精密应用提供了新可能

Abstract: Plasma accelerators now produce GeV-class electron beams with brightness and stability sufficient to drive free-electron lasers. Beyond this, they possess a unique yet largely unexplored capability: shaping the phase space of the beam in situ during injection, on femtosecond or shorter timescales. Here we demonstrate this capability by generating a multi-GeV electron comb comprising more than ten microbunches simultaneously separated in both energy and time. Periodic pinching of the drive beam inside its self-excited plasma wake sequentially injects microbunches via ionization of embedded helium atoms at successive betatron oscillations, while the gently varying plasma density maps each bunchlet to a distinct wake phase, compressing electrons trapped over a ~17 cm region into a comb only micrometers long. Individual microbunches exhibit percent-level energy spreads, energy spacing up to ten percent, and contain several picocoulomb charge. The percent-level spreads and parabolic energy-spacing trend provide experimental evidence for sub-femtosecond microbunch durations and few-femtosecond separations as revealed by beam-loading analysis and confirmed by particle-in-cell simulations. This work demonstrates femtosecond, in-situ phase-space shaping in plasma accelerators, paving the way for electron beams with tailored energy-time structure.

### [3] [Analysis of collisional and facility effects in a magnetic nozzle plasma expansion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07861)
*Manuel Cortés-Hernán,Mario Merino,Diego García-Lahuerta,Eduardo Ahedo*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 提出了一个轴对称、准中性的三流体模型来研究磁场喷嘴中在有中性粒子存在时的等离子体膨胀。该模型通过处理电子能量通量主要为对流型且无需假设异常电阻率来实现电子冷却。研究发现在高磁化条件下，电离过程增强了等离子体流动但降低了电势降落，并且全局电流自由边界条件比局部电流双极性条件更可靠。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究磁场喷嘴中伴随中性粒子（来自等离子体源或均匀背景）存在的等离子体膨胀过程，旨在改进现有模型，特别是更准确地处理电子冷却效应而不依赖异常电阻率的假设。

Method: 提出了轴对称、准中性的三流体模型，将电子、离子和中性粒子作为三种流体处理。模型在高电子磁化极限下求解，其中电子主要参数可沿磁力线积分。考虑了电离、弹性和电荷交换碰撞，但发现这些碰撞并不会改变等离子体膨胀的基本定性特征。讨论了两种电子流边界条件：喷嘴喉部的局部电流双极性条件和外边界（真空室金属壁）的全局电流自由条件。

Result: 1）电离增强了喷嘴中的等离子体流动，导致额外的电子冷却，降低了沿喷嘴的电势降落；2）全局电流自由边界条件（考虑真空室壁影响）比局部电流双极性条件更可靠：它们引入了真空室壁对等离子体膨胀的影响（通过塑造双极性电场），允许外推到不受干扰的自由空间条件，并且更好地近似实验趋势；3）电离、弹性和电荷交换碰撞与中性粒子的相互作用不会改变先前无碰撞模型已知的等离子体膨胀的主要定性特征；4）喷嘴效率可通过磁推力增益和羽流发散角来量化。

Conclusion: 提出的三流体模型成功地研究了磁场喷嘴中伴随中性粒子存在的等离子体膨胀过程。全局电流自由边界条件被证明在物理上更可靠，能够更好地模拟实验现象并考虑外部影响。研究发现尽管考虑了碰撞效应，但等离子体膨胀的基本定性行为与之前无碰撞模型保持一致。

Abstract: An axisymmetric, quasineutral three-fluid model is proposed to study the plasma expansion in a magnetic nozzle under the presence of neutrals coming either from the plasma source or as an homogeneous background. As a difference with other models, electron cooling in the plume is achieved by treating the electron energy flux as mainly convective and without the need to postulate any anomalous resistivity. Solutions are presented for the electron high-magnetization limit, in which the electron main magnitudes can be integrated along magnetic lines. Ionization, elastic and charge-exchange collisions with neutrals do not change the main qualitative features of the plasma expansion, known from previous collisionless models. Ionization enhances the plasma flow in the nozzle, and leads to additional electron cooling, which decreases the electric potential fall along the nozzle. The efficiency of the nozzle is quantified in terms of the gain of magnetic thrust and the plume divergence angle. Two types of boundary conditions are discussed for the electron flow: local current ambipolarity conditions at the nozzle throat and global current-free conditions at the outer boundary (i.e., metallic vacuum chamber walls). These last ones are shown to be physically more reliable: they introduce the influence of the chamber walls on the plasma expansion by shaping the ambipolar electric field; they permit the extrapolation to undisturbed free space conditions; and they approximate better experimental trends with the background pressure.

### [4] [Sensitivity of a low-shear heliotron configuration to localised ferrite perturbations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08290)
*A. Matsuyama,Y. Nakamura,S. Inagaki,F. Tanji,Yusuke Yamashita,Akihisa Yamamoto,S. Kobayashi,F. Kin,S. Kado,S. I. Inagaki,S. Konoshima,T. Mizuuchi,K. Nagasaki*

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 采用点偶极子磁化模型，在螺旋桨J装置真空容器内假设多个位置放置铁素体钢板，通过数值计算评估其对磁配置的影响。特别关注旋转变换和磁岛宽度的变化。

Abstract: The influence of ferritic steel on low-shear stellarator/heliotron magnetic configurations is investigated for the Heliotron J device using a point dipole magnetisation model. This sensitivity arises from the coupling between the background nonaxisymmetric field and ferrite perturbation, rather than being determined solely by the perturbation amplitude. Ferritic steel plates placed on the outer side of a straight section produce the most significant changes in the magnetic topology and exhibit the highest sensitivity to violations of the $M = 4$ toroidal periodicity. Additionally, we show that appropriate arrangements of passive magnetic dipoles can reduce the effective helical ripple while preserving the vacuum magnetic well depth in Heliotron J, and can induce stellaratorasymmetric boundary perturbation in low-field experiments near the ferrite saturation threshold.

### [5] [Precision mapping of laser-driven magnetic fields and their evolution in high-energy-density plasmas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08680)

TL;DR: 使用超快质子照相在激光加速箔片中首次直接观测到瑞利-泰勒不稳定性产生的兆高斯级别磁场，实验结果与2D磁流体模拟一致。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 使用超快质子照相技术测量激光加速平面箔片中瑞利-泰勒不稳定性产生的磁场。薄塑料箔片被~4 kJ、2.5 ns的激光脉冲照射，聚焦强度为~10^14 W/cm^2。靶调制通过激光不均匀性启动，并在瑞利-泰勒不稳定性作用下的目标加速过程中放大。

Target modulations were seeded by laser nonuniformities and amplified during target acceleration by the Rayleigh-Taylor instability. The experimental data show the hydrodynamic evolution of the target and MG-level magnetic fields generated in the broken foil. The experimental data are in good agreement with predictions from 2-D magnetohydrodynamic simulations.
