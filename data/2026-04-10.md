### [1] [Criteria for the economic viability of fusion power plants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07367)
*D. G. Whyte,A. Lo,R. Bielajew,M. Hancock,R. Moeykens,G. Shaw*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 开发了一个评估聚变电厂经济增益的通用框架，包含十个标准化参数，独立于绝对功率和具体技术


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 商业聚变能源需要能够同时评估科学可行性和经济可行性的框架。传统劳森准则只描述聚变科学增益，需要扩展来评估经济效益

Method: 利用时间平衡原理，以及通过能量捕获表面归一化的工程和成本参数，推导出经济增益因子Qecon的非线性方程

Result: 得出的经济增益标准与电厂绝对功率无关，对具体聚变技术不偏不倚，可应用于任何聚变约束概念

Conclusion: 通过广泛变化十个控制参数，该框架为设计、财务和运行权衡提供了高水平见解，有助于改善经济可行性聚变能源的前景

Abstract: Commercial fusion energy requires frameworks to assess both the scientific and economic viability of a wide variety of fusion concepts. Inspired by the Lawson criterion's ability to universally describe fusion energy gain, a generalized framework is developed to determine the economic gain of fusion power plants. The model exploits temporal equilibrium, and engineering and cost parameters normalized to the energy capture surface. The derived criteria for economic gain are therefore independent of the power plant's absolute power, impartial to the particulars of its fusion technology, and can be applied to any fusion confinement concept. The derivation of the economic gain factor, $Q_{econ}$, results in nonlinear equations with ten controlling normalized design parameters ranging from fusion power density and surface component lifetime to energy fluence, price of energy, and component efficiency and cost. These ten controlling parameters are varied over a wide range to provide high-level insights in design, finance and operational tradeoffs that improve the prospects for economically viable fusion energy.

### [2] [Anderson Localization of Ion-Temperature-Gradient Modes and Ion Temperature Clamping in Aperiodic Stellarators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08321)
*Amitava Bhattacharjee*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 本文提出基于安德森局域化的最小化模型，解释stellarator中观察到的离子温度钳制现象，通过AAH框架揭示磁几何非周期性驱动的全局局域化转变及其拓扑特征。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: stellarator实验中普遍观察到的离子温度钳制现象——无论加热功率如何，Ti始终固定在Te的固定比例——需要理论解释。当前模型未能充分解释这一饱和机制，需建立基于非周期磁几何的新物理框架。

Method: 从漂移波的简化流体模型出发，利用stellarator的非周期磁几何特性，将离子温度梯度（ITG）本征值方程转化为可精确求解的Aubry–André–Harper（AAH）差分方程形式。通过Mathieu判别式Δ(ηi)=Tr[M(ηi)]确定连续准周期Hill方程的精确局域化阈值，建立线性不稳定性阈值、安德森局域化阈值和观测钳制值的三阈值排序。

Result: 理论模型表明：非周期曲率谱在气球空间驱动全局局域化转变；AAH框架精确识别转变的拓扑特征——对于不可通约波数比α，所有本征态同时局域化；在临界ηi值以上的安德森局域化低输运区域，观测梯度存在与功率无关的下界。

Conclusion: 安德森局域化为stellarator中的离子温度钳制现象提供了自然解释框架，磁几何的非周期性导致本征态局域化，从而限制湍流输运并产生温度饱和效应。该模型将实验观测与拓扑数学理论相结合，为聚变等离子体湍流控制提供了新视角。

Abstract: Ion temperature clamping -- the saturation of $T_i$ at a fixed fraction of $T_e$ regardless of heating power -- is observed across stellarator experiments. We propose a minimal model based on Anderson localization. Starting from a reduced fluid model for drift waves, we show that the aperiodic magnetic geometry of a stellarator enables us to cast the ion-temperature-gradient (ITG) eigenvalue equation in the form of the Aubry--André--Harper (AAH) difference equation, which is an exactly solvable mathematical model exhibiting Anderson localization. The incommensurate aperiodicity of the curvature spectrum drives a global localization transition in ballooning space. The AAH framework identifies the topological character of the transition exactly: for incommensurate wavenumber ratio $α$, all eigenstates localize simultaneously. For the continuous quasiperiodic Hill equation appropriate to the physical ITG problem, the precise localization threshold is determined by the Mathieu discriminant $Δ(η_i) \equiv \mathrm{Tr}[M(η_i)]$, where $M$ is the transfer matrix, and $η_i = L_n/L_{T_i}$ is the dimensionless ratio of the logarithmic density gradient scale length to the logarithmic ITG scale length. We identify a three-threshold ordering: the linear instability threshold lies below the Anderson localization threshold, which lies below the observed clamp. The Anderson-localized low-transport regime, which lies above a critical value of $η_i$, enforces a power-independent lower bound on the observed gradient.
