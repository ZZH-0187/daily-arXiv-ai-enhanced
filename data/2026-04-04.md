### [1] [High-power Test and System Integration of Direct RF Sampling Based LLRF Control and Monitoring System for S-Band Accelerating Structures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01698)
*Chao Liu,Ankur Dhar,Michael Hoganson,Emilio Nanni,Joseph Olszewski,Emma Snively,Wei-Hou Tan*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 本文介绍了用于下一代S波段加速结构的基于射频片上系统（RFSoC）的新型LLRF系统开发与集成，该系统无需传统模拟混频器，性能显著优于NLCTA升级等应用要求，同时开发了定制固态放大器并成功集成到高功率测试设施中。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 未来粒子加速器对低电平射频（LLRF）控制监测系统的性能、灵活性和成本效益提出了更高要求，传统系统难以满足。为应对S波段加速结构的升级需求，特别是NLCTA等设施的升级，需要开发下一代高性能LLRF系统。

Method: 开发了基于射频片上系统（RFSoC）的下一代LLRF系统，直接对射频脉冲进行采样与合成，无需传统模拟混频器；同时设计了定制固态放大器以满足速调管所需功率级别；并开展了LLRF与固态放大器及高功率测试设施的集成方案。

Result: RFSoC平台性能显著优于目标应用要求；成功实现LLRF与固态放大器在高功率测试设施中的集成；优化了高功率射频驱动系统各阶段的功率水平和射频脉冲稳定性；验证了系统在各集成阶段的测试与表征结果。

Conclusion: 该集成是实现NG-LLRF系统在不同频段测试设施和加速器中全面部署的关键步骤，为未来高性能加速器RF控制系统的实际应用奠定了重要基础。

Abstract: High precision Low-level RF (LLRF) control and monitoring systems for future particle accelerators will be a significant technical challenge as the requirements in performance, flexibility and affordability become increasingly stringent. We have developed an RF system-on-chip (RFSoC) based next-generation LLRF (NG-LLRF) for S-band accelerating structures, which samples and synthesizes the RF pulses directly without the analog mixers used for traditional LLRF systems. The platform delivered considerably better performance than the requirements of the targeted applications, such as the upgrades for Next Linear Collider Test Accelerator (NLCTA) and test facilities at SLAC. As part of the upgrade program, we also developed a custom solid-state amplifier (SSA) to deliver RF pulses at the desired power level of the klystron. Integration of the LLRF with the SSA and the high-power test facility could be challenging. The power levels and RF pulse stability at each stage of the high-power RF drive system must be optimized to deliver the desired RF performance. In this paper, the integration procedure and the test and characterization results at each stage of integration will be summarized, analyzed and discussed. This integration is an essential step for the full deployment of the NG-LLRF system to test facilities and accelerators in different frequency bands.

### [2] [From Galactic Clusters to Plasmas in a Single Monte Carlo: Branching Paths Statistics for Poisson-Vlasov/Boltzmann](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01458)
*Daniel Yaacoub,Gaëtan Brunetto,Stéphane Blanco,Richard Fournier,Gerjan Hagelaar*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 论文在概率路径空间框架下，对自由空间的Poisson-Vlasov和Poisson-Boltzmann系统提出基于连续分枝随机过程的表示方法，并开发了新的分枝反向蒙特卡罗算法，用于引力和等离子体动力学的基准测试。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 目前对介观玻尔兹曼输运与力场的非线性耦合已有路径空间概率表示的研究，但在自由空间下对等离子体物理和引力系统中Poisson-Vlasov和Poisson-Boltzmann系统的概率表示仍有待进一步发展，以实现更有效的基准模拟。

Method: 1. 构造连续概率表示，将路径空间随机过程与Poisson-Vlasov和Poisson-Boltzmann系统关联；2. 发展基于分枝过程的反向蒙特卡罗算法；3. 实现针对引力和等离子体系统的数值模拟方案。

Result: 该方法成功应用于引力和等离子体动力学模拟，实现了高效的统计估计和基准测试，证明了所提出的概率表示和算法的有效性。

Conclusion: 该工作通过构建自由空间下Poisson-Vlasov和Poisson-Boltzmann系统的路径空间概率表示，为这类耦合系统的数值模拟提供了新的概率算法途径，尤其是在引力团簇和等离子体领域展现出应用潜力。

Abstract: Recent advances have allowed to tackle path-space probabilistic representations of mesoscopic Boltzmann transport nonlinearly coupled to a sub-model of the force-field by step forward approaches in terms of continuous branching stochastic processes. In this work, path-space probabilistic representations of free-space Poisson-Vlasov and Poisson-Boltzmann systems are exhibited. This yields novel propagator representations and opens new routes for efficient and reference simulations by use of new branching backward Monte Carlo algorithms. Subsequent statistical estimator are benchmarked on gravitational clusters and plasmas dynamics.

</details>
