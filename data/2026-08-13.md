### [1] [Reconstructive AI Spectroscopy of Charged Particle Beams](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11628)
*Vasily Kozhevnikov,Andrey Kozyrev,Elena Klepalova,Victor Tarasenko,Evgenii Baksht*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 提出一种基于物理原理的神经网络框架，直接从稀疏的衰减曲线数据重建电子能量频谱，成功解析复杂多峰分布。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 解决从稀疏采样的衰减曲线数据中重建电子能量频谱的挑战，特别是在数据噪声大、精度低、采样稀疏的情况下，传统方法难以准确解析复杂的多峰能量分布特征。

Method: 采用基于物理原理的神经网络（PINN）框架，直接处理原始实验数据，使用NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo平台，通过网格无关方法嵌入实验不确定度。

Result: 验证表明该方法能够准确解析亚纳秒电子束测量中的复杂多峰能量分布特征，即使面对噪声大、精度低、采样稀疏的实验数据，也能实现可靠的频谱重建。

Conclusion: 该研究提出的PINN框架成功实现了对电子能量频谱的高精度重建，能够从稀疏和噪声污染的衰减曲线数据中准确解析复杂的多峰能量分布特征，展现了在实验物理数据处理中的实用价值。

Abstract: This work introduces a physics-informed neural network (PINN) framework for reconstructing electron energy spectra from sparsely sampled attenuation-curve data. Leveraging the NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo platform, the proposed mesh-free methodology operates directly on raw experimental datasets while explicitly incorporating all experimental uncertainties. Validation on subnanosecond electron beam measurements demonstrates that the approach accurately resolves complex, multi-peaked spectral features of energy distribution. The framework enforces physical consistency through embedded governing principles and exhibits substantial predictive capability for energy spectrum reconstruction from noisy, low-precision, and sparse experimental data.

### [2] [Measurements of Laser-Driven Plasma Expansion into Hohlraum-Relevant Background Gas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11664)
*S. Hilsabeck,S. Dannhoff,C. A. Walsh,M. Sherlock,G. D. Sutcliffe,E. R. Tubman*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 在OMEGA EP激光装置上进行实验，研究激光驱动铜等离子体在低Z背景气体中的扩展行为，用于模拟黑腔物理机制，并对比实验观测与磁流体动力学模拟结果之间的差异。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究目的是为黑腔物理提供一个研究平台，用于理解黑腔壁吹出、非局域输运和磁化等离子体效应等关键物理过程。

Method: 在OMEGA EP激光装置上，让激光驱动的铜等离子体扩展到低Z背景气体（氦气填充密度0.3-0.6 mg/cc）中；采用阴影成像技术观测密度特征和时间演化，利用质子放射摄影测量路径积分磁场和密度调制；实验压力条件为350 psi（0.3 mg/cc）和700 psi（0.6 mg/cc），并将实验结果与Gorgon和HYDRA磁流体动力学模拟结果进行对比。

Result: 实验观察到明确定义的密度特征和丝状结构，并测量了气泡随时间的扩散行为；虽然大尺度气泡形状模拟结果与实验一致，但气泡扩散速率在1-3 ns期间比实验观测快20-50%，导致后期气泡尺寸差异增大。

Conclusion: 实验结果指出需要对Biermann电池场生成、热输运和不稳定性增长等物理过程施加额外的约束条件，才能确保气填充黑腔的预测建模准确性；目前的磁流体动力学模拟在描述这些关键物理过程方面存在不足。

Abstract: Experiments at the OMEGA EP laser facility were designed and executed to study plasma expansion into hohlraum-relevant gas fills (0.3-0.6 mg/cc of helium), providing a surrogate platform for investigating hohlraum wall blow-off, non-local transport, and magnetized plasma effects. We observe well-defined density features and filamentary structures as laser-driven copper plasma expands into a low-Z background gas. Shadowgraphy resolves sharp density features over time and reveals fine-scale filamentation in the laser spot region with characteristic transverse scales of 10-100 microns near the foil surface. Proton radiography provides sensitivity to path-integrated magnetic fields and density modulations throughout the bubble volume. We extract the bubble expansion as a function of time for two gas pressures, 350 psi (producing 0.3 mg/cc equivalent conditions) and 700 psi (0.6 mg/cc equivalent conditions), and compare the measured propagation to magnetohydrodynamic simulations performed with Gorgon and HYDRA. While the large-scale shape of the bubble is well reproduced by both codes, the time-dependent expansion rate shows significant discrepancies (20-50% faster) compared to experimental observations between 1 and 3 ns. This leads to increasingly larger differences in bubble sizes at later times. The optical measurements of bubble expansion and evolution of small-scale structures point to additional constraints required for Biermann-battery field generation, thermal transport, and instability growth in hohlraum-relevant plasmas, to ensure accurate, predictive modeling of gas-filled hohlraums.

### [3] [The Ambipolar electric field in multispecies plasma atmospheres: effects of alpha particles and stochastic heating](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12014)
*Luca Barbieri,Pascal Démoulin,Daniel Verscharen*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 扩展潘内科克-罗瑟兰理论到多种成分等离子体，推导了重力分层等离子体中自洽双极电场，揭示了成分依赖的分层行为，并将形式推广到多温度情况以研究随机加热效应。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究由电子、质子和α粒子组成的无碰撞重力分层等离子体大气中的平稳态，扩展潘内科克-罗瑟兰理论以处理多种成分和多温度等离子体系，为理解天体物理等离子体（如日冕、星际介质）中的成分分层和加热效应提供理论框架。

Method: 1. 扩展潘内科克-罗瑟兰理论到多种成分和多温度等离子体
2. 从刘维尔定理出发推导动力学平衡下的自洽双极电场
3. 计算电荷中性条件
4. 对于单温度大气获得双极电场的解析表达式
5. 使用一阶解析近似精确重现数值解
6. 将形式推广到由随机边界加热产生的多温度等离子体
7. 将平稳分布表示为麦克斯韦分布的叠加

Result: 1. 获得了单温度大气中双极电场的解析表达式，揭示了其与α粒子丰度的依赖关系
2. 确定了三种成分的相对分层程度：α粒子分层最强，质子分层最弱
3. 一阶解析近似能够准确重现静电势的数值解
4. 在多温度情况下，重力过滤产生了非指数密度分布和随高度增加的温度
5. 相对物种分层得以保持
6. 双极电场包含主导的重力分量（广义潘内科克-罗瑟兰场）和由物种依赖的温度梯度引起的热电贡献，后者解释了电场的非单调结构

Conclusion: 该论文建立了一个研究重力分层天体物理等离子体中多种成分与随机加热联合效应的理论框架，为理解日冕、星际介质等天体环境中等离子体分层与温度分布提供了基础。

Abstract: We investigate stationary states of a collisionless, gravitationally stratified plasma atmosphere composed of electrons, protons, and alpha particles by extending Pannekoek--Rosseland theory to multispecies and multi-temperature plasmas. Starting from Liouville's theorem, we derive the self-consistent ambipolar electric field from kinetic equilibrium and charge neutrality. For a single-temperature atmosphere, we obtain analytical expressions for the ambipolar field, show its dependence on alpha-particle abundance, and determine the relative stratification of the three species. A first-order analytical approximation to the electrostatic potential accurately reproduces the numerical solution. We then generalize the formalism to multi-temperature plasmas generated by stochastic boundary heating, representing the stationary distribution as a superposition of Maxwellian populations. Gravitational filtering produces non-exponential density profiles and increasing temperatures with altitude, while preserving the relative species stratification, with alpha particles most strongly stratified and protons least. The ambipolar electric field contains a dominant gravitational contribution, corresponding to the generalized Pannekoek--Rosseland field, and a thermoelectric contribution arising from species-dependent temperature gradients, which accounts for its non-monotonic structure. These results provide a framework for studying the combined effects of plasma composition and stochastic heating in gravitationally stratified astrophysical plasmas.

### [4] [Derivation and application of sheath boundary conditions for drift-kinetic simulations in a linear plasma device based on a gyromoment approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12205)
*Jacob Emil Mencke,Thomas Stucker,Paolo Ricci*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 为磁场垂直于壁面的碰撞前鞘入口推导了新漂移动理学边界条件，并数值实现。新边界条件相比经验边界导致等离子体流出增加，设备整体密度降低。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当前等离子体模拟中使用的经验边界条件可能无法准确描述磁场垂直于壁面时的碰撞前鞘入口物理过程，需要推导更精确的物理边界条件来改进等离子体与壁面相互作用的模拟。

Method: 1. 推导磁场垂直于壁面情况下碰撞前鞘入口处的漂移动理学模型边界条件
2. 基于Hermite-Laguerre基展开离子分布函数
3. 考虑线性等离子体设备几何结构
4. 实现边界条件的数值计算
5. 与先前使用的经验边界条件进行模拟比较

Abstract: Boundary conditions for a drift-kinetic model at the collisional presheath entrance with perpendicular incidence of the magnetic field to the wall are derived and numerically implemented. The drift-kinetic model for the plasma is based on the expansion of the ion distribution function on a Hermite-Laguerre basis, and the evolution of the resulting gyromoments. A linear-plasma-device geometry is considered. Comparison with simpler simulations with previously used ad hoc boundary conditions is presented. For the new set of boundary conditions, a significant increase of the plasma outflow to the wall is observed, leading to a significantly smaller plasma density in the whole volume of the device.

### [5] [State-resolved quantum transport of vortex electrons in accelerators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11934)
*S. S. Baturin*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 关于加速器晶格中涡旋电子传输的密度矩阵理论：周期性圆形晶格中受保护对象是Lewis-Floquet轨道角动量不变量而非瞬时动能角动量；理想传输具有元模提升特性；随机缺陷产生Lindblad通道。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究涡旋电子在加速器晶格中的传输特性，特别是轨道角动量在周期性系统中的保护机制以及随机缺陷对传输稳定性的影响。

Method: 开发涡旋电子传输的密度矩阵理论，分析周期性圆形晶格中的Lewis-Floquet轨道角动量不变量，研究理想传输的元模提升特性，以及处理随机缺陷的Lindblad通道方法。

Result: 偶极噪声产生可移动的质心展宽；四极噪声驱动固有的Δℓ=±2泄漏。在显式白噪声基准下，IOTA的初始泄漏尺度倒数为2×10^5圈，PETRA III为2×10^6圈。

Conclusion: 提出了涡旋电子传输的密度矩阵框架，识别了轨道角动量的保护机制和泄漏通道，为加速器设计中的涡旋电子稳定性分析提供了理论工具。

Abstract: We develop a density-matrix theory of vortex-electron transport in accelerator lattices. In a periodic round lattice, the protected object is a Lewis-Floquet orbital angular momentum (OAM) invariant rather than the instantaneous kinetic OAM. Ideal transport has a metaplectic lift; stochastic imperfections generate a Lindblad channel. Dipole noise produces removable centroid smearing; quadrupole noise drives intrinsic $Δ\ell=\pm2$ leakage. Under explicit white-noise benchmarks, the inverse initial leakage scale is $2\times10^5$ turns for IOTA and $2\times10^6$ turns for PETRA III.

</details>
