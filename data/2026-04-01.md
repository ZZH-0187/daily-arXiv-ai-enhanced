Here, we combine nuclear data for deuterium-tritium and lithium reactions with a reduced thermohydraulic model of a liquid lithium jet and an operator-theoretic formulation of feedback control. <details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 解决早期研究中关于氢同位素在FLiBe中传输参数存在广泛分散性的知识空白，探索未经验证的假设对渗透性测量的影响

Method: 在HYPERION设施中采用盐侧充氘方法，研究一维渗透、盐覆盖膜表面和盐-金属界面理想润湿性等假设，特别关注了气泡富集的Ni-FLiBe界面的渗透屏障特征

Result: 金属侧充气时同位素传输行为受到抑制，气泡富集的Ni-FLiBe界面形成渗透屏障，使渗透性降低高达77%；盐侧充气方法能更准确地测量渗透性，解释了早期研究中观察到的H/D/T传输参数分散现象

To address the knowledge gap resulting from widely scattered transport parameters reported in the earlier studies, HYPERION experiments incorporate specific provisions to probe unsubstantiated assumptions, including a one-dimensional permeation, membrane surface coverage by the salt, and ideal wettability of the salt-metal interface. The initial insights from HYPERION experiments call into question the design choices and estimated transport properties in earlier works. This study provides a plausible explanation for the observed scatter in H/D/T transport in FLiBe.

### [3] [FDTD Simulation of O-X Mode Conversion Process in Non-uniform Magnetized Plasma](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29513)

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 研究使用FDTD方法在非均匀密度磁化等离子体中分析O-X模式转换，发现存在最佳入射角度可实现波的有效传播和电场增强，偏离该角度会导致衰减。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 电子伯恩斯坦波能在过密等离子体中无密度截止传播，适合高密度等离子体加热，但无法直接从真空激发，需要O-X-B模式转换过程。研究旨在优化O-X模式转换效率。

Method: 使用有限差分时域（FDTD）方法模拟磁化等离子体中的O-X模式转换，重点分析入射角度对模式转换特性的影响。

Result: 存在最佳入射角度能使波无显著衰减地传播，并在上混合共振层附近观察到强电场增强。偏离该角度会出现衰减区域导致波衰减。

Since EBWs cannot be directly launched from vacuum, mode conversion processes such as O-X-B conversion are required. In this study, the O-X mode conversion process is investigated using the finite difference time-domain (FDTD) method in a magnetized plasma with non-uniform density. In particular, the dependence of mode conversion characteristics on the incident angle of the injected wave is studied. The results show that an optimal incident angle exists at which the wave propagates without significant attenuation and strong electric field enhancement is observed near the upper hybrid resonance (UHR) layer. When the incident angle deviates from this optimal angle, an evanescent region appears, resulting in attenuation of the wave. These results demonstrate that angular optimization is essential for efficient wave propagation toward the UHR region and EBW excitation.

### [4] [Anisotropy-induced Inhomogeneous Melting in Finite Dust Clusters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29682)
*Sushree Monalisha Sahu,Hirakjyoti Sarma,Ankit Dhaka,Pintu Bandyopadhyay,Abhijit Sen*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 首次在有限尘埃等离子体晶体中实验观测到各向异性约束势阱中的不均匀熔化现象，揭示了几何各向异性在有限耦合系统熔化动力学中的关键调控作用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究各向异性约束势阱中有限尘埃等离子体晶体的熔化行为，探究几何约束条件如何影响非平衡态下有限耦合系统的相变过程。虽然晶格系统的熔化理论已有进展，但有限系统在非对称约束下不均匀熔化的实验证据尚属首次需要。

Method: 结合激光加热与光谱模式分析：1）通过实验手段在尘埃等离子体晶体中系统调节约束各向异性，施加可控激光加热；2）基于粒子轨迹的奇异值分解进行光谱模式分析，确定能量分布模式；3）采用分子动力学模拟复现实验观察，揭示约束调控的模式耦合熔化动力学机制。

Result: 观测到明显的非均匀熔化模式：特定模式的重组触发局域结构失稳，熔化前沿表现出空间异质性。分子动力学模拟成功再现实验结果，证实了约束控制模式耦合与激光加热共同主导熔化动力学过程。能量通过特定集体模式重新分布是该不均匀熔化的核心机制。

Conclusion: 几何各向异性是有限空间耦合系统中不均匀熔化的关键调控参数。这项研究为理解和设计受限条件下的非平衡相变过程提供了新视角和新方法框架。

Abstract: We present the first experimental evidence of inhomogeneous melting in a finite dusty plasma crystal confined in an anisotropic potential well. By systematically tuning the confinement anisotropy and applying controlled laser heating, distinct melting patterns are observed. Spectral-mode analysis based on Singular Value Decomposition of particle trajectories reveals that increasing laser power redistributes energy into specific collective modes, triggering localized structural destabilization. Molecular Dynamics simulations reproduce the observations and show that confinement-controlled mode coupling with laser heating governs the melting dynamics. These results establish geometric anisotropy as a key control parameter for inhomogeneous melting in finite coupled systems.
