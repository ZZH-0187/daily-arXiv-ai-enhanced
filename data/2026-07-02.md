### [1] [Improved Particle Confinement with Resonant Magnetic Perturbations in DIII-D Tokamak H-Mode Plasmas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00287)
*N. C. Logan,Q. Hu,C. Paz-Soldan,R. Nazikian,T. Rhodes,T. Wilks,S. Munaretto,A. Bortolon,F. Laggner,F. Scotti,R. Hong,H. Wang*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: DIII-D托卡马克实验发现，在特定逆向电流旋转条件下施加共振磁扰动能够提高粒子约束，此效应源于湍流输运减少和经典输运符号反转，为反应堆相关的三维磁约束提供了新优化方向。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索共振磁扰动在特定运行条件下对托卡马克等离子体约束性能的影响，特别关注其能否突破传统认知实现性能提升，为未来聚变反应堆的三维磁约束优化提供新思路。

Method: 通过在DIII-D托卡马克上进行实验，在特定的逆向电流旋转参数范围内施加共振磁扰动，分析其对等离子体约束和性能的影响，并结合湍流输运和经典输运变化的测量数据。

Result: 实验发现了一个逆向电流旋转的稳健参数范围，在此范围内RMP能够显著增加粒子约束和整体性能（密度泵入效应），这种效应是由于湍流输运减少和经典输运符号反转共同作用的结果。

Conclusion: 研究表明，逆向电流旋转条件下RMP导致的密度泵入效应代表了托卡马克运行中一种新的输运逆转现象，这一发现对于提高反应堆相关的三维磁约束性能具有重要潜力。

Abstract: Experiments on the DIII-D tokamak have identified a novel regime in which applied resonant magnetic perturbations (RMPs) increase the particle confinement and overall performance. This Letter details a robust range of counter-current rotation over which RMPs cause this density pump-in effect for high confinement (H mode) plasmas. The pump in is shown to be caused by a reduction of the turbulent transport and to be correlated with a change in the sign of the induced neoclassical transport. This novel reversal of the RMP induced transport has the potential to significantly improve reactor relevant, three-dimensional magnetic confinement scenarios.

### [2] [Reexamination of collisional ionization cross sections including double photoionization processes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00875)
*Lucas Ansia,Pedro Velarde,Marta Fajardo,Gareth Williams*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 通过BibBarT模型重新分析XFEL加热铝的实验数据，动态演化非平衡电子分布并包含简并效应，同时加入shake-off过程，改进了与观测光谱的匹配度，发现三体复合可能被高估。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 稠密等离子体中的碰撞电离截面仍难以精确确定，主要由于等离子体条件的不确定性和竞争原子过程光谱信号的相互重叠。虽然XFEL技术显著推进了该领域发展，但仍存在关于碰撞截面、超热电子演化和竞争原子过程的问题。

Method: 采用BibBarT模型进行新分析，该模型能动态演化非热电子分布，并明确包含简并效应。同时加入了最近在等离子体状态中观察到的模仿CI信号的shake-off过程。

Result: 包含shake-off过程能够改善与观测发射特征的匹配度，进一步降低复合速率能更好地与数据吻合，表明在这些条件下三体复合可能被高估了。

Conclusion: 我们的分析表明，综合考虑简并电子分布、非平衡电子演化和shake-off过程后，能够更好地解释实验数据。同时观察到三体复合速率可能被高估，这为稠密等离子体中碰撞电离截面的精确确定提供了新的可能性。

Abstract: Collisional ionization (CI) cross sections in dense plasmas remain difficult to constrain due to uncertainties in plasma conditions and the overlapping spectral signatures of competing atomic processes. The use of x-ray free electron lasers (XFELs) to both heat and probe solid-density targets has significantly advanced the field by eliminating assumptions about ion density. However, questions remain regarding collisional cross sections, suprathermal electron evolution and competing atomic processes.
  In this work, we revisit experimental data from XFEL-heated aluminum, previously analyzed using collisional radiative models that did not treat the degenerate electron distribution and atomic processes self consistently. We present a new analysis using BibBarT which dynamically evolves non-thermal electron populations and explicitly includes degeneracy effects. Furthermore, we incorporate an important atomic process recently observed in plasma state that mimic signatures of CI, shake-off. Our results show that including shake-off processes improves agreement with observed emission features, and lowering recombination rates further improves the agreement with data -- indicating a possible overestimate of three-body recombination in these conditions.
