Motivation: 传统汤姆逊散射分析通常假设麦克斯韦速度分布，但在高能量密度和实验室天体物理等离子体中，无碰撞、多群粒子动力学会产生非热特征，导致麦克斯韦模型可能产生误导性的等离子体参数。

Method: 提出了一个通用的汤姆逊散射分析框架，使用灵活且物理上有意义的基函数来表示物种特异的速度分布函数。构建可微分的正向模型，通过梯度下降优化VDF参数。应用该方法于碰撞冲击中的氢和碳粒子群产生的合成汤姆逊散射光谱。

Result: 在冲击上游最非热区，灵活基函数将离子VDF的平均归一化残差从麦克斯韦模型的38.7%降低到20.3%和20.5%，相应改进了时间分辨的等离子体参数精度。

Conclusion: 该研究展示了灵活VDF建模和统计映射解空间对这种固有不适定反演问题的重要性，通过系统的不确定性量化揭示了离子物种间的简并性，为复杂等离子体诊断提供了更准确的分析工具。

Abstract: Thomson scattering (TS) is a key diagnostic for inferring plasma parameters from scattered power spectra, but its interpretation depends strongly on assumptions about the underlying velocity distribution functions (VDFs). Conventional analyses typically assume Maxwellian distributions, which can be invalid in high-energy-density and laboratory astrophysics plasmas where collisionless, multi-population dynamics produce non-thermal features. In such cases, Maxwellian models may reproduce observed spectra while yielding inaccurate or misleading plasma parameters. We present a generalized TS analysis framework that represents species-specific VDFs in flexible, physically motivated bases and so reconstructs complex, non-equilibrium distributions. To explore the resulting high-dimensional parameter space, we build the forward model from differentiable operations and optimize the VDF parameters by gradient descent. We apply the method to synthetic TS spectra generated from particle-in-cell simulations of a collisionless shock with interacting hydrogen and carbon populations. Immediately upstream of the shock, where the plasma is most non-thermal, the flexible bases cut the mean normalized ion VDF residual from 38.7% for a Maxwellian model to 20.3% and 20.5%, with correspondingly improved time-resolved plasma parameters. We also introduce a systematic method of uncertainty quantification that reveals degeneracies between ion species, highlighting the importance of both flexible VDF modeling and a statistical mapping of the solution space for this inherently ill-posed inversion problem.
