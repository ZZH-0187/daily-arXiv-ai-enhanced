### [1] [High temporal resolution THz streaking of high brightness relativistic electron beams](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00125)
*Maximilian Lenz,Brian Schaap,Atharva Kulkarni,Yuemei Tan,Yining Yang,Renkai Li,Pietro Musumeci*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 该论文系统研究了太赫兹频段条纹结构，用于超快表征相对论性高亮度电子束。研究比较了喇叭耦合波导几何结构，分析了条纹强度、色散、传输和时间保真度等性能参数。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 开发适用于超快电子束诊断的太赫兹条纹结构需要明确的设计原则和性能极限。目前缺乏对这类结构在太赫兹频段的系统性实验研究，特别是关于条纹强度、色散特性和时间保真度的比较分析。

Method: 采用分析模型和电磁模拟来描述波导尺寸和驱动频率对条纹功率的影响。实验上使用射频光电枪产生的压缩电子束，在太赫兹场强、束流能量和束团持续时间变化范围内对结构进行表征。研究比较了不同喇叭耦合波导几何结构的性能。

Result: 通过实验获得了太赫兹条纹结构在条纹强度、色散、传输和时间保真度方面的性能数据。建立了太赫兹条纹结构的一般设计原则和性能极限，为超快电子束诊断提供了实用指导。

Conclusion: 研究为太赫兹频段的电子束诊断条纹结构建立了系统的设计框架和性能评估标准，推动了超快电子束表征技术的发展，为相关应用提供了可靠的工具。

Abstract: We report a systematic experimental study of terahertz (THz) streaking structures for ultrafast characterization of relativistic, high-brightness electron beams. Horn-coupled waveguide geometries are investigated, enabling a comparative characterization of streaking strength, dispersion, transmission, and temporal fidelity. Analytical models and electromagnetic simulations are used to describe the dependence of streaking power on the waveguide dimensions and the drive frequency. Experimentally, the structures are characterized using compressed electron beam from an RF photoinjector over a range of THz field strengths, beam energies, and bunch durations. These results establish general design principles and performance limits for THz streaking structures applicable to ultrafast electron beam diagnostics.

### [2] [Achromatic Telescopic Squeezing for Dynamic Aperture Optimization in the Electron Storage Ring of the EIC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00142)
*Jonathan Unger,Georg Hoffstaetter*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: ATS方案应用于EIC的ESR可以在保持动量接受度的同时减少六极磁铁数量，但会导致发射度增长。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究ATS方案在EIC电子存储环中的应用，旨在改善动态孔径和动量接受度，同时探索减少六极磁铁数量和缓解高阶非线性效应的可能性。

Method: 在简化测试晶格和完整ESR晶格上，对传统六极磁铁校正方案与ATS基光學进行对比研究，分析两种方案的差异。

Result: ATS光學可降低所需六极磁铁强度，减轻高阶非线性效应，提高动量孔径。在测试条件下，ATS方案可将动量接受度提高约0.1%，但会导致发射度增长。同时，在保持相似动量孔径的情况下，ATS可将弧段六极磁铁数量减少一半。

Conclusion: ATS方案对ESR的适用性受到发射度增长的限制，但对发射度增长可接受的存储环具有潜在优势。

Abstract: We investigate the application of the Achromatic Telescopic Squeezing (ATS) scheme to the Electron Storage Ring (ESR) of the Electron-Ion Collider (EIC) as a method to improve dynamic aperture and momentum acceptance. A comparative study is performed between conventional sextupole correction schemes and ATS-based optics using both a simplified test lattice and the full ESR lattice. We show that ATS optics can reduce the required sextupole strengths and mitigate higher-order nonlinear effects, leading to improved momentum aperture. With the ATS principle one could reduce the number of sextupoles in an arcs by a factor of two while maintaining similar momentum aperture. We additionally show a scheme utilizing all sextupoles which provides an advantage in momentum aperture. While the resulting ATS optics provides a measurable increase in momentum acceptance ($\sim$0.1\% under the test conditions), it also induces emittance growth due to increased $β$-functions in the arcs. This trade-off limits its applicability for the ESR but suggests potential advantages for storage rings where moderate emittance growth is acceptable.

### [3] [Development of Ultra High Power Compact X-Band Pulse Compressor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00166)
*Ankur Dhar,Mohamed A. K. Othman,Valery A. Dolgashev*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 介绍了一种新型的11.424 GHz SLED型射频脉冲压缩器，用于为脉冲长度约20纳秒的高梯度X波段光电注入器供电。该压缩器使用支持轴对称TE模式的球形腔，表面电场最小，与现有的紧凑型球形SLED相比，提高了高功率稳定性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 射频脉冲压缩为各种应用（如自由电子激光器的电子束诊断射频偏转器）提供了在脉冲长度代价下实现更高峰值功率的实用途径。当前紧凑型球形SLED使用的TE偶极子模式存在局限性，需要改进高功率稳定性。

Method: 设计了一种新型紧凑脉冲压缩器，由两个球形腔和一个波导混合器组成。球形腔支持轴对称TE模式，具有最小表面电场，从而提高功率耐受性。采用TE01圆形波导端口连接腔体和混合器。

Result: 在SLAC建造并进行了高功率测试，使用SLAC XL-4速调管生成的52 MW、1微秒脉冲进行压缩，实现了317 MW的峰值功率，压缩脉冲的半高全宽为27纳秒。

Conclusion: 这一发展展示了实现加速结构和射频光电注入器高梯度、短脉冲工作模式的可行途径，为相关应用提供了实用的高功率解决方案。

Abstract: We demonstrate a new 11.424 GHz SLED-type RF pulse compressor for powering high-gradient X-band photoinjectors with pulse lengths around 20 ns. RF pulse compression provides a practical path to higher peak power at the cost of pulse length for various applications such as RF deflectors for electron beam diagnostics on free electron lasers. Our new compact pulse compressor uses spherical cavities supporting axially symmetric TE modes which have minimal electric fields on the cavity surfaces, intended to improve high-power robustness as compared to existing compact spherical SLEDs which use a TE dipole mode. We present the design of this pulse compressor composed of two spherical cavities and a waveguide hybrid. The two cavities and hybrid have TE01 circular waveguide ports. This pulse compressor was built and high power tested at SLAC. These tests demonstrated a peak power of 317 MW by compressing 52 MW, 1 μs pulses generated by a SLAC XL-4 klystron. The full-width at half-maximum of this compressed pulse was 27 ns. We conjecture that this development demonstrates a viable route to reaching the high-gradient, short pulse regime for accelerating structures and RF photoinjectors.

### [4] [Characterization of ELM Pacing via Vertical Jogs on DIII-D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00165)
*Kei Yasoda,Dario Panici,Andrew Oak Nelson,Florian M. Laggner,Sangkyeun Kim,Egemen Kolemen*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 通过在DIII-D上进行的等离子体垂直振荡或jogging实验，成功实现了边缘局域模(ELM)的定频触发，将ELM频率从自然状态的约5Hz提高到20Hz，同时降低了储能损失和偏滤器热负荷。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 边缘局域模(ELM)是托卡马克等离子体中的一种不稳定性，会瞬间释放大量能量到偏滤器，影响装置运行安全。传统ELM频率低、能量释放大，需要寻找有效控制方法。

Method: 通过垂直振荡或快速下移(jogging)等离子体，使其向X点运动。实验中以20Hz频率垂直振荡等离子体，并监测ELM触发情况。同时建立了边缘环向电流的简化模型，结合实验数据进行ELITE稳定性分析。

Result: 1. ELM频率从自然状态的~5Hz提高到20Hz；2. 触发的高频ELM使储能损失从~10%降至1%以下；3. 偏滤器峰值热通量降低约2倍；4. 观察到碳杂质浓度降低；5. 模型分析表明等离子体下移时边缘区域产生净正环向电流，推动等离子体跨越剥离-气球模稳定性边界，触发ELM。

Conclusion: 等离子体垂直振荡/jogging是有效触发高频、低强度ELM的控制方法，能显著降低储能损失和偏滤器热负荷，为聚变装置ELM控制提供了新途径。

Abstract: Edge localized mode (ELM) pacing via vertical plasma oscillations or jogging has been successfully demonstrated on DIII-D. Rapid vertical movement of the plasma toward the X-point has been shown to effectively trigger ELMs. By vertically oscillating the plasma at a rate of 20 Hz, the ELM frequency increased from $\sim$5~Hz, the natural ELM frequency in similar DIII-D discharges, to 20~Hz. Downward jogs have been observed to trigger multiple ELMs in one cycle. ELMs triggered at higher than natural frequencies lead to smaller decreases in stored energy, from ~10\% to as little as below 1\%. As a consequence, the peak heat flux to the divertor has been observed to be reduced by a factor of $\sim$2. In addition, a reduction in the carbon impurity concentration has been observed. During downward jogs in the lower single null (LSN) configuration, the X-point movement is slower and smaller than the top of the plasma. As a result, a reduction in the plasma cross section and hence volume has been observed. To understand the mechanism of ELM triggering by jogging, a toy model of the edge toroidal current has been built and tested with DIII-D experiment data. The experimental data and model suggest that when the plasma moves down towards the X-point, a net positive toroidal current is locally induced in the edge region. ELITE stability analysis suggests that this current pushes the plasma state across the peeling side of the peeling-ballooning stability boundary into the unstable region triggering ELMs.

### [5] [The L-H transition in tokamaks: power threshold, density minimum and toroidal-field asymmetry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00624)
*Brenno De Lucca,Paolo Ricci,Benoit Labit,Davide Mancini,Louis Stenger,Zeno Tecchiolli*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究通过三维通量驱动双流体模拟，发现电磁漂移波湍流能在有利环向场位形中自发产生剪切E×B流，从而解释托卡马克L-H转换的物理机制，并首次推导出L-H功率阈值等关键参数的first-principles标度律。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 托卡马克中L-H转换的物理机制四十多年来一直未得到解决，特别是在不同环向场位形中存在不对称性的原因。

Method: 使用三维通量驱动双流体模拟在偏滤器位形中研究，并结合准线性动量通量计算分析时间反演对称性破缺效应。

Result: 1. 模拟显示电磁漂移波湍流能自发产生剪切E×B流，导致输运抑制
2. 环向场方向不对称性源于有限碰撞率引起的时间反演对称性破缺
3. 推导出L-H功率阈值、密度分支、最小密度和最小功率的first-principles标度律，优于现有经验标度

Conclusion: 该研究首次从第一性原理层面解释了L-H转换的物理机制，特别是环向场方向不对称性，并为关键参数提供了理论标度律，解决了这一长期存在的开放性问题。

Abstract: The physical mechanism underlying the L--H transition in tokamaks has remained an open problem for over forty years. We present three-dimensional flux-driven two-fluid simulations in a diverted geometry that exhibit a confinement transition at lower power in the favourable toroidal-field configuration. The simulations show that electromagnetic drift-wave turbulence spontaneously generates a sheared $\bm{E}\times \bm{B}$ flow responsible for transport suppression. The toroidal-field-direction asymmetry arises from time-reversal symmetry breaking by finite collisionality, as demonstrated by a quasilinear calculation of the turbulent momentum flux. First-principles scaling laws are derived for the L--H power threshold in both density branches, the density minimum, and the minimum power, all matching or surpassing existing empirical scalings.

</details>
