*J. K. Jones,A. D. Brynes,M. King,H. L. Owen,A. Pollard*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: Slipstream是一个用于存储环、直线加速器和光电注入器非线性束流动力学的PyTorch包，提供CPU/GPU粒子追踪、含空间电荷的场图注入模型以及沿支持追踪路径的自动微分。

Motivation: 开发一个集成的GPU加速束流动力学仿真工具，利用自动微分和GPU计算优势，提高重复仿真（扫描、拟合和优化）的效率。

Method: 基于PyTorch框架，实现CPU和GPU粒子追踪；包含场图注入器模型与空间电荷效应；集成自动微分功能；与elegant、ASTRA、GPT等现有代码进行精度和计算成本对比验证。

Result: 存储环工作频率与elegant一致达10^{-9}精度；GPU浮点32追踪比单线程CPU快数百倍；自动微分使24参数光学匹配时间相比有限差分减少一个数量级；光电注入器仿真最终横向发射度与ASTRA在20-100pC下偏差约1%，250pC下偏差约4%；2^{18}宏粒子在RTX 3050 GPU上14秒完成，发射度偏差在2%内。

Conclusion: Slipstream在GPU执行、自动微分和模型精度方面表现出色，特别适用于需要重复仿真的扫描、拟合和优化任务；该包主要由AI编程助手在人类指导下开发完成。

Abstract: Slipstream is a PyTorch package for nonlinear beam dynamics in storage rings, linacs and photoinjectors. It provides CPU and GPU particle tracking, field-map injector models with space charge, and automatic differentiation along supported tracking paths. Comparisons with elegant, ASTRA, GPT and other codes assess accuracy and computational cost for nonlinear ring tracking, optics matching and photoinjectors. Ring tunes agree with elegant to $10^{-9}$. For the tested ring workloads with tens of thousands of particles, GPU tracking in float32 is hundreds of times faster than the single-threaded CPU implementations compared here. Automatic differentiation reduces the time for 24-parameter optics matching by an order of magnitude compared to finite differences. Using a high-brightness photo-injector as an example, final horizontal emittances agree with ASTRA to about 1% at 20 and 100pC and differ by $\approx$4% at 250pC. For $2^{18}$ macroparticles, Slipstream's faster default settings take 14s on an RTX 3050 GPU and give emittances within 2% of that of ASTRA. Additional tests examine coherent synchrotron radiation, dynamic aperture and resistive-wall impedance. The package was developed predominantly by AI coding assistants under human direction. These comparisons identify where its models, GPU execution and derivatives are useful for repeated simulations in scans, fits and optimisation.
