<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 需要为随机相位近似中的介电响应函数提供一个准确、高效的计算方法，以支持电子屏蔽、动态介电计算和X射线汤姆逊散射分析

Abstract: An accurate analytic approximation is developed for the real boundary component of a regularised, causal complex auxiliary function describing the random phase approximation dielectric response of the weakly coupled electron gas across classical to strongly degenerate conditions. Its collisionless imaginary boundary component is known exactly from the Fermi--Dirac occupation logarithm. Following an assessment of existing approximations and the establishment of a high-precision numerical benchmark, a two-regime representation is constructed: a $6/7$ rational function at weak and moderate degeneracy and a composite polynomial approximation at strong degeneracy. Exact asymptotic constraints determine the rational denominator algebraically, leaving six independent state functions. In the strongly degenerate regime, the logarithmic Kohn contribution is isolated explicitly, with its regular remainder represented by analytically integrable polynomial expansions. Validation using the electron--electron dynamic structure factor and imaginary-time correlation function yields maximum plasmon peak-amplitude errors of approximately $1.04\%$ and maximum pointwise correlation-function errors below $0.166\%$. Comparisons with established approximations demonstrate substantial improvements, particularly for weakly damped collective excitations. The resulting model provides an accurate, computationally efficient foundation for electronic screening, dynamic dielectric calculations and X-ray Thomson scattering analysis.
