### [1] [AloHEP Software: A luminosity optimizer for High Energy Physics colliders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09839)
*Burak Dagli,Yigitali Karaca*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 开发了AloHEP工具，用于准确模拟非对称对撞机（如EIC、LHeC、FCC-eh）的亮度计算，考虑小时玻璃效应、交叉角、束束相互作用等几何公式忽略的耦合效应。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统基于解析几何公式的亮度计算假设相同的刚性高斯束对头碰撞，对于新一代非对称对撞机（特别是轻子-强子对撞机），这种假设会错误估计亮度，因为它忽略了相互作用区的多个耦合效应。

Method: 开发AloHEP（高能物理亮度优化器），这是一个开源跨平台Java程序，使用宏粒子云格方法模拟相互作用区，包括纵向切片、二维静电问题求解、有限差分等算法。

Result: AloHEP能够准确模拟对称和非对称对撞机的亮度，考虑了小时玻璃效应、交叉角、束束挤压/破坏等多个效应，并通过验证显示出在适当极限下能够重现解析几何亮度。

Conclusion: AloHEP是一个灵活的工具，可用于快速原型制作实际亮度估计，特别适用于需要考虑多个耦合效应的非对称对撞机亮度计算。

Abstract: The luminosity quoted for proposed particle colliders is usually derived from the analytic geometric formula, which assumes head-on collisions of identical, rigid Gaussian bunches. For the new generation of asymmetric machines---in particular lepton--hadron colliders such as the EIC, LHeC and FCC-eh---this assumption can misestimate the luminosity, because it ignores a few coupled effects that act in the interaction region such as hourglass effect, crossing angle, and mutual electromagnetic (beam--beam) deformation of the colliding bunches. We present \textbf{AloHEP} (A luminosity optimizer for High Energy Physics), an open-source, cross-platform Java application that simulates the interaction region of both symmetric and asymmetric colliders. AloHEP represents each bunch by an ensemble of macroparticles deposited on the grid with a cloud-in-cell scheme, and slices the crossing longitudinally. On each transverse slice it solves the two-dimensional electrostatic problem by a direct Green's-function convolution, then obtains the beam--beam forces by finite differencing and continuous bicubic interpolation. The hourglass effect, finite crossing angle and beam--beam pinch/disruption can be enabled independently, and the two colliding beams may consist of entirely different species (leptons, protons, heavy ions). A JSON-backed parameter database and an interactive graphical interface make AloHEP a flexible tool for the rapid prototyping of realistic luminosity estimates. We verify that the code reproduces the analytic geometric luminosity in the appropriate limit and present scaling, grid-convergence and asymmetric-collider benchmarks.

### [2] [Numerical error analysis of EMC3-EIRENE simulations in the HSX stellarator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09190)
*F. A. Chavarria,D. Boeyaert,H. Frerichs,K. A. Garcia,B. Geiger*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究首次对EMC3-EIRENE在仿星器几何结构中的数值误差进行了全面分析，包括统计误差、偏差、时间积分误差和离散误差，揭示了常用评估指标低估统计误差的问题，并建立了改进仿星器建模准确性和效率的最佳实践。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 像EMC3-EIRENE这样的等离子体边缘模拟代码广泛应用于研究聚变磁约束装置，但数值误差会显著影响模拟结果且特征描述不足。这些误差来自蒙特卡洛粒子的有限采样和追踪，以及有限网格分辨率。需要全面分析以改进模拟的准确性和效率。

Method: 在Helically Symmetric eXperiment (HSX)的仿星器几何结构中，对EMC3-EIRENE模拟进行全面的数值误差分析。分析基本等离子体和中性粒子量，比较误差减少率与理论预期，表征统计误差、偏差和时间积分误差，并探讨离散误差的量化挑战。

Result: 研究发现常用的体积平均相对变化指标系统性地低估了统计误差；与托卡马克模拟相比，仿星器模拟中时间积分误差的主导贡献不同，这与两种装置中交叉场输运的相对重要性相关；离散误差的量化因复杂的网格几何结构而具有挑战性。

Conclusion: 这项工作填补了仿星器几何结构中EMC3-EIRENE模拟数值误差分析的空白，揭示了现有分析方法的缺陷，并根据结果和现有文献建立了改进仿星器建模准确性和效率的最佳实践，为更好的聚变研究模拟提供了重要参考。

Abstract: Plasma edge simulations with codes like EMC3-EIRENE are widely used to study fusion magnetic confinement devices. However, numerical errors can significantly impact the simulation solution and are often insufficiently characterized. These errors consist of the statistical error, bias, and time integration error, resulting from the finite sampling and tracking of Monte Carlo particles, and the discretization error, resulting from a finite grid resolution. This work presents the first comprehensive numerical error analysis of EMC3-EIRENE simulations in stellarator geometries, focusing on edge plasmas in the Helically Symmetric eXperiment (HSX). Fundamental plasma and neutral quantities are analyzed, and error reduction rates are compared against theoretical expectations. The statistical error, bias, and time integration error are fully characterized; however, quantification of the discretization error remains challenging because of the complex grid geometries used in EMC3-EIRENE. The commonly used volume averaged relative change metric is shown to systematically underestimate the statistical error, highlighting a critical flaw in EMC3-EIRENE analysis, where convergence is claimed despite significant noise remaining in the simulation solution. Dominant contributions of the time integration error are shown to differ in stellarator simulations compared to tokamak simulations, related to the relative importance of cross-field transport between the two devices. Based on these results and existing literature, we establish best practices towards improved accuracy and efficiency in general stellarator modeling with EMC3-EIRENE.

### [3] [Real-time Tokamak Equilibrium Reconstruction Under Limited Experimental Data via Physics-Grounded Synthetic Pre-training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09674)
*Mingdong He,Qixian Hu,Tengfei Xu,Tailin Wu,Zheng Xiong*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该论文提出使用合成数据预训练物理模拟模型，再通过少量真实数据微调，以解决托卡马克等离子体平衡重建中真实标签数据稀缺的问题。实验表明，在仅有1%真实数据的情况下，相对于从头训练，该方法可将重建误差降低58-64%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 托卡马克等离子体实时平衡重建需要大量高质量的标记实验数据，但获取这些数据在新实验或新设备启动阶段成本高昂且稀缺。需要开发能够减少对真实实验数据依赖的方法。

Method: 提出合成数据预训练方法：首先利用物理模拟生成低成本合成平衡数据进行预训练，然后在有限的真实实验数据上进行微调。在三种具有不同难度的场景下进行评估：分布内数据、分布外形状数据和跨实验（时间）数据。

Result: 当真实数据稀缺时（仅有1%的真实数据），与在同一数量级真实数据上从头训练的模型相比，合成预训练方法在分布外形状数据上将归一化均方根误差降低约58%，在跨实验外推场景中降低约64%。

Conclusion: 合成数据预训练能显著提高托卡马克等离子体平衡重建的准确性，特别是在真实实验数据稀缺的场景下，为托卡马克实验面临的实际数据匮乏问题提供了有效的解决方案。

Abstract: Real-time equilibrium reconstruction is essential for tokamak plasma control. Data-driven surrogates are usually trained on high-quality labeled experimental data, but producing a large amount of labeled data sufficient for surrogate learning requires many expensive discharge shots, and few exist at the start of a new campaign or the operation of a new device. To reduce this cost, we propose to pre-train on simulated equilibria, generated at low cost by physics-based simulation, then fine-tune on limited real data. We evaluate under three settings with increasing difficulties, i.e., in-distribution, out-of-distribution (shape), and cross-campaign (temporal) splits, which mirror how reconstruction is actually deployed. Experimental results validate that synthetic pre-training is highly effective when few real labels are available: fine-tuning on only 1% of the full available real dataset cuts the per-sample normalized root-mean-square error (nRMSE) of the reconstructed poloidal flux by roughly 58% under out-of-distribution shapes and 64% under cross-campaign extrapolation, compared to a model trained from scratch on the same amount of real data. These realistic evaluations show that synthetic pre-training improves equilibrium reconstruction accuracy in the data-scarce regime that real tokamak campaigns face.

### [4] [On the particle and electromagnetic boundary conditions in a mesothermal plasma flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09948)
*J. Gonzalez*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该论文针对中等热等离子体全动力学建模中电子和电磁场的边界条件描述挑战，提出了新的边界条件处理方法，包括考虑电子漂移速度的部分反射模型和使用安培-麦克斯韦定律改进暂态电场表示。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 全动力学中等热等离子体流动建模面临电子和电磁场边界条件的显著挑战。由于电子比离子具有更高的迁移率，在暂态过程中超出离子流向下游，阻碍了出流边界条件的实施。通常忽略的电子漂移速度在实际模拟中对于实现电荷和电流密度的准中性至关重要。

Method: 提出了新的边界条件处理方法：1) 在粒子注入和出流边界条件中都考虑电子漂移速度；2) 提出新的电子部分反射模型来表征准稳态和暂态状态；3) 使用安培-麦克斯韦定律改进出流边界处的暂态电场表示，相比经典的"零场"诺伊曼边界条件无需任何数值参数。

Result: 这些新的边界条件改善了扩展中等热等离子体的准稳态和暂态描述。通过考虑电子漂移速度和改进电场边界条件，能够更好地实现电荷和电流密度的准中性，提高了仿真的精度和稳定性。

Conclusion: 论文展示了在中等热等离子体全动力学建模中，正确考虑电子漂移速度对于实现准中性、改进边界条件的重要性。新的部分反射模型和基于安培-麦克斯韦定律的边界条件方法为等离子体模拟提供了更准确和稳定的数值工具。

Abstract: The fully kinetic modeling of mesothermal plasma flows presents significant challenges in the description of boundary conditions for the electrons and the electromagnetic field. Due to their higher mobility compared to ions, electrons extend downstream of the ions, which hinders the implementation of the outflow boundary conditions during transient states. Although the electron drift velocity, $u_e$, is usually neglected as $u_e \simeq u_i < v_{th,e}$, where $u_i$ is the bulk velocity of the ions and $v_{th,e}$ is the thermal velocity of the electrons, we will show how accounting for it, both at the injection of particles and at the outflow boundary condition, is of great importance to achieve quasi-neutrality of charge and current densities. A new partial reflection model for electrons is presented to represent quasi-steady and transitory states. Additionally, we will show how we can employ Ampere-Maxwell's law to better represent the transitory electric field at the outflow boundary compared to the classical `zero-field' Neumann boundary without requiring any numerical parameters. With these new boundary conditions, we improve the quasi-steady and transitory descriptions of expanding mesothermal plasmas.

</details>
