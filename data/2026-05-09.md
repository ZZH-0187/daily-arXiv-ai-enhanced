### [1] [An Electromagnetic Particle-Particle Method for Relativistic Electron Bunch Dynamics from Early Expansion to Long-Range Transport](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05543)
*Yinjian Zhao,Yibo Liang,Yanan Zhang,Xiaochun Ma,Hui Liu*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 开发了无网格电磁粒子-粒子模型，用于精确模拟地球磁层中相对论性电子束的长距离传输，同时考虑短程电磁相互作用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 粒子-网格方法如粒子-胞方法无法在亚胞尺度上保留精确的粒子间相互作用，对于致密非中性相对论性电子束难以准确捕捉粒子间电磁相互作用及相关的束流发散。

Method: 1) 扩展电磁粒子-粒子模型至多粒子电子束传输 2) 结合李纳-维谢尔场、改进的延迟时间评估程序和相对论性粒子推进器 3) 采用两阶段策略耦合早期自场主导演化与后期长距离地球磁场控制传输

Result: 该方法提供了实用的无网格方法，在短程电磁相互作用重要时能精确模拟相对论性电子束的长距离传输。

Conclusion: 该方法成功解决了相对论性电子束传输中短程与长程相互作用的耦合问题，为地球磁层中高能电子束行为模拟提供了有效的数值工具。

Abstract: Particle-mesh methods, such as the particle-in-cell (PIC) method, cannot retain exact pairwise interaction at sub-cell scales. For dense nonneutral relativistic electron bunches, this makes it difficult to accurately capture the inter-particle electromagnetic interaction and the associated bunch divergence. In this work, the previously developed electromagnetic particle-particle (EM-PP) model for relativistic two-particle interaction is extended to many-particle electron bunch transport in the Earth's magnetosphere. The method combines the Liénard--Wiechert fields, an improved retarded-time evaluation procedure, and a relativistic particle pusher, and adopts a two-stage strategy to couple the dense early self-field-dominated evolution to the later long-range geomagnetic-field-controlled transport. The method provides a practical mesh-free approach for accurately simulating long-range transport of relativistic electron bunches when short-range electromagnetic interaction is important.

### [2] [Proton probing measurements of filamentary electromagnetic structure in laser ablation of solids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05614)
*J. Peebles,P. V. Heuer,D. H. Barnak,V. Y. Zhang,J. R. Davies*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 通过质子照相观察激光直接驱动球形内爆中出现的反常电场/磁场结构，这些场会影响激光靶相互作用并耗散能量。利用OMEGA EP激光器在平面几何下进行简化实验，通过双轴质子照相和4w探针对不同靶材、尺寸、脉冲形状和激光强度的场结构进行测量，发现这些场的增长主要受激光能量和靶材Z值支配，研究证实这些场是由膨胀驱动的Weibel不稳定性引发的二次不稳定性作为主要驱动力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 激光直接驱动球形内爆中观察到的反常电场/磁场结构会影响激光与靶的相互作用，并可能成为能量耗散的途径。为了更好地理解这些场的起源和特性。

Method: 在OMEGA EP激光器上使用平面几何进行简化实验：1）改变靶材料、靶尺寸、脉冲形状和激光强度；2）采用双轴质子照相技术和4w探针测量场结构；3）对质子照相图像进行分析，定量研究场特征的增长规律。

Result: 实验数据表明，这些电场/磁场结构的增长主要受激光能量和靶材料原子序数（Z）支配。数据强有力地支持了以下结论：膨胀驱动的Weibel不稳定性引发的二次不稳定性是这些场的主要驱动力。

Conclusion: 激光直接驱动内爆中观测到的反常电场/磁场结构主要由膨胀驱动的Weibel不稳定性及其导致的二次不稳定性产生，其增长特征与激光能量和靶材原子序数密切相关。这些发现有助于理解和控制激光等离子体相互作用中的场效应。

Abstract: Proton radiography of laser direct-drive spherical implosions has shown anomalous structures that correspond to strong electric or magnetic fields extending throughout the corona. These fields have the ability to affect laser-target interactions and act as an energy sink. To better understand the these fields, simplified experiments were conducted in planar geometry on the OMEGA EP laser at the Laboratory for Laser Energetics. Varying target material, target size, pulse shape, and intensity, and measured the field structure using dual-axis proton radiography and a 4w probe. Proton radiographs were analyzed and quantitatively demonstrate that the growth of these features is dominated by laser energy and target Z. The data strongly supports that a secondary instability as a consequence of the expansion driven Weibel instability in these interactions is the primary driver for these fields.

### [3] [Hugoniot Relation for Multi-Temperature Euler Equations of Compressible Plasma Flows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06131)
*Zhifang Du,Aleksey Sikstel*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 多温度Euler方程存在冲击波解非唯一性问题，本文通过分析发现两种不同的Hugoniot关系对应物理可接受的冲击波解，揭示了微观物理对解决冲击模糊性的重要性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 多温度Euler方程的冲击波解由于模型简化过程中微观物理细节的丢失以及非守恒项的出现而存在固有的模糊性，需要为建立和评估数值方法提供物理依据。

Method: 采用Courant-Friedrichs经典分析方法，推导了多温度情况下和两温度等离子体流动情况下的两种不同的Hugoniot关系，通过结构保持和黏性消失两种数值方法分析解的存在性。

Result: 证明两种Hugoniot关系都满足可接受性条件，揭示了冲击波结构的根本非唯一性，微观物理信息对确定冲击波结构具有决定性作用。

Conclusion: Hugoniot关系不能仅由宏观PDE唯一确定，必须通过实验或第一性原理模拟等外部来源提供，这一认识有助于为模拟不连续等离子体流动建立理论基础。

Abstract: Shock solutions for multi-temperature Euler equations are inherently ambiguous due to the loss of microscopic physical detail during model reduction and occurrence of non-conservative terms. This paper presents a detailed analytical study of shock structures in such models. We derive two distinct Hugoniot relations, each corresponding to a physically admissible shock solution: one for the general multi-temperature case and one for two-temperature plasma flows. Through classical analysis à la Courant--Friedrichs, we demonstrate that both satisfy admissibility conditions, revealing a fundamental non-uniqueness in shock structures. By relating these solutions to existing numerical schemes, the structure preserving and vanishing viscosity approaches, we provide physically justified references for constructing and evaluating discontinuous numerical approximations. In particular, we emphasize that the Hugoniot relation is not uniquely determined by the macroscopic PDEs alone, but must be supplied from external sources such as experiments or first-principles simulations. This insight demonstrates the essential role of microscopic physics in resolving shock ambiguity and contributes to the theoretical foundation for modeling discontinuous plasma flows.
