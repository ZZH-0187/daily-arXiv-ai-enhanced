### [1] [Phase-Selective Excitation of Betatron Oscillations by Nonadiabatic Magnetic-Field Switching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07305)
*R. S. Anandu,B. Ramakrishna*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 非绝热关闭横向磁场可选择性控制激光尾波加速器中电子的betatron振荡相位，通过干涉效应调控振幅


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为等离子体加速器中的betatron振荡提供直接的相位控制机制，以调控betatron辐射频谱而不影响纵向加速

Method: 建立理论模型，引入无量纲开关参数χ=ω_βL_s/c区分非绝热和绝热区域；采用PIC粒子模拟验证预言

Result: 模拟确认了理论标度律，证明磁场开关可调节betatron辐射频谱，同时基本不影响纵向加速

Conclusion: 磁场开关为等离子体加速中相对论betatron振荡的相位控制提供了直接机制

Abstract: Nonadiabatic removal of an external transverse magnetic field provides a phase-selective mechanism for controlling betatron oscillations in laser wakefield accelerators. When the field is switched off on a timescale shorter than the betatron period, the equilibrium orbit shifts abruptly and acts as an impulsive transverse drive. The induced motion interferes coherently with the preexisting betatron oscillation, leading to phase-dependent enhancement or suppression of the oscillation amplitude. A theoretical model shows that the excitation is governed by the dimensionless switching parameter $χ=ω_βL_s/c$, which distinguishes nonadiabatic and adiabatic regimes. Particle-in-cell simulations confirm the predicted scaling and demonstrate controllable modulation of the betatron radiation spectrum while leaving longitudinal acceleration largely unaffected. These results establish magnetic-field switching as a direct mechanism for phase control of relativistic betatron oscillations in plasma-based accelerators.

### [2] [Plasma GraphRAG: Physics-Grounded Parameter Selection for Gyrokinetic Simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06279)

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 从精选的等离子体文献构建领域知识图谱，整合图检索增强生成技术，通过结构化检索图谱锚定的实体和关系，使大语言模型能生成背景感知的参数推荐

By constructing a domain-specific knowledge graph from curated plasma literature and enabling structured retrieval over graph-anchored entities and relations, Plasma GraphRAG enables LLMs to generate accurate, context-aware recommendations. Extensive evaluations across five metrics, comprehensiveness, diversity, grounding, hallucination, and empowerment, demonstrate that Plasma GraphRAG outperforms vanilla RAG by over $10\%$ in overall quality and reduces hallucination rates by up to $25\%$. {Beyond enhancing simulation reliability, Plasma GraphRAG offers a methodology for accelerating scientific discovery across complex, data-rich domains.

### [3] [Monte Carlo Simulations of Suprathermal Enhancement in Advanced Nuclear Fusion Fuels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06769)
*Marcus Borscz,Thomas A. Mehlhorn,Patrick A. Burr,Igor Morozov,Sergey Pikuz*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究通过蒙特卡洛模拟分析了DT、氘、11BH3和11BHDT燃料中的超热核反应特性，发现早期的氘临界性预测被高估一个数量级以上，仅DT在无中子泄漏时存在临界区，11BH3中超热增强最大仅40%，排除了α粒子驱动的雪崩机制可能性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究超热核反应在惯性约束聚变中的作用，评估不同燃料中由高能粒子引发的非热核反应的贡献，澄清以往对纯氘临界性的过高估计，为指导实际ICF应用提供理论依据。

Method: 开发了0D时间相关的蒙特卡洛模拟代码，采用改进的Li-Petrasso阻止本领计算方法，考虑热展宽截面、各向异性核弹性散射和中子弹性散射，并引入p11B α粒子能谱的物理模型。

Result: 1. 早期纯氘超热临界性预测被高估一个量级，无实际密度-温度条件能维持自持链式反应。2. 仅DT燃料在无中子泄漏时存在临界区。3. 11BH3中最优质子能量为4MeV，但超热增益不超过初始束能的40%。4. α粒子驱动的雪崩机制被排除，因离子阻止主要来自小能量转移碰撞。5. 超热倍增过程主要由中子驱动的离子上散射主导，在纯无中子燃料中作用有限。

Conclusion: 超热核反应在多数燃料中作用有限，仅DT具备临界条件但需完全约束中子；纯无中子燃料中难以实现显著能量增益，为惯性约束聚变燃料选择提供了关键界限参数。

Abstract: Suprathermal fusion reactions, initiated by energetic particles slowing down and scattering in dense plasmas, can modify the burn dynamics at inertial confinement fusion (ICF) regimes. A 0D time-dependent Monte-Carlo code has been developed to assess the suprathermal energy gain from fast fusions in DT, deuterium, $^{11}$BH$_3$ and $^{11}$BHDT fuels. It incorporates modified Li-Petrasso stopping powers, thermal broadening of cross-sections, anisotropic nuclear elastic and neutron elastic scattering, and a physical model for the p$^{11}$B alpha-particle spectra. Results show that earlier predictions of suprathermal criticality in pure deuterium are overestimated by more than an order of magnitude; no realistic density-temperature regime supports a self-sustaining chain reaction. Only DT demonstrates a critical regime provided there is no neutron leakage. Fast protons in $^{11}$BH$_3$ have an optimum energy of 4 MeV for maximising suprathermal enhancement. In this case the additional energy from fast fusions is unlikely to exceed 40% of the initial proton beam energy. The possibility of an alpha-particle-driven "avalanche" mechanism is ruled out since the ionic stopping is dominated by collisions involving small energy transfer. Suprathermal multiplication processes are dominated by neutron-driven ion up-scattering and likely play a limited role in purely aneutronic fuels.

### [4] [Complex plasma with Janus particles as a model active-matter system](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06850)
*Volodymyr Nosenko*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 活性物质（如Janus粒子）在气体放电等离子体中的实验研究


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 活性物质系统展示了丰富的物理现象和潜在应用价值，但其天然复杂性使得模型系统研究变得重要。复杂等离子体中的活性粒子为研究不易实验的自然活性物质系统提供了优良模型。

Method: 在氩气射频放电等离子体鞘层中悬浮单层微米塑料微球（一侧镀薄金层），通过激光诱导光泳力和不对称离子阻力组合驱动粒子。

Result: 这种高度驱动的惯性活性物质系统中，增强的粒子活动导致集体粒子动力学特征包括：速度场的扩展自相似性、间歇性、以及具有非通用标度指数的直接能量级联的出现。

Conclusion: 复杂等离子体中的活性粒子是优秀的模型系统，可以增强我们对不易实验的自然活性物质系统的理解，该实验观察到了独特的集体动力学现象。

Abstract: Active matter classifies systems consisting of self-propelled units which convert the energy stored locally or extracted from their environment into directed motion. It has recently attracted considerable attention due to rich new physics it displays and potential applications in various fields including materials science. Active matter found in nature is inherently complex, so model systems are of interest where the main relevant features can be isolated and studied in laboratory experiments. An interesting instance of active matter is a suspension of active particles (e.g., the so-called Janus particles, where the two halves have different properties) in a gas discharge plasma. Such complex plasmas with active particles are excellent model systems which can enhance our understanding of natural active matter systems not easily amenable to experiment. In the present experimental study, micrometer-size plastic microspheres with thin gold coating on one side were suspended as a single layer in the plasma sheath of a radio-frequency discharge in argon and driven by a combination of laser-induced photophoretic force and asymmetric ion drag force. Enhanced particle activity in this highly driven, inertial active-matter system leads to collective particle dynamics characterized by extended self-similarity of the velocity field, intermittency, and the emergence of a direct energy cascade with non-universal scaling exponent.
