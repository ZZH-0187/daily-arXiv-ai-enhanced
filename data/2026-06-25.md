### [1] [Rise Time Effects of a Portable Inductive Energy Storage Pulse Generator on NO Production in Spark Discharges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24905)
*Cheng-Hsiao Hsueh,Yu-Hsuan Chen,Chao-Yu Chen,Yun-Chien Cheng*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 研发便携式电感储能脉冲功率系统，通过并联电容调节上升时间研究大气火花放电中NO生成。发现NO产量主要取决于能量输入而非波形上升时间，为便携式等离子NO生产系统设计提供指导。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 尽管先前研究关注了脉冲能量、脉宽和重复频率，但上升时间的作用仍未被充分探索，且现有具有可调上升时间的脉冲发生器体积庞大，不适合便携应用。本研究旨在解决这些局限性。

Method: 1. 在紧凑型IES架构中研究上升时间控制策略
2. 采用并联高压电容器生成可调波形
3. 使用光学发射光谱法测量激发态N2和NO伽马发射
4. 使用NO分析仪监测NO浓度
5. 估算电子激发温度、等离子体吸收功率和旋转温度来分析波形特性对等离子体化学的影响

Result: 1. 上升时间可调节，但系统中与脉冲宽度和输入能量耦合
2. 比较光谱强度、NO浓度、电子激发温度和吸收能量表明，NO产生主要取决于能量输入而非上升时间
3. 不同波形条件下电极温度变化极小，表明NO形成主要受电子碰撞和电离过程影响

Conclusion: 本研究提出了一种便携式简化IES高压脉冲功率发生器，识别了上升时间控制相关的约束条件，证明了波形调节的可行性，阐明了主导NO生成的主导机制，为未来便携式等离子体基NO生产系统的设计提供了参考。

Abstract: This study investigates pulse waveform adjustment in a portable inductive energy storage (IES) pulsed power system and its effect on nitric oxide (NO) generation in atmospheric spark discharges. While previous studies have focused on pulse energy, pulse width, and repetition rate, the role of rise time remains insufficiently explored. Existing pulse generators with adjustable rise time are also bulky and unsuitable for portable applications. To address these limitations, this study examines rise time control strategies within a compact IES architecture and employs a parallel high-voltage capacitor to generate adjustable waveforms. Optical emission spectroscopy was used to measure excited N2 and NO gamma emissions, and a NO analyzer was used to monitor NO concentration. In addition, electron excitation temperature, plasma-absorbed power, and rotational temperature were estimated to clarify the influence of waveform characteristics on plasma chemistry. The results show that although rise time can be adjusted, it is coupled with pulse width and input energy in the system. Comparative analysis of spectral intensities, NO concentration, electron excitation temperature, and absorbed energy indicates that NO production depends primarily on energy input rather than rise time. Electrode temperature varies only minimally under different waveform conditions, suggesting that NO formation is mainly influenced by electron impact and ionization processes. This work proposes a portable and simplified IES-based high-voltage pulsed power generator, identifies the constraints associated with rise time control, and demonstrates the feasibility of waveform adjustment. The findings clarify the dominant mechanisms governing NO generation and provide a reference for the design of future portable plasma-based NO production systems.

### [2] [A toroidally spectral field solver in the X-point Gyrokinetic Code for accurate simulation of reduced magneto-hydrodynamic modes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25213)
*Robert Hager,C. S. Chang,Thomas Gade,Eric Held,Seung-Hoe Ku,Alexey Mishchenko,Aaron Scheinberg,Benjamin Sturdevant*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: XGC代码中实现了一个新的场求解器，用于更精确地模拟大尺度MHD不稳定模态（如内弯折模、撕裂模和剥离模），同时保持对微观湍流模拟的兼容性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 虽然XGC的常规场求解器在典型的微观湍流尺度（离子Larmor半径量级）上对任意纵横比的托卡马克等离子体已具有足够精度，但对于大尺度（低toroidal模数）的MHD型不稳定模态（如内弯折模、撕裂模和剥离模）需要更高精度的场求解器。

Method: 推导新的场求解器，放弃传统的poloidal磁场远小于toroidal磁场的大纵横比假设，同时在toroidal方向使用谱离散化来控制数值复杂度。新的场求解器可以与原有的常规求解器并行运行。

Result: 新的场求解器通过解析预测以及与其他代码（ORB5和NIMROD）的验证得到了验证。该方法能够覆盖从大尺度MHD型模态到小尺度微观湍流的完整谱段，且可以与原有的常规求解器并行运行。

Conclusion: XGC代码中新的场求解器成功扩展了其能力范围，能够同时处理从大尺度MHD不稳定到小尺度微观湍流的完整谱段。该求解器放弃了传统的大纵横比近似，采用谱方法实现数值可控，并通过与解析预测和其他代码的验证证明了其准确性。

Abstract: A new field solver has been implemented in the global electromagnetic total-$f$ gyrokinetic particle-in-cell code XGC to extend the code's capability to large-scale reduced MHD-type instabilities in tokamak plasma. While XGC's regular field solver is accurate at typical microturbulence scales of the order of the ion Larmor radius in tokamaks with arbitrary aspect ratio, a more accurate field solver is required for large-scale (i.e., low toroidal mode number) MHD-type modes such as internal kink, tearing and peeling modes. The higher accuracy of the new field solver is achieved by dropping the (large aspect ratio) assumption that the poloidal magnetic field is much smaller than the toroidal magnetic field, while its numerical complexity is controlled by using a spectral discretization in the toroidal direction. To cover the entire spectrum from large-scale MHD-type modes to small-scale microturbulence, the regular and the new field solver can be run alongside each other. This work details the derivation of the new field solver, analyzes the differences between the XGC's regular and new field solvers, and verifies the new field solver against analytic predictions and the gyrokinetic code ORB5 and the MHD code NIMROD.

### [3] [Nanosecond-resolved 266 nm Mach-Zehnder interferometry for electron-density measurements of dense plasmas generated in supercritical fluids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25327)
*Kyusang Cho,Juho Lee,Gunsu Yun*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 研究人员开发了266纳米马赫-曾德尔干涉仪，用于测量100巴超临界流体氦中激光产生稠密等离子体的电子密度，实现了纳秒级时间分辨。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 开发适用于高压超临界流体环境中稠密等离子体的高时间分辨率电子密度诊断技术，因为传统的诊断方法在这种高温高压条件下可能受到限制。

Method: 使用266纳米紫外探针光束的马赫-曾德尔干涉仪，通过1064纳米泵浦脉冲在超临界流体氦中产生等离子体。采用ICCD成像系统记录等离子体诱导的相移，使用二维傅里叶变换方法重建相移图，通过阿贝尔反演得到局部电子密度分布。

Result: 测量得到的最大线积分电子密度通过阿贝尔反演后，局部电子密度约为2.5×10¹⁸ cm⁻³。通过评估自由-自由吸收、折射效应和碰撞频率影响，确定在这些实验条件下推断的电子密度误差不超过一个数量级。

Conclusion: 266纳米紫外干涉技术适用于高压超临界流体中稠密等离子体的纳秒级密度诊断，为这类极端环境下的等离子体研究提供了有效的测量手段。

Abstract: We developed a nanosecond-resolved 266 nm Mach-Zehnder interferometer for electron-density measurements of dense laser-produced plasmas generated in 100-bar supercritical-fluid (SCF) helium. A 1064 nm pump pulse was focused into the SCF helium medium, and the plasma-induced phase shift of a 266 nm UV probe beam was recorded using an ICCD-based interferometric imaging system. Plasma-arm-only and reference-arm-only images were used to normalize raw interferograms and improve the effective fringe visibility. The corrected interferograms were analyzed using a two-dimensional Fourier-transform method to reconstruct phase-shift maps, which were converted into line-integrated electron-density distributions through the plasma dispersion relation. Assuming cylindrical symmetry, Abel inversion was applied to the plasma with the largest line-integrated electron density, yielding a maximum local electron density of approximately \(2.5\times10^{18}~\mathrm{cm^{-3}}\). The measurement fidelity was evaluated by considering free-free absorption of the probe beam, probe-beam refraction by plasma electron density gradients, and effect of finite-collision-frequency effects on the plasma dielectric response. These estimates indicate that the inferred electron density is not altered by more than an order of magnitude under the present experimental conditions. The present system demonstrates the applicability of 266 nm UV interferometry to nanosecond-resolved density diagnostics of dense plasmas in high-pressure supercritical fluids.

### [4] [Numerical thermalization in $n$-D particle-in-cell simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25528)
*R. M. Park,C. H. Moore,S. D. Baalrud*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: PIC模拟中的人工碰撞性可通过理论预测，3D模拟在解析德拜长度时难以达到物理热化时间。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探究PIC模拟方法中由于大权重宏粒子使用（粒子数分辨率不足）导致的人工高碰撞性问题，以及粒子形状效应对不同维度（1D、2D、3D）中大权重宏粒子的补偿程度。

Method: 通过分析速度自相关函数的衰减率计算PIC模拟中的碰撞时间，并与Okuda、Birdsall和Langdon的动力学理论进行直接比较。

Result: 该理论能够准确预测不同网格间隔、等离子体条件和模拟维度下的碰撞时间。研究提供了预测PIC模拟中自洽库仑相互作用时间尺度的方法，从而可表征数值热化作为网格间隔和宏粒子权重函数的相关性和影响。

Conclusion: 粒子云网格（PIC）模拟中的人工高碰撞性可通过Okuda-Birdsall-Langdon理论准确预测，表明在实际3D模拟中难以达到物理热化时间或近似无碰撞Vlasov极限。

Abstract: The particle-in-cell (PIC) simulation method is often understood to solve the collisionless Vlasov equation due to the finite shape of its macroparticles. In reality, it can suffer from artificially high collisionality due to the underresolution of particle number; i.e., the use of a large macroparticle weight. The degree to which particle shape effects compensate for a large macroparticle weight in 1D, 2D, and 3D is presented. The collision time is calculated from PIC simulations based on the decay rate of the velocity autocorrelation function and compared directly with the kinetic theory of Okuda, Birdsall, and Langdon. The theory is found to accurately predict the simulated collision time with varied grid spacings, plasma conditions, and simulation dimensionalities. The result is a means to predict the timescale of self-consistent Coulomb interactions in the PIC simulation and thus characterize the relevance and implications of numerical thermalization as a function of grid spacing and macroparticle weight. It is determined that reaching the physical thermalization time, let alone approximating the collisionless Vlasov limit, may often be intractable in 3D for macroparticle sizes that resolve the Debye length.

### [5] [Laser-intensity-spike-dominated hot electron generation from two-plasmon decay instability driven by moderate-bandwidth pulses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26054)

Method: 在低相干"昆吾"激光装置上进行实验，使用粒子模拟方法验证理论分析

These findings suggest that mitigating hot electron generation requires suppressing these intensity spikes.
