Dense surrogate scans quantify the dependence of carbon radiation front motion on separatrix density, divertor geometry and fuelling location. Nitrogen seeding scans reveal a strong dependence on seeding location, with high-field-side seeding rapidly reducing core-boundary electron temperature, while divertor seeding produces little response. Batch inference for all twelve outputs at 786,432 operating points takes 1.76 s on GPU, compared with a median wall time of 2.61 hours using four CPU cores for separate EDGE2D-EIRENE simulations. These models enable rapid comparison of geometry and source-location effects on divertor and core-boundary conditions within the sampled simulation domain.
