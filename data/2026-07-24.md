### [1] [yancc: A GPU-accelerated, differentiable solver for neoclassical transport in tokamaks and stellarators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20861)
*Rory Conlin,Matt Landreman*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: yancc是一个新的GPU加速求解器，用于解决托卡马克和仿星器中的漂移动理学方程，并计算新经典输运通量、流动和电流。该求解器在计算速度和内存使用上相比现有方法有显著提升，并具有完全可微分特性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 漂移动理学方程在数值求解上具有挑战性，存在强对流主导、再循环流动、内部边界层、严重各向异性和高维度等问题。现有方法在计算效率和内存使用方面存在限制，需要一种更高效、内存友好的求解器，特别是能够支持梯度优化和伴随敏感性分析的工具。

Method: yancc结合了速度空间的Maxwell多项式配置网格与俯仰角度和磁通面坐标的有限差分法，采用改进的上风格式以提高多重网格法的对角占优性。生成的线性系统使用多重网格预处理的Krylov方法求解。该代码基于JAX构建，完全可微分。

Result: 与MONKES和SFINCS的基准测试显示，在不同碰撞率、几何形状和多物种配置下结果均能保持在1%的一致性范围内。yancc相比SFINCS在每次扫描中实现约一个数量级的加速，同时使用一个数量级更少的内存，且运行时间在整个碰撞率范围内保持几乎稳定。

Conclusion: yancc的快速计算能力、低内存占用和可微分特性使其非常适合集成到仿星器优化工作流、不确定性量化和剖面预测中，为聚变等离子体物理学研究提供了强大的数值工具。

Abstract: We present yancc, a new GPU-accelerated solver for the drift kinetic equation that computes neoclassical transport fluxes, flows, and currents in tokamaks and stellarators. The drift kinetic equation is challenging to solve numerically due to strong advection-dominance, recirculating flows, internal boundary layers, severe anisotropy, and high dimensionality. The code solves both the full four-dimensional drift kinetic equation (retaining speed-dependent collisions, energy scattering, and full interspecies coupling), and the reduced monoenergetic form. The discretization combines a Maxwell polynomial collocation grid in speed with finite differences in pitch angle and the flux surface coordinates, using a modified upwind stencil designed to improve diagonal dominance for multigrid efficiency. The resulting linear system is solved with a multigrid-preconditioned Krylov method. Built in JAX, yancc is fully differentiable, enabling gradient-based optimization and adjoint sensitivity analysis. Benchmarks against MONKES and SFINCS show agreement within 1\% across a range of collisionalities, geometries, and multi-species configurations. yancc achieves roughly an order of magnitude speedup over SFINCS on a per-scan basis while using an order of magnitude less memory, with runtime remaining nearly flat across the full range of collisionality. The combination of speed, low memory footprint, and differentiability makes yancc well suited for integration into stellarator optimization workflows, uncertainty quantification, and profile prediction.

### [2] [Langmuir probe and infrared thermography measurements of wide and narrow heat flux profiles in the ST40 tokamak](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21362)
*T. Brewer,X. Zhang,C. Marsden,A. Scarabosio,J. Bryant,P. F. Buxton,M. Fontana,T. Gray,N. Lopez,L. Martinelli,A. Rengle,E. J. C. Tinacba,E. Vekshina,H. V. Willett*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: ST40在H模等离子体中观测到窄热流分布（λ_{q,near}<1 mm）。研究发现这种窄分布与流向偏滤器的增强电子电流相关，考虑电子电流贡献后模型能够准确再现红外热成像的观测结果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: ST40装置在H模等离子体中观测到非常窄的热流分布，近刮削层的衰减长度小于1毫米。这促使研究者深入了解窄热流分布的形成机制以及与电子电流的关系。

Method: 使用偏滤器朗缪尔探针测量与红外热成像系统进行比较，测量上层单零偏滤器等离子体在L模和无ELM的H模状态下的热流分布。通过朗缪尔探针测量流向接地偏滤器的刮削层电流。

Result: 观测到近刮削层热流衰减长度λ_{q,near}<1 mm。朗缪尔探针测量的刮削层电流分布显示出与λ_{q,near}相似的指数衰减趋势，表明近刮削层热流与流向偏滤器的增强电子电流相关。考虑电子电流对热流传输系数的贡献能够准确再现红外热成像系统观测到的窄分布特征。

Conclusion: 通过对ST40装置窄热流分布的研究，发现近刮削层窄热流分布与增强的流向偏滤器的电子电流有关。将电子电流贡献纳入热流传输系数对于准确捕捉红外热成像系统观察到的窄分布至关重要。未来ST40的升级将支持更多关于内外靶面功率衰减长度的研究。

Abstract: Very narrow heat flux profiles have been observed on ST40 with decay lengths in the near scrape-off-layer (SOL) of $λ_{q,near}<$~1~mm in H-mode plasmas. Measurements from divertor Langmuir probes are compared with an infrared (IR) thermography system for upper-single null diverted plasmas in L-mode and ELM-free H-mode. SOL current to the grounded divertor targets is measured from the Langmuir probes with profiles exhibiting a similar exponential decay to $λ_{q,near}$ suggesting the near SOL heat flux is related to enhanced electron current to the divertor. Inclusion of electron current contributions to the heat flux transmission coefficient is crucial in capturing similar narrow profiles to those from the IR thermography system. Ongoing upgrades to ST40 will enable more investigations on SOL power decay lengths on inboard and outboard targets in double and single null configurations.
