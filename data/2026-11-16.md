### [1] [Reactive feedback for suppression of the periodic transient beam loading effect in storage rings: theory and simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17912)

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 本文研究了第四代同步辐射光源中利用谐波腔实现束团长度的增加时，由于瞬态束流负载效应引起的多束团不稳定性问题。基于已有的反应式反馈控制提议，建立了频域理论模型，并在时域进行了模拟验证。结果表明，理想化的纯反应式反馈能够抑制该不稳定性，且硬件限制条件与现有束团逐束反馈系统兼容。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 第四代同步辐射光源中，谐波腔用于增加束团长度，但纵向聚焦减弱和高总R/Q值使束流容易受到周期性瞬态束流负载效应的影响，这种多束团不稳定性表现为束团质心和长度的缓慢振荡，且传统反馈阻尼难以抑制。为解决这一问题，本文基于近期提出的反应式反馈控制方法，进行理论建模和模拟验证。

Method: 建立了包含反馈电压的线性化Vlasov方程的频域理论模型，推导出由反馈传递函数修正的Lebedev方程。同时，在宏粒子追踪代码STABLE中实现了时域的反应式反馈模拟。方法应用于MAX IV存储环的双射频系统参数，并考虑了硬件限制条件。

Result: 理论分析和模拟均表明，超过一定增益阈值后，理想化的纯反应式反馈能够有效抑制PTBL效应，且在不同束流强度和R/Q值下理论模拟结果吻合良好。硬件限制分析显示反馈增益、延迟和带宽要求与现有束团逐束反馈系统兼容。使用HALF存储环参数的半解析结果表明，反应式反馈可扩展至三射频系统，但需要更高的增益阈值。

Conclusion: 反应式反馈是控制第四代同步辐射光源中周期性瞬态束流负载效应的有效方法，理论模型与模拟结果一致，且硬件实现可行性高。该方法可推广到更复杂的射频系统，为实际工程应用提供了理论依据和技术指导。

The reduced longitudinal focusing, combined with high total $R/Q$ of these cavities, can make the beam susceptible to the periodic transient beam loading (PTBL) effect, a multibunch instability characterized by slow oscillations in bunch centroids and lengths and by its resistance to conventional feedback damping. In this work, building on a recent proposal of applying reactive feedback to control the PTBL effect, we developed a frequency-domain theoretical model, including the feedback voltage in the linearized Vlasov equation to derive a Lebedev equation modified by the feedback transfer function. The reactive feedback was also implemented in the time domain in the macroparticle tracking code \texttt{STABLE}. The methods were applied to the parameters of the double-rf system of the MAX IV storage ring. Both approaches show that, above a threshold gain, an idealized pure reactive feedback is able to suppress the PTBL effect, with good agreement between theory and simulations across beam currents and $R/Q$ values. Realistic hardware limitations were included in the analysis, determining the limits for feedback gain, latency, and bandwidth, which are compatible with existing bunch-by-bunch feedback systems. Using the HALF storage ring parameters, the semi-analytical results indicate that the reactive feedback can be extended to suppress the PTBL effect in triple-rf systems, albeit at higher threshold gains than for double-rf systems.
