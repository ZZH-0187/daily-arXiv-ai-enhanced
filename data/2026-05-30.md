### [2] [Updates on Impedance Studies for the FCC-ee High Energy Booster](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29799)
*Keon Hee Kim,Adnan Ghribi,Santiago Martinez,Quentin Bruant,Antoine Chance,Barbara Dalena,Chiara Antuono,Dora Gibellieri,Carlo Zannini,Frank Zimmermann,Ahmad Mashal,Mauro Migliorati,Mikhail Zobov*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: FCC-ee高能增强环的阻抗模型在铜真空室电阻壁分析基础上大幅扩展，纳入了射频腔、波纹管和束流位置监测器等多种部件，通过三维电磁仿真和解析方法建立了完整的阻抗和尾场预算。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在完成未来环形对撞机(FCC)可行性研究后，需要建立更全面的高能增强环阻抗模型，为束流动力学研究提供准确的基础数据，包括横向耦合束不稳定性分析和单束团追踪模拟。

Method: 采用三维电磁仿真与解析方法相结合的方式，对RF腔体、波纹管、束流位置监测器等关键部件的阻抗和尾场贡献进行全面评估和比较。

Result: 识别了主要的噪声源和需要进一步研究的部件，更新后的模型为主流束动力学研究提供了基础，并可用于精细化集体效应分析和稳定性裕度评估。

Conclusion: 扩展后的阻抗模型为FCC-ee高能增强环的集体效应研究提供了更全面的基础，支持未来对不稳定性裕度和缓解要求的评估，有助于优化机器设计。

Abstract: Following the Future Circular Collider (FCC) Feasibility Study completion, the impedance model for the FCC-ee High-Energy Booster (HEB) has been significantly expanded beyond the initial copper vacuum pipe resistive wall analysis. This paper presents a comprehensive impedance and wake budget incorporating RF cavities, bellows, and beam position monitors, evaluated through 3D electromagnetic simulations and analytical methods.
  The updated model provides the basis for future beam dynamics studies, including transverse coupled bunch instability analyses and single bunch tracking simulations. The present work focuses on the construction and comparison of the main impedance and wake contributions, identifying the dominant sources and the components requiring further investigation. These results will be used to refine the HEB collective effects studies and to support future assessments of instability margins and mitigation requirements.

</details>
