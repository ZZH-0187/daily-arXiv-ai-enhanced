TL;DR: 该论文提出了一种针对暖密物质中离子对自由电子碰撞阻止的模型，基于等离子体动力学理论，通过Boltzmann-Uehling-Uhlenbeck方程考虑电子费米简并效应，使用平均原子模型计算量子散射截面。

Motivation: 开发一个计算效率高且能准确描述暖密物质中离子阻止特性的模型，以克服传统密度泛函理论计算成本高的问题。

Method: 基于等离子体动力学理论，采用Boltzmann-Uehling-Uhlenbeck方程处理电子费米简并，通过平均原子模型计算量子散射截面，考虑强库仑关联效应。

Result: 模型在数电子伏温度、近固体密度的氘中与含时密度泛函理论计算结果精度相当，但计算成本显著降低，且能捕捉从经典极限到简并极限的等离子体转变。

Conclusion: 开发的模型在暖密物质中离子阻止计算方面具有显著计算效率优势，同时保持了与复杂从头算方法相当的准确性，能够描述不同物理极限下的行为。

Abstract: A model for the collisional stopping of ions on free electrons in warm dense matter is developed and explored. It is based on plasma kinetic theory, but with modifications to address the warm dense matter regime. Specifically, it uses the Boltzmann-Uehling-Uhlenbeck kinetic equation to incorporate effects of Fermi degeneracy of electrons. The cross section is computed from quantum scattering of electrons and ions occuring via the potential of mean force derived from an average atom model, which incorporates effects of strong Coulomb correlations. Predictions from this model show comparable accuracy to results from time-dependent density functional theory calculations for deuterium near solid density and a temperature of several electronvolts, at a fraction of the computational cost. Further, the model captures the transition of a plasma from the classical limit to the degenerate limit, including qualitative behaviors of solid state theory.
