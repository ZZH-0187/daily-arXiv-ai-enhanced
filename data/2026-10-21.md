### [1] [Dynamics of thermalization in classical three-dimensional many-body systems: Non-Maxwellian distributions and the role of anisotropic trapping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27368)
*Roberto Onofrio,Bala Sundaram*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 研究发现三维系统中热化过程会出现偏离麦克斯韦-玻尔兹曼分布的κ分布特征，且热化行为与各向异性囚禁、粒子数标度反常有关。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究三维系统中两浴热化的特征，探讨其与一维情况的差异，特别是能量分布的变化和各向异性囚禁的影响。

Method: 基于早期研究的三维浴模型，分析三维系统中热化过程的能量分布特征，考察各向异性囚禁条件下的热化行为，研究粒子数标度反常现象。

Result: 三维系统中热化过程中能量分布会偏离麦克斯韦-玻尔兹曼分布，呈现κ分布特征，分布峰值发生变化；各向异性囚禁会影响热化过程，并导致粒子数标度出现反常行为。

Conclusion: 三维系统中的热化过程展现出与一维系统不同的特征，特别是能量分布的κ分布偏离和各向异性囚禁引起的标度反常，这些发现有助于理解湍流行为和非线性在多体经典系统中的相互作用。

Abstract: The physics of interacting baths is of interest in a variety of contexts, ranging from ultracold atoms to ionized gases. While many features can already be captured in a one-dimensional model, others are specific to the situation of two or three-dimensional systems. In this paper, we focus on three-dimensional features of a model for thermalization between two baths that we have explored in earlier publications. In contrast to the one-dimensional situation we show that, en route to thermalization, deviations from the Maxwell-Boltzmann energy distributions more akin to $κ$-distributions are observed. In three dimensions, these can be seen in changes at the peak of the distribution, which is precluded by the expected monotonic behavior of the energy distributions in one dimension. Furthermore, we consider thermalization for anisotropic trapping and explore its role in the onset of anomalous scaling with respect to the particle number. These results may be pertinent to the understanding of the interplay between turbulent behavior and nonlinearities for a variety of many-body classical systems.

### [2] [Generation of non-Maxwell-Boltzmann energy distributions during thermalization of interacting baths](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27369)
*Roberto Onofrio,Bala Sundaram*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 研究两个热库之间通过高斯相互作用调制的热化过程，分析相互作用范围、强度以及浴参数（质量、陷阱频率）对偏离麦克斯韦-玻尔兹曼分布的影响


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究粒子间相互作用如何影响热化动力学，特别是在高斯相互作用下，相互作用范围、强度以及系统参数如何导致偏离平衡态麦克斯韦-玻尔兹曼分布

Method: 通过高统计量的数值实验，研究两个热库之间的热化过程，其中种间相互作用由高斯距离依赖的哈密顿量调节，分析不同相互作用范围、强度以及浴参数（质量、陷阱频率）对空间重叠的影响

Result: 强相互作用下，任何范围都会因潜在的相互作用能突然转化为粒子动能而产生偏离；长程相互作用在弱耦合区域也会产生偏离；非均匀约束进一步导致偏离麦克斯韦-玻尔兹曼分布，出现瞬态双温度分布；得到了弱相互作用区域平衡温度的简单表达式

Conclusion: 高斯相互作用调制的热化过程展现复杂动力学行为，研究结果对外放热化学反应、超冷原子混合物和自持燃烧等离子体动力学有潜在应用价值

Abstract: We report on numerical experiments of thermalization between two reservoirs in which interspecies interactions are mediated via a Hamiltonian with a Gaussian dependence upon the distance between the particles. By using relatively high statistics, we are able to study deviations from the initially imprinted Maxwell-Boltzmann energy distributions as a function of the range and strength of the interaction, as well as the parameters of the two baths such as masses, trapping frequencies, that strongly affect the spatial overlap of the two species. At large interaction strengths and for any range, deviations are attributable to the sudden conversion of latent interaction energy into kinetic energy of the particles. In addition, long range interactions generate deviations even for interaction strengths in the weak regime, and non-uniform confinement introduces a further source of deviation from the Maxwell-Boltzmann energy distribution. This latter results in transient two temperature Maxwell-Boltzmann distributions suggestive of an inverse cascading route from inter-particle dynamics to thermalization. In the weakly-interacting regime, a simple expression for the equilibrium temperature is also obtained and discussed. These findings may be of relevance in the dynamics of exothermic chemical reactions, in the physics of ultracold atomic mixtures, and in self-sustained burning plasmas.

### [3] [Exact Nonsymmetric Vacuum Magnetic Fields with Nested Toroidal Surfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28466)
*W. Sengupta,A. Bhattacharjee,T. J. Klotz,A. O. Brown,J. W. Burby,V. N. Duarte,S. Patil,S. Buller*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该论文提出了一种构造性方法，用于生成精确的真空磁场，其磁力线填充嵌套的环形曲面（无对称性），基于代数曲线上的复势理论和孤立子理论中的双周期行波。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 动机是开发一种能够解析表达、不含连续空间对称性的精密磁场结构，用于改善准对称托卡马克和仿星器中的磁约束，并为标准仿星器代码提供精确的测试基准。

Method: 方法结合了代数曲线上的复势理论和孤立子理论中的双周期行波，通过闭合形式表达磁场、势能和曲面标记。自由参数可以设置旋转变换和磁剪切，包括恒定的无理旋转变换。

Result: 获得了精确的真空磁场解析解，其磁力线填充嵌套的环形曲面；这些场同时也是精确的势流场，具有环形流面；该方法为正则仿星器代码提供了严格的测试案例。

Conclusion: 该研究为无对称性嵌套环形磁面提供了一种新颖的解析构造方法，这些精确解在等离子体磁约束优化和数值代码验证方面具有重要价值。

Abstract: We give a constructive method for exact vacuum magnetic fields whose lines fill nested toroidal surfaces in ordinary space, without continuous spatial symmetry. Complex potential theory on an algebraic curve, combined with doubly periodic traveling waves from soliton theory, gives closed forms for the field, its potential and a surface label. Free parameters set the rotational transform and the magnetic shear, including exactly constant irrational transform. The same fields are exact potential flows with toroidal stream surfaces, and give stringent tests for standard stellarator codes.

### [4] [Physics-constrained graph closures and working-point conditioning for a fast surrogate of linac beam dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28250)
*Bowen Zhang,Dazhang Huang,Nengyuan Zhang,Duan Gu,Chao Feng*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 研究基于图神经网络的加速器束流动力学代理模型，通过设计网络架构保持物理定律对称性，验证发现特征归一化对训练效果影响巨大。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当图神经网络用于估算物理量时，其框架、边方向、聚合和特征归一化决定了它能违反哪些物理定律。错误选择会留下损失函数无法检测的系统误差。本文旨在构建一个直线加速器束流动力学的代理模型，利用物理约束来提高精度。

Method: 构建基于图神经网络的束流动力学代理模型，通过设计网络架构保持空间电荷自作用力的对称性（自作用力为零）。采用有界闭包处理空间电荷、短程尾场和相干同步辐射效应。模型通过工作点约束的均方根包络方程进行蒸馏，形成一个可条件于15个机器设置的统一模型。

Result: 在上海软X射线自由电子激光直线加速器18.4米L0+L1段上，该模型在50个未见设置上预测横向束包络的误差约为7%，同时预测束心轨迹和发射度。GPU推理时间仅0.38秒。验证显示，当破坏关键特征对称性时，不变性指标会恶化5-8个数量级。数据驱动的输出范围归一化将初始误差降低了2.2-8.5倍，固定了收敛速度。

Conclusion: 图神经网络架构的物理约束设计对保持物理定律对称性至关重要，特征归一化选择对训练效果有决定性影响。混合架构与纯数据驱动架构的精度差异随工作点和观测密度变化，集体效应强的场景（如磁压缩器）中物理约束的效果将是后续研究的重点。

Abstract: When a graph neural network estimates a physical quantity, its frame, edge direction, aggregation and feature normalization decide which laws it can break. Wrong choices leave systematic errors the loss never registers. We build a linac beam-dynamics surrogate on this observation. Space charge, short-range wakefields and coherent synchrotron radiation enter as bounded closures on analytic kernels, on graphs whose frame and direction follow the interaction and whose symmetry makes the net space-charge self-force vanish for any weights. Verified without training, with randomized weights, the invariants break by five to eight orders of magnitude once the responsible feature is inverted. Per-working-point models constrained by the rms envelope equation are distilled into one model conditioned on 15 machine settings. On the 18.4~m L0+L1 section of the Shanghai soft x-ray free-electron laser linac, this model predicts the transverse beam envelope at 50 unseen settings to about $7\%$, with the centroid trajectory and emittance, in 0.38~s on a single Quadro RTX 6000 GPU. Training is instead dominated by a normalization choice, a data-derived output range for the beam size: at all 16 working points it lowers the starting error 2.2- to 8.5-fold and fixes the convergence speed. The remaining accuracy differences between hybrid and data-driven architectures vary with the working point and with the density of observation. The held-out error scales roughly as $N^{-0.6}$ with the corpus size $N$. Whether the physics pays off where collective effects are strong, as in a magnetic bunch compressor, is in the next phase of our study.

</details>
