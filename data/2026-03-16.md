### [1] [The N=126 Factory: A New Multi-Nucleon Transfer Reaction Facility](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12359)
*A. A. Valverde,M. S. Martin,W. S. Porter,A. M. Houff,M. Brodeur,J. A. Clark,Y. Cho,A. Jacobs,R. A. Knaack,F. Köhler,K. König,O. S. Kubiniec,A. LaLiberte,B. Liu,B. Maass,A. Mitra,P. Mueller,C. Müller-Gatermann,W. Nörtershäuser,M. B. Oberling,J. Palmes,C. Quick,E. S. C. Ribeiro,J. Rohrer,G. Savard,J. Spahn*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: MNT反应作为生产传统方法难以获得的重、富中子核素的有效手段，计划用于N=126工厂研究中子壳层相关核结构与天体物理过程。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 重、富中子核素对理解r过程A~195丰度峰等天体物理现象至关重要，但传统生产方法效率低下，需要开发新的生产途径

Method: 利用重离子间的多核子转移反应产生目标核素，通过气体捕集器停止反应产物，射频四极杆离子导向器提取，初步磁分离，冷却/聚束，多反射飞行时间质量分离器最终纯化

Result: N=126工厂在ATLAS设施建设中，将基于此方法为实验系统提供准直、聚束的束流（未提供具体数据）

Conclusion: 多核子转移反应结合气体捕集与分离纯化系统能有效生产传统技术难以获得的富中子核素，为核物理与天体物理研究提供新途径

Abstract: Multi-nucleon transfer (MNT) reactions between two heavy ions offer an effective method of producing heavy, neutron-rich nuclei that cannot currently be accessed efficiently using traditional production techniques. These nuclei are important for understanding many astrophysical phenomena, such as the formation of the r-process $A\sim 195$ abundance peak. The N=126 Factory currently commissioning at Argonne National Laboratory's ATLAS facility will make use of these reactions to allow for the study of these nuclei. To convert MNT reaction products, which have a wide angular distribution, into a collimated, bunched beam suitable for experiments, a series of apparatus will be used. These start with a large-volume gas catcher for stopping the reaction products, which are then extracted through a radiofrequency quadrupole ion guide, undergo preliminary dipole magnetic separation, cooling and bunching in a Cooler-Buncher, and final separation using a multi-reflection time-of-flight mass separator, before final delivery to experimental systems.
