### [1] [Physics of stabilization and dynamic control of CW magnetrons for superconducting accelerators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13769)
*G. Kazakevich,R. P. Johnson,I. Gonin,V. Yakovlev,Ya. Derbenev*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 该论文探讨如何使用连续波磁控管为超导加速器提供射频功率的方案优化问题，分析磁控管作为再生自振荡器时的高噪声问题，并研究适用于SRF加速器的相位稳定锁定方案。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 连续波磁控管比传统速调管、IOT或固态放大器具有更高效率和更低射频功率成本，但作为自振荡器时存在显著的相位噪声问题，这可能阻碍其在超导射频加速器中的应用。

Method: 采用分析理论、粒子模拟仿真方法，并结合先前获得的分析和实验数据，研究连续波磁控管的运行和控制特性，开发适用于SRF加速器的相位稳定方案。

Result: 确定了连续波磁控管作为自振荡器时存在显著的再生不稳定性和噪声问题，即使采用注入锁定技术后仍然存在明显噪声，无法满足超导加速器应用要求。

Conclusion: 开发了专门适用于SRF加速器的磁控管控制方法，解决了传统方案的相位噪声问题，为连续波磁控管在超导加速器中的应用提供了可行方案。

Abstract: Powerful CW magnetrons were suggested for feeding RF cavities of superconducting accelerators because they provide higher efficiency and lower RF power cost than the traditionally used klystrons, IOTs, or solid-state amplifiers. RF amplifiers driven by a master oscillator serve as coherent RF sources. CW magnetrons are regenerative RF self-oscillators with a huge regenerative gain. This causes regenerative instability with notable noise when a magnetron operates as a self-oscillator, i.e., with the anode voltage above the threshold of self-excitation. An injection locking by a small signal was proposed for phase stabilization of magnetrons. In this case, CW magnetrons with injection-locked (coherent) oscillations generate a notable level of noise. This may preclude the use of CW magnetrons in this mode in superconducting RF (SRF) accelerators. This work discusses our analytical results, PIC simulations, and previously obtained analytical and experimental data regarding the operation and control of CW magnetrons. These results led to the development of methods which are most suitable for SRF accelerators.

### [2] [Implementation and verification of a new free boundary module for Resistive Wall Modes simulations with the nonlinear 3D full MHD code SpeCyl](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13428)
*Francesco Galeazzi,Daniele Bonfiglio,Luca Spinicci,Susanna Cappello*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 提出并验证了SpeCyl MHD代码的新自由边界条件实现，用于模拟电阻壁模和外扭曲模等边界不稳定性


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自由边界不稳定性（如电阻壁模和外扭曲模）的精确数值模拟对磁约束聚变反应堆研发至关重要，需要能够自洽处理边界条件和三维流动的模拟工具

Method: 在SpeCyl三维全磁流体力学代码中实现了新的边界条件，包括自由流动三维处理和双电阻壁薄壳磁边界条件，使用同轴电阻壳包围等离子体，能够灵活调整壳层位置和时间常数

Result: 通过Shafranov解析理论广泛验证了实现，在(m=2,n=1)电阻壁模和外扭曲模的线性增长率方面获得良好一致性，验证了边界条件的正确性，并与现有RFX-mod模型进行基准比较

Conclusion: 新的边界条件使SpeCyl成为研究大纵横比磁约束配置中自由边界MHD不稳定性（如RFX-mod和RFX-mod2装置）的灵活稳健工具

Abstract: Self-consistent numerical simulations of free boundary instabilities, such as resistive wall modes (RWMs) and external kink modes, require special attention given their relevance on the path towards magnetic fusion based nuclear reactors. In this work, a new implementation and a verification benchmark are presented for the nonlinear three-dimensional (3D) full magneto-hydrodynamics (MHD) code SpeCyl, which self-consistently combines a free flow 3D treatment for the edge plasma velocity with double resistive wall thin shell magnetic boundary conditions, capable of capturing the essential features of common setups in experimental devices. Two coaxial resistive shells surround the plasma. Vacuum conditions are assumed between and outside the resistive shells, whose relative positions and time constants can be varied on purpose.
  The implementation is extensively verified against Shafranov's analytic theory, valid for a circular cross section large aspect ratio tokamak and assuming a flat current profile. The correctness of newly implemented Boundary Conditions (BCs) is confirmed by the good agreement obtained for the linear growth rates of (m=2,n=1) RWM and external kink, over a wide range of wall positions, resistive shell time constants, and for different initial reference equilibria. The (m=2,n=1) RWM growth rates are also benchmarked against previous different RWMs modelling and successful validation studies for RFX-mod tokamak plasmas. The new BCs make SpeCyl a flexible and robust tool to investigate free boundary MHD modes in large aspect ratio magnetic confinement configurations, such as those achieved with the RFX-mod machine and expected in RFX-mod2.
