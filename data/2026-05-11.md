### [1] [Full-gap kinetic limitation of thermionic-electron transport for electron transpiration cooling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07083)
*Wushun Zhang,Weixing Zhou,Yinjian Zhao*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究通过粒子网格/蒙特卡罗碰撞模拟揭示了电子透热冷却中发射电子反向流动限制现象，发现存在弱反向流和反向流限制两个不同传输模式，并确定了临界发射通量阈值。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 电子透热冷却技术能够减轻高超声速前缘的热负荷，但其性能取决于热离子发射电子能否逃离热表面而不会形成阴极反向流。目前对于发射电子传输和反向流限制机制的理解尚不完善，需要建立能够精确解析这些关键物理过程的模型。

Method: 开发了一维空间、三维速度的静电粒子网格/蒙特卡罗碰撞模型，模拟完整的阴极-阳极等离子体二极管。该模型解析了热离子发射、碰撞性等离子体传输、发射电子反向流以及下游收集等过程，并使用氦气基准测试验证了模型的有效性。

Result: 研究发现随着发射通量的增加，二极管首先处于弱反向流状态，净发射电子传输和下游收集效率随发射增加而提高。当发射通量达到7.0×10¹⁹-7.5×10¹⁹ m⁻²·s⁻¹范围时，系统发生急剧转变进入反向流限制状态。在7.25×10¹⁹ m⁻²·s⁻¹时，反向流比例达到54.03%，而净传输和下游收集效率降至约46%。超过这个阈值后，增加的反向流反而会降低有用的发射电子传输。

Conclusion: 研制的PIC-MCC框架成功捕捉了电子透热冷却中关键的动力学过程，包括发射电子的逃逸和反向流限制机制。研究确定了电子透热冷却系统的临界工作状态，为优化冷却性能提供了理论指导。

Abstract: Electron transpiration cooling (ETC) can reduce aerothermal loads on sharp hypersonic leading edges, but its performance is governed by whether thermionically emitted electrons escape the hot surface or return as cathode-directed backflow. Here, a one-dimensional-in-space, three-dimensional-in-velocity electrostatic particle-in-cell/Monte Carlo collision model is developed for a full cathode--anode plasma diode, resolving thermionic emission, collisional plasma transport, emitted-electron backflow, and downstream collection. A helium benchmark is used to examine emitted-electron transport and backflow-limited current flow. With increasing imposed emission, the diode first remains in a weak-backflow regime, where net emitted-electron transport and downstream collection both increase with emission. Further increasing the emission produces a sharp transition to backflow-limited transport between $7.0\times10^{19}$ and $7.5\times10^{19},\mathrm{m^{-2},s^{-1}}$. At $7.25\times10^{19},\mathrm{m^{-2},s^{-1}}$, the backflow ratio reaches $54.03%$, while the net transport and downstream collection efficiencies fall to about $46%$. Above this transition, added backflow overcompensates the imposed emission increase, reducing useful emitted-electron transport rather than causing saturation. Boundary energy diagnostics show that stronger emission may still increase the nominal cathode-side cooling metric, but after transition this metric no longer indicates improved emitted-electron escape or full-gap transport. These results show that the present PIC-MCC framework captures the key kinetic processes governing ETC-relevant emitted-electron escape and backflow limitation.

### [2] [Warm Topological Langmuir Cyclotron Wave](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07845)
*Virginia Billings,Hong Qin,Chuang Ren,J. B. Marston*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 本文发现磁化电子等离子体中的有限温度效应会在暖朗缪尔波和右旋偏振波之间产生Weyl点简并，该点的拓扑电荷为1，根据指标定理预测存在穿越能隙的拓扑边缘模。通过数值求解1D非均匀平衡下的暖流体本征模问题，识别出该模式为暖拓扑朗缪尔-回旋波，该模式在冷极限下不存在，且参数范围与UCLA的LAPD装置相关。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究磁化电子等离子体中有限温度效应如何影响波动模式的拓扑特性，特别是探索温度诱导的Weyl简并点及其相关的拓扑边缘态在实验室等离子体中的可观测性。

Method: 采用暖流体模型分析磁化电子等离子体，通过数值求解一维非均匀平衡下的本征模问题，计算Weyl点的拓扑电荷并验证指标定理预测的拓扑边缘模。

Result: 发现在暖等离子体中朗缪尔波和右旋偏振波之间存在温度诱导的Weyl点简并，拓扑电荷为1，数值模拟确认了预测的拓扑边缘模——暖拓扑朗缪尔-回旋波，该模式在UCLA LAPD装置的参数范围内可实现。

Conclusion: 有限温度效应在磁化电子等离子体中引入了新的拓扑相，产生了在冷极限下不存在的拓扑边缘模，为实验室等离子体中的拓扑波物理研究提供了新平台。

Abstract: Finite-temperature effects in magnetized electron plasmas create a new Weyl-point degeneracy between the warm Langmuir and right-circularly polarized waves. The associated topological charge at this warm Weyl point is found to be 1, which, by the index theorem, predicts a gap-traversing topological edge mode. Solving the full warm-fluid eigenmode problem In a 1D inhomogeneous equilibrium, we numerically identify this anticipated mode as the warm topological Langmuir-cyclotron wave, which is absent in the cold limit and occurs in a parameter regime relevant to the LArge Plasma Device (LAPD) at UCLA.

</details>

### [3] [Coherent γ-ray Generation By Partially Stripped Ion Beams](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07871)
*Nicola Piovella,Gordon R. M Robb*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种通过逆向散射强可见激光从部分剥离离子束中产生相干γ射线的方案，类似于CERN的Gamma Factory，但通过集体不稳定性实现相干散射。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有γ射线源的可调谐性和相干性有限，需要开发新型相干γ射线源以拓展应用范围。逆向散射方案虽有潜力但需解决散射过程的相干性问题。

Method: 利用高强度可见激光与部分剥离离子束的逆向散射，通过类似高增益自由电子激光中的集体不稳定性机制使离子微束团化，从而实现相干散射产生γ射线。

Result: 发现实现相干γ射线产生所需的参数要求比Gamma Factory方案更为严格，需要显著提高泵浦激光强度和可能的离子束电流。

Conclusion: 该方案为产生可调谐相干γ射线提供了潜在途径，但需要克服技术挑战以提高激光强度和离子束电流。

Abstract: We describe a scheme for generation of coherent $γ$-rays by backscattering intense visible laser light from a beam of partially stripped ions. The scheme is similar in principle to the proposed Gamma Factory at CERN, with the important difference that the scattering becomes coherent as a result of a collective instability which microbunches the ions. This instability is analogous to that which occurs in high-gain free electron lasers (FELs). The scheme potentially offers a route to a source of tuneable, coherent $γ$-rays, opening up a wide range of possible new applications and opportunities. We find that the parameter requirements for realization of coherent $γ$-ray generation regime are considerably more stringent than those proposed for the Gamma Factory, requiring significant increases in the pump laser intensity and possibly the ion beam current.

</details>


### [4] [An electron injector for the Electron-Ion Collider based on proton-driven plasma wakefield acceleration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07929)
*J. P. Farmer,H. Jaworska,A. Caldwell,N. Lopes,A. Pukhov,L Reichwein,F. Willeke,M. Wing*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 该研究提出了一种基于质子驱动等离子体尾场加速的电子束注入方案，用于电子-离子对撞机。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为电子-离子对撞机开发高效的电子束注入方案，利用现有RHIC的蓝环质子束作为驱动源，旨在实现高极化度和高亮度的电子束。

Method: 采用质子驱动等离子体尾场加速技术，从现有RHIC蓝环获取质子束，同时利用当前EIC设计的极化电子源。

Result: 初步研究表明，在质子驱动等离子体尾场中加速电子束可以接近EIC的设计参数，平均极化度约70%，亮度达1e34 cm⁻²s⁻¹。

Conclusion: 该注入方案有望实现电子-离子对撞机所需的高性能参数，验证了质子驱动等离子体尾场加速技术在EIC应用中的可行性。

Abstract: We describe an electron bunch injector scheme based on proton-driven plasma wakefield acceleration for the Electron-Ion Collider. The proton bunches needed to drive the plasma wake are taken from the existing Blue-Ring of RHIC. The polarized electron source is taken from the current EIC design. We describe the different elements making up the injection scheme and give an estimate for the performance. Our initial study indicates that the design parameters of the EIC are within reach when accelerating the electron bunches in the proton-driven plasma wake, with average polarization of ~70% and a luminosity of 1e34 cm$^{-2}$s$^{-1}$.

### [5] [Multilayer model for coatings with arbitrary layers for superconducting radio-frequency applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07958)
*Aaron Gobeyn,Wolfgang Ackermann,Herbert De Gersem*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 本文扩展了多层模型，能够处理任意序列的超导、正常导电和绝缘层，并考虑了所有损耗和介电效应贡献，发现n=1是最优结构配置。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为了精确模拟超导-绝缘体-超导（SIS）结构和更广泛的多层异质结构中的电磁行为，需要考虑所有损耗机制，并建立能够处理任意材料序列的通用模型。

Method: 扩展Kubo等人的多层模型，使其适用于任意序列的超导、正常导电和绝缘层，同时考虑全部损耗贡献（包括欧姆损耗和介电效应）。使用Leontovich边界条件计算表面阻抗，并通过Poynting定理确定各层损耗贡献。

Result: 发现(SI)^nS结构的最优配置是n=1（单层绝缘间隔）。超导涂层的厚度可减少到低于其穿透深度而性能损失很小。过渡层厚度增加会降低最大适用场强并增大有效电磁场穿透深度。

Conclusion: 建立的通用模型能够准确模拟复杂多层超导结构的电磁特性，为超导器件设计提供了计算工具，特别是在有限元仿真中的整合应用具有重要意义。

Abstract: We extend the multilayer model of \etal{Kubo} for superconductor-insulator-superconductor (SIS) structures in two ways:
  first, by generalizing it to arbitrary sequences of layers of arbitrary type, i.e. superconducting, normal conducting, and
  insulating; and second, by accounting for all contributions, including ohmic losses and dielectric effects. We examine the maximum
  applicable field for $(\text{SI})^n\text{S}$ structures. We find that the optimum configuration corresponds to the
  $n=1$ case. However, the thickness of the superconducting coating layers can be reduced to below their penetration depth
  with minor performance penalty. We discuss the ability to model transitions in SS bilayers by introducing a set of virtual layers
  that represent the transition region through interpolated parameters. We find degradation of the maximum applicable field
  with thicker transition layers, and a larger effective penetration depth of the electromagnetic fields. Furthermore,
  the surface impedance of the multilayer structure is calculated using the Leontovich boundary condition, yielding a formulation
  suitable for integration into finite element simulations. Additionally, the Poynting theorem is used to determine the loss
  contributions of individual layers.

</details>
