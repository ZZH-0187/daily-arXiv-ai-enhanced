### [1] [Closing the Loop: Deploying Auto-Generating Digital Twins for Particle Accelerators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19101)
*A. D. Brynes,M. King,K. R. L. Baker,R. Banerjee,R. Clarke,D. J. Dunning,J. K. Jones,M. Leputa,A. E. Pollard,M. Romanovschi,M. Shaw,N. Ziyan*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 研究团队为粒子加速器开发了一种可自动生成的数字孪生架构，通过虚拟控制系统镜像物理硬件，并利用仿真模型更新虚拟诊断，最终建立了一个模块化、可扩展的数字孪生架构。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 数字孪生作为一种非侵入式的物理系统虚拟复现技术，能实现预测性维护与监测，同时在不干扰实际系统的情况下探索新配置。粒子加速器是一种高度复杂的物理系统，需要建立高效、统一且可扩展的数字孪生架构以支持多机构协同研究。

Method: 研究团队设计了一种基于‘单一真实数据源’的自动化数字孪生架构，具体包括：从顶层系统统一传递加速器晶格信息至虚拟控制系统，避免仿真模型与虚拟硬件之间的参数命名歧义；采用模块化和可扩展架构，使不同研究机构能够整合各自的模型（如机器学习模型）与加速器晶格，同时保持数字孪生的结构一致性。

Result: 该架构已在三个粒子加速器设施（CLARA、ISIS注入器和英国拟建的XFEL）中进行了测试，实现了数字孪生系统的有效部署和运行，并为多机构协作共享技术的通用数字孪生解决方案提供了基础框架。

Conclusion: 本文提出的自动生成数字孪生架构为粒子加速器提供了一种可扩展、模块化的解决方案，能够有效消除模型与硬件间的不一致性，促进多机构研究合作，并为未来通用数字孪生技术的发展奠定了基础。

Abstract: The simulation of a physical system in a virtual replica, known as a digital twin, is a useful way to interrogate the system non-invasively, providing the ability to perform predictive maintenance and surveillance, and to investigate potential novel configurations without perturbing the system. This article presents the implementation of an auto-generating digital twin architecture for particle accelerators: a virtual control system is generated to mirror the physical accelerator hardware, and used to update a simulation model which then feeds back the results into virtual diagnostics. All of the information about the accelerator lattice is cascaded down from a ground source of truth, removing any ambiguity about the naming of parameters between the simulation model and the virtual hardware. This design is modular and extensible, allowing researchers from different institutions to use their own models (for example, a machine learning model) and accelerator lattices while maintaining the overall structural coherence of the digital twin. This architecture has been tested for three accelerator facilities \textendash~CLARA, the ISIS injector, and the proposed UK XFEL \textendash~and aims to provide the foundation for a collaborative community effort in the development of shared technology towards a generic digital twin solution.

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 开发了一个全面的粒子加速器晶格描述模式，包含物理元素信息、模拟代码参数、控制系统变量等多种数据，同时提供翻译层以支持多种模拟代码。

A translation layer is also provided in order to export this lattice into formats suitable for a variety of standard accelerator simulation codes. Based on this format, a framework has been developed for generating, tracking and analyzing beams through the lattice, providing a seamless transfer between simulation codes and the basis for a fully generic start-to-end simulation framework.

### [3] [System Size Dependence of Collisionless Reconnection Rate](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18787)
*Yi-Min Huang,Naoki Bessho,Li-Jen Chen,Judith T. Karpen,Amitava Bhattacharjee*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 传统观点认为无碰撞磁重联以约0.1的普遍快速速率进行，与宏观系统尺寸无关。本文通过严格的比例研究证明，当初始电流片厚度按系统尺寸成比例缩放时，这种普遍快速速率消失，重联速率随系统尺寸增大而减小。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有研究存在矛盾：一方面认为无碰撞磁重联以约0.1的普遍快速速率进行，与宏观系统尺寸无关；另一方面在强迫重联和磁岛合并中却发现速率显著依赖于系统尺寸。本文旨在弥合这种差异，探究宏观尺度依赖性是否是无碰撞磁重联的基本性质。

Method: 使用粒子网格法和霍尔磁流体动力学模拟进行严格的比例研究，通过按系统尺寸成比例缩放初始电流片厚度来保持一致的系统范围磁位形。

Result: 当初始电流片厚度随系统尺寸成比例缩放时，普遍快速速率消失，重联速率随系统尺寸增大而减小。表明宏观尺度依赖性并非特定几何形状的独特现象，而是无碰撞磁重联的基本性质。

Conclusion: 无碰撞磁重联的速率本质上依赖于宏观系统尺寸，这统一了Harris电流片与其他显示尺寸依赖性的构型，颠覆了普遍快速速率独立于宏观尺度的传统范式。

Abstract: It is a widely accepted paradigm that collisionless magnetic reconnection proceeds at a universal fast rate of $\sim0.1$ when normalized to a properly defined reconnecting magnetic field and Alfvén speed, effectively independent of the macroscopic system size. This conclusion, derived primarily from kinetic simulations of classical Harris current sheets with kinetic-scale thickness, stands in contrast to results from forced reconnection and island coalescence, where the rate significantly depends on the system size. Here, we reconcile this disparity by performing a rigorous scaling study using both particle-in-cell and Hall magnetohydrodynamic simulations. We demonstrate that when the global magnetic configuration is self-consistently preserved by scaling the initial current sheet thickness proportionally with the system size, the ``universal'' fast rate disappears. Instead, the reconnection rate decreases as the system size increases. These results indicate that dependence on macroscopic scales is not peculiar to specific geometries but is a fundamental property of collisionless reconnection, effectively unifying the Harris sheet with other configurations exhibiting size-dependence.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

These results establish a new pathway toward next-generation intense terahertz sources with performance well beyond the limits of existing plasma mechanisms and conventional crystal-based approaches.
