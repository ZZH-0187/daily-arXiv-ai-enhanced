### [1] [On magnetic-field consistency in the sideways force calculation of Phys. Plasmas 27, 012508 (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20644)
*Denis Mironov*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该论文指出之前两篇论文中用于计算壁面侧向力的平衡磁场存在不一致性问题，包括散度自由和旋度自由条件在两种坐标系下分别被违反，导致侧向力的解析推导不可靠。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 检验 Martynov 和 Medvedev (2020) 提出的用于侧向壁力计算的平衡磁场 B₀ 的自洽性，因为他们的分析中包含没有明确定义微分算符的环向修正。

Method: 使用环向坐标系和准柱坐标系两种表述测试 B₀ 是否符合麦克斯韦方程，验证其在真空区域的散度为零和旋度为零条件。

Result: 在环向坐标系下，磁场不满足散度为零条件；在准柱坐标系下，磁场不满足旋度为零条件。后续的重新表述也无法定义出一个包含环向修正的自洽平衡磁场。

Conclusion: 由于侧向力的计算建立在不一致的磁场描述上，这些分析推导存在问题，不能作为修订之前 Mironov 和 Pustovitov (2017) 以及单谐波分析结果的有效依据。

Abstract: We examine the consistency of the equilibrium magnetic field $\mathbf{B}_0$ used in the analytical calculation of the sideways wall force by A. A. Martynov and S. Yu. Medvedev [Phys. Plasmas 27, 012508 (2020)] and the subsequent reformulation presented in [Phys. Plasmas 27, 054702 (2020)]. In these attempts, the current density $\mathbf{j}_0 = \nabla \times \mathbf{B}_0$ must vanish in the vacuum regions adjacent to the wall, making $\mathbf{B}_0$ curl-free as a complement to the natural $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B}_0 = 0$. Since the cited works incorporate selected toroidal corrections without explicitly specifying the corresponding differential operators, we test $\mathbf{B}_0$ adopted in these models against Maxwell's equations using both toroidal and quasi-cylindrical formulations. We show that the divergence-free requirement is violated in the toroidal interpretation, while the curl-free condition is not satisfied in the quasi-cylindrical limit. The reformulation likewise fails to define a self-consistent equilibrium field with toroidal effects because the accompanying field corrections required for consistency with Maxwell's equations are not specified. Since the sideways force calculations are built upon these inconsistent field prescriptions, their analytical derivations are consequently compromised. The resulting arguments therefore cannot serve as a valid basis for revising either the earlier results established by D. V. Mironov and V. D. Pustovitov [Phys. Plasmas 24, 092508 (2017)] or those obtained within the single-harmonic analysis [Phys. Plasmas 22, 052502 (2015)].
