### [1] [Experimental observation of drift acoustic cnoidal waves in a magnetized plasma](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19927)
*Tanmay Karmakar,Rosh Roy,Lavkesh Lachhvani,Raju Daniel,Bhoomi Khodiyar,Prabal K. Chattopadhyay,Abhijit Sen,Sayak Bose*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 实验中首次观测到碰撞磁化等离子体中的Cnoidal波列，这种非线性相干结构是强密度梯度和速度剪切下漂移声波的非线性演化形式。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究不均匀、剪切、高碰撞磁化等离子体中漂移声波的非线性演化机制，特别是非线性波列的形成和饱和过程。

Method: 在线性磁化等离子体中进行实验，系统改变剖面梯度，观测高碰撞、强密度梯度和显著ExB速度剪切条件下产生的非线性相干结构。

Result: 观测到大振幅（达10%）的漂移声波，呈现出周期性的锯齿状波形，这些波形可用Cnoidal函数精确描述，对应KdV型方程的稳态非线性波列解。

Conclusion: 实现了对碰撞磁化等离子体中Cnoidal波列的首次可控实验观测，为理解漂移声波在这种复杂等离子体环境中的非线性演化和饱和机制提供了新见解。

Abstract: We report the experimental observation of highly nonlinear coherent structures in a linear magnettized plasma characterized by a strong background density gradient and significant ExB velocity shear under high ion-neutral collisionality. These structures, identified as drift acoustic waves, exhibit large normalized density fluctuations reaching amplitudes of up to ~10% and show periodic sawtooth-like waveforms. These observed waveforms are well described by cnoidal functions, corresponding to stationary nonlinear wave trains. Cnoidal waves are exact solutions of the Korteweg-de Vries (KdV)-type equations, alongside the more commonly studied soliton solutions. To the best of our knowledge, this work presents the first controlled experimental observation of cnoidal wave trains in a highly collisional magnetized plasma through systematic variation of profile gradients. These findings provide important new insights into the nonlinear evolution and saturation of drift acoustic waves in inhomogeneous, sheared, and collisional magnetized plasmas.

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 摘要介绍了一项研究，揭示了带状流和湍流扩散在磁约束聚变等离子体中的耦合机制，发现湍流扩散能将带状流径向输运到线性稳定区域，并从动量定理理论角度阐明了湍流驱动熵运输与垂直动量生成之间的直接关系。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 带状流和湍流扩散对磁约束聚变等离子体的约束性能至关重要，但两者之间的耦合机制一直不甚明确。这项研究旨在阐明带状流与湍流扩散的相互耦合作用机理。

Method: 利用全局非线性回旋动理学模拟方法，分析带状流在湍流局域非线性饱和后的径向输运行为，并结合托卡马克等离子体中的动量定理（Charney-Drazin无加速定理的扩展）进行理论分析。

Result: 模拟表明，湍流扩散能够将带状流径向输运到线性稳定区域。理论分析揭示，湍流驱动的熵运输与垂直动量生成之间存在直接联系。

Conclusion: 该研究成功阐明了带状流与湍流扩散之间的耦合机制，发现湍流扩散能有效运输带状流至稳定区域，并通过动量定理建立了湍流驱动输运与动量生成的定量关系，对理解等离子体约束机制有重要意义。

Abstract: Zonal flows and turbulence spreading play important roles in magnetic fusion plasma confinement, yet their coupling mechanisms remain elusive. Using global nonlinear gyrokinetic simulations, we show that turbulence spreading transports zonal flow radially, extending into the linearly stable regions after local nonlinear saturation of turbulence. Theoretical understanding has been gained by analyzing the simulation results in the context of a momentum theorem in toroidal plasmas [T.S. Hahm \textit{et al.}, Phys. Plasmas \textbf{31}, 032310 (2024)] which is an extension of the Charney-Drazin non-acceleration theorem [J.G. Charney and P.G. Drazin, J. Geophys. Res. \textbf{66}, 83 (1961)]. It indicates a direct relation between turbulence-driven enstrophy transport and perpendicular momentum generation.

Particular focus is paid to the performance of the QCE in terms of the achieved pedestal top values, which, when appropriately normalised, do not differ significantly from ELMy H-mode plasmas. This, combined with the predicted minimum separatrix density for the 15 MA ITER baseline plasma, highlight the relevance of the QCE as a potential operational scenario for both ITER and future reactors.

### [4] [Attosecond Nonlinear Quantum Electrodynamics in Laser-Driven Plasmas via Two-Photon Synchrotron Emission](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20672)
*Vedin Dewan,Aleksei M. Zheltikov,Julia M. Mikhailova*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 研究提出了一种在强激光等离子体相互作用中实现相对论性非线性量子电动力学实验的框架，无需外部相对论粒子束，可产生阿秒尺度的相关光子对源


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有非线性QED实验通常需要外部相对论粒子束，而本研究旨在探索利用强激光等离子体相互作用本身产生高能电子，从而在更简单的实验设置下研究QED效应

Method: 利用超强飞秒激光脉冲在等离子体中产生并加速稠密电子束至相对论能量，电子在横向洛伦兹力作用下产生曲率辐射，导致受限至纳米空间阿秒时间尺度的光子对发射

Result: 理论分析表明最低阶非线性QED过程会产生阿秒尺度的双光子发射，可用作超宽带相关光子对源。双光子发射率约为α²γω_turn，最强相关性的光子对发射率约为α²γ²ω_turnE_⊥/E_S，为光子纠缠提供了资源

Conclusion: 该研究为在复杂激光等离子体相互作用中分离量子电动力学效应与经典对应物提供了明确指导，建立了物理上合理的非线性QED现象分析方法

Abstract: Ultrafast strong-field laser--plasma physics is shown to offer a promising framework for relativistic nonlinear quantum electrodynamics (QED). As one of its key advantages, this approach to relativistic nonlinear QED does not require an external beam of relativistic particles. Instead, high-energy electrons are produced in this setting as a part of ultrafast strong-field laser--plasma interactions. An intense ultrashort laser pulse generates and accelerates dense electron bunches to relativistic energies, giving rise to photon-pair emission confined to the nanometer scale in space and the attosecond scale in time. As a lowest-order nonlinear QED process, relativistic electrons in laser-driven plasmas are shown to give rise to attosecond bursts of two-photon emission, providing an ultrabroadband source of correlated photon pairs. As a physically insightful estimate, the rate of this two-photon emission is expressed via a product $ α^2 γω_{turn}$, where $α$ is the fine-structure constant, $γ$ is the Lorentz factor, and $ ω_{turn}$ is the local relativistic curvature frequency. Photon pairs with strongest correlations, providing a resource for photon entanglement, are emitted at a much lower rate, estimated as $ α^2 γ^2 ω_{turn} E_{\perp} /E_S$, where $E_{\perp}$ is the laser electromagnetic field, determining the transverse Lorentz force, and $E_S$ is the Schwinger critical field. Our study offers a clear guidance on how quantum aspects of laser-driven relativistic plasma electrodynamics can be isolated from their classical counterparts, enabling a physically justifiable approach to the analysis of nonlinear QED phenomena in complex laser--plasma interactions driven by ultrashort high-intensity laser pulses.

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 使用磁场的数学框架，推导出表面电流分布的解析公式，该公式能同时满足磁场匹配条件和电流复杂度调整需求，并讨论了数值近似实现的关键技术细节。

Further, our formula allows to adjust the toroidal complexity of the current without changing the magnetic field. Some discussions regarding aspects of numerical approximations are also included.
