### [1] [Brilliant multi-GeV Compton gamma-ray source seeded by a photon accelerator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02373)
*Michael J. Quin,Stepan S. Bulanov,Arkady Gonoskov,Christopher D. Murphy,Mattias Marklund,Alexander G. R. Thomas,Thomas G. Blackburn*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 提出了一种通过等离子体场加速激光光子并反射到电子束上的方案，实现了高亮度、高偏振度的多GeV伽马射线产生，为相关物理研究提供了新光源。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的加速器设施和光学激光器无法通过逆康普顿散射从未极化电子中产生足够高质心能量的偏振、多GeV伽马射线。然而，高亮度偏振伽马射线源对于在亚原子尺度上的物质泵浦和探测至关重要。

Method: 提出了一种创新方案：首先通过束驱动等离子体尾场将光学激光光子"加速"到极紫外波段，然后利用等离子体镜反射回到尾随的电子束上，通过逆康普顿散射产生伽马射线。

Result: 数值模拟表明，该光源可实现高峰值亮度（10^25 光子/s mm^2 mrad^2 0.1%带宽）和高程度的圆偏振（95%）或线偏振（77%），在多GeV光子能量范围内具有优良性能。

Conclusion: 该方案通过等离子体场加速-反射机制，将传统无法达到的逆康普顿散射能量范围提升至多GeV级别，成功实现了高亮度偏振伽马射线的产生。这为自旋极化正电子产生和光子-光子散射研究开辟了新的实验途径。

Abstract: High-brilliance sources of polarized gamma rays are widely sought after to pump and probe matter at subatomic length scales. However, existing accelerator facilities and optical lasers cannot reach a sufficiently high center-of-mass energy to produce polarized, multi-GeV gamma rays from unpolarized electrons via inverse Compton scattering. Here we propose a scheme where the optical laser photons are first "accelerated" to the extreme ultraviolet in a beam-driven plasma wakefield, then reflected by a plasma mirror back onto a trailing electron beam, producing a flash of gamma rays. Numerical simulations demonstrate this light source can achieve a high peak-brilliance (10^25 photons/s mm^2 mrad^2 0.1% BW) and a high degree of circular (95 %) or linear (77 %) polarization at multi-GeV photon energies, paving the way for the production of spin-polarized positrons and tests of light-by-light scattering.
