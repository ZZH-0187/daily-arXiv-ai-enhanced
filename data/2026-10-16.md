### [1] [Aluminum wide-range EOS sensitivity in Z-pinch dynamic-hohlraum liner-foam impact and outgoing radiation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21107)
*Cong Wang,Zi Li,Weijie Li,Xianjue Peng*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 论文通过DFT方法构建了宽范围铝状态方程表(DFT-eos)，与QEOS对比，研究Z-pinch动态黑腔中铝衬套方程状态对能量转换和辐射特性的影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在Z-pinch动态黑腔中，铝衬套与中心泡沫碰撞将动能转化为辐射，这一过程对铝的状态方程(EOS)具有依赖性。传统的日常状态方程(QEOS)可能无法准确描述铝在固-液-等离子体区域的物理特性，需要更精确的基于第一性原理的状态方程。

Method: 采用密度泛函理论(DFT)模拟构建宽范围铝状态方程表(DFT-eos)：1)混合确定性-随机性有限温度DFT(mDFT)计算电子热贡献；2)量子分子动力学(QMD)与经典标定结合得到离子部分。使用一维柱状辐射磁流体动力学(MHD)模拟，在峰值电流50MA、上升时间220-300ns条件下，对单层轻泡沫负载进行模拟，比较DFT-eos和QEOS对物理过程的影响。

Result: 1)冲击时间和峰值衬套动能在两种状态方程下变化很小；2)在飞行段密度、碰撞时刻的接触状态、以及外部界面出射辐射功率的时间历程上出现较大差异；3)DFT-eos提供了更准确的状态描述，尤其在固-液-等离子体相变区域。

Conclusion: 铝状态方程的精确性对Z-pinch动态黑腔的辐射特性和能量转换过程具有重要影响，基于DFT的状态方程相比传统QEOS在描述碰撞状态和辐射历程方面存在显著差异，为高能量密度物理实验提供了更可信的模拟工具。

Abstract: In a Z-pinch dynamic hohlraum, an aluminum liner collides with a central foam and converts kinetic energy into radiation, and the liner-foam response depends on the aluminum equation of state (EOS). We construct a wide-range aluminum equation-of-state table from density functional theory (DFT) simulations over solid-liquid-plasma regions, denoted DFT-eos. Mixed deterministic-stochastic finite-temperature DFT (mDFT) gives the electron thermal contribution; quantum molecular dynamics (QMD) with classical calibration gives the ion part. We perform one-dimensional cylindrical radiation magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) simulations at a peak current of 50 MA and rise times of 220-300 ns on a single-layer light foam load. Opacity and electrical conductivity are fixed; only the liner Al EOS is changed between DFT-eos and the quotidian EOS (QEOS). Impact time and peak liner kinetic energy change little between the two tables. Larger differences appear in flight-segment density, the contact state at impact, and the time history of outgoing radiation power at the outer interface.

### [2] [Quantitative validation of SMBI deposition profiles on EAST using experimentally constrained simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21546)
*Yangdi Yu,Tianyang Xia,Jie Zhang,Jia Huang,Huimin Qi,Dazheng Li,Yanlong Li,Xiaoxue He,Xiaowei Mao,Hang Yu,Shuang Zheng,Haoyu Nan,Junyi Yang,Kaining Lin,the EAST team*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究通过实验提取SMBI脉冲的粒子增量来校准模拟源，解决了SMBI燃料注入模拟与实验长期存在的有效源约束不足问题。对于EAST装置第71438次放电，分析了九个SMBI脉冲组的密度演化，分离出SMBI引起的密度增加，并确定其在中间pedestal区域达到峰值。通过体积积分获得实验参考值，并开发了多流体模型和改进的输运算子进行模拟。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: SMBI（超音速分子束注入）燃料注入模拟与实验之间的定量比较长期以来受到有效源约束不足的阻碍。传统方法难以准确确定SMBI注入的实际粒子源强，导致模拟结果与实验数据难以有效比较。

Method: 1. 实验方面：分析EAST装置第71438次放电中九个SMBI脉冲组的微波反射计密度演化数据；通过比较每个脉冲组前后的上升和下降阶段，分离SMBI引起的密度增加；提取径向分布并确定峰值位置；通过体积积分获得总粒子增量作为实验参考。
2. 模拟方面：在BOUT++框架中使用多流体模型；改进两个输运算子为守恒通量形式，将粒子平衡残差降至注入量的约2%；构造垂直输运系数基于离散径向通量平衡，将初始密度和温度分布在无源演化中的非物理变化降低一个数量级；使用校准后的源进行模拟。

Result: 1. 实验发现SMBI引起的密度增量在每次脉冲组的中间pedestal区域达到峰值。
2. 模拟的沉积峰值位置与实验观测在相对pedestal坐标下一致。
3. 模拟中注入结束时的粒子转换率略低于实验推断值，但脉冲结束时的保留分数较高。
4. 模拟中的扰动衰减较慢，归因于模型中未包含的快速损失通道（如平行损失到刮削层）。
5. 边缘电子温度在整个脉冲期间保持在解离和电离速率系数的高温平台区域，未观察到早期报道的边缘冷却导致的沉积向内偏移现象。

Conclusion: 通过实验提取SMBI粒子增量并校准模拟源，实现了SMBI燃料注入模拟与实验的改进定量比较。模拟与实验在沉积峰值位置方面一致，但存在差异表明需要进一步考虑快速损失通道的建模。该方法为解决长期存在的SMBI模拟源约束问题提供了有效途径。

Abstract: Quantitative comparison between SMBI fueling simulations and experiments has long been hampered by the poor constraint on the effective source. Here, the particle increment per pulse is extracted experimentally and used to calibrate the simulation source. For EAST shot 71438, the density evolution from microwave reflectometry is analyzed for nine SMBI pulse groups. The SMBI-induced density increase is isolated from the background by comparing the rising and falling phases around each pulse group. Its radial profile peaks in the mid-pedestal region for every pulse group. Volume integration of this increment provides the experimental reference. The simulations use a multi-fluid model in the BOUT++ framework in which two transport operators are recast into conservative flux form, with the particle balance residual reduced to about 2% of the injected amount, and the perpendicular transport coefficients are constructed from the discrete radial flux balance, with the unphysical changes of the initial density and temperature profiles in source-free evolution reduced by an order of magnitude. For three cases sharing the same nominal molecular flux, the simulated deposition peak agrees with the experimental one in the relative pedestal coordinate. The simulated conversion of the injected particles at the end of the injection is slightly lower than the value inferred from the experiment, while the fraction retained at the end of the pulse is higher. The perturbation thus decays more slowly in the simulation, which we attribute to fast loss channels not represented in the model, such as parallel losses to the scrape-off layer. The edge electron temperature stays on the high-temperature plateau of the dissociation and ionization rate coefficients throughout the pulse, and the inward shift of the deposition with edge cooling reported earlier does not occur under the present conditions.
