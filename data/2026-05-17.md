### [1] [Boris and Exponential Integrators in the Theory of Particles Interacting with Magnetic Turbulence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14065)
*Andreas Shalchi*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 本文讨论了带电粒子在磁场中运动的数值模拟方法，比较了指数积分器、Rodrigues方案和著名的Boris积分器，发现Rodrigues方案在理论上更精确，实际应用中两者结果相似，且计算时间相当。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 带电粒子与磁场的相互作用是物理学中的基本问题，如高能粒子在磁化等离子体中的运动。对此类问题进行理论探索的最准确可靠方法是测试粒子模拟，其中需要构建湍流磁场并通过积分方案数值求解牛顿-洛伦兹方程。为了更好地理解和比较不同的数值积分方法，本文系统推导并比较了几种常用方案。

Method: 本文首先讨论指数积分器，然后系统推导了Rodrigues方案以及著名的Boris积分器。研究采用在每个时间步重新创建磁场的方法，比较了两种积分器的性能。Rodrigues方案在理论上的优势在于它无需进一步近似即可计算矩阵指数。

Result: 研究发现，对于在每个时间步重新创建磁场的模拟方法，Rodrigues方案和Boris积分器的整体表现相当。理论上，由于直接计算矩阵指数而无近似，Rodrigues方案应该更精确；但在实际应用中，两种方法提供了非常相似的结果。同时，对于本文讨论的特定问题，Rodrigues方案并未要求更长的计算时间。

Conclusion: 本文认为基于Rodrigues的积分器是一个非常有竞争力的替代方案，因为在所讨论的特定问题中，它不增加计算负担，且理论上具有更高的精度，尽管在实践中与Boris积分器结果相似。

Abstract: The interaction of electrically charged particles with magnetic fields is a fundamental problem in several areas of physics. An example is the motion of energetic particles through a magnetized plasma. The most accurate and reliable way to explore theoretically the interactions between particles and fields is via test-particle simulations. In such simulations one creates the turbulent magnetic field and solves the Newton-Lorentz equation numerically by employing an integration scheme. In the current article we discuss exponential integrators and derive systematically from this the Rodrigues scheme as well as the famous Boris integrator. For an approach where one creates the magnetic field anew at each time step, both integrators are overall comparable. In theory the Rodrigues approach should be more accurate due to the fact that the occurring matrix exponential is evaluated without further approximations. Practically, both methods provide very similar results. It is argued in the current article that a Rodrigues based integrator is a very strong alternative because for the specific problem discussed here, it does not require longer computing times.

### [2] [Few-Attosecond electron pulse trains with tunable periods produced by two counter-propagating lasers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14873)
*Qi Huang,Qing Jia,Zhongxuan Wang,Jian Zheng*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 提出和演示了一种利用双逆传播激光的紧凑方案，通过特定参数共振条件，将电子束稳定调制为高度周期性的阿秒脉冲序列，实现约1阿秒脉冲，洛伦兹因子达15，相对能散低于0.02%，相比传统方法将焦深延长三个数量级。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统调制方案在追求亚阿秒持续时间时会急剧缩短纵向焦深，这限制了阿秒电子脉冲在超快物质动力学实时探测中的应用。为了解决这一瓶颈，需要一种能克服焦深障碍的新型调制方案。

Method: 采用两个逆传播激光的紧凑方案，发现了先前未识别的稳定调制机制。通过特定的参数共振条件，使电子束能够在双激光场中被稳定调制，形成高度周期性的阿秒脉冲序列，同时获得快速能量增益。使用亚相对论电子束进行了仿真验证。

Result: 仿真证实能够产生约1阿秒脉冲，洛伦兹因子高达15，相对能散低于0.02%。与常规方法相比，该方案将焦深延长了三个数量级。同时揭示了从有序调制到随机加速的关键转变阈值

Conclusion: 这项工作不仅提供了一种可行的方案来克服阿秒电子脉冲应用中的焦深障碍，还揭示了电子束与双激光场相互作用中从有序调制到随机加速的临界转变机制，为阿秒电子脉冲技术发展开辟了新路径。

Abstract: Attosecond electron pulses enable real-time probing of ultrafast matter dynamics, yet conventional modulation schemes suffer from drastically shortened longitudinal focal lengths when targeting sub-attosecond durations. To address this bottleneck, we propose and demonstrate a compact scheme utilizing two counter-propagating lasers that reveals a previously unidentified stable modulation regime. Contrary to established models of ponderomotive forces and stochastic acceleration in dual-laser fields, we show that a specific parametric resonance condition permits the electron beam to be stably modulated into highly periodic attosecond trains with rapid energy gain. Using a sub-relativistic electron beam, simulations confirm the generation of ~1 as pulses with a Lorentz factor up to 15 and a relative energy spread below 0.02%, extending the focal length by three orders of magnitude compared with conventional approaches. This work identifies the critical transition from ordered modulation to stochastic acceleration, offering a viable route to overcoming the focal-length barrier in attosecond electron-pulse applications.

### [3] [Numerical simulations of waves and turbulence in coronal loops: observables and spectra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15057)
*Fabio Feraco,Francesco Pucci,Claudio Meringolo,Giuseppe Nisticò,Fabio Reale,Paolo Pagano,Gabriele Cozzo,Tom Van Doorsselaere,Bart De Pontieu,Paola Testa,Sergio Servidio,Oreste Pezzi,Francesco Valentini,Francesco Malara*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究通过数值模拟表明，MUSE任务的高分辨率光谱观测能够探测日冕环中的相混合和湍流级联现象，其强度图的功率谱分析可有效反演环内的密度扰动谱。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究旨在评估即将发射的Multi-slit Solar Explorer (MUSE) 任务能否通过其前所未有的高分辨率光谱观测，揭示太阳日冕中相混合和湍流级联这两种动力学现象的存在。这些过程对理解日冕加热和太阳风加速机制至关重要。

Method: 采用数值模拟方法研究日冕环中速度和磁场扰动的演化。模型设定为圆柱形压力平衡磁结构，包含横向密度和磁场不均匀性。初始扰动为扭转阿尔文波和横向湍流分量的叠加。通过计算Fe IX 171 Å谱线的矩，合成发射强度I₀、多普勒位移I₁和非热增宽I₂的二维分布图，并模拟MUSE探测器的空间分辨率（312 km×312 km）进行重建。最后计算并比较强度图和密度场的功率谱指数。

Result: 模拟结果显示：1）强度图显示纵向纤维结构的形成；2）小尺度扰动主要在环边界的不均匀区域产生，该区域的相混合效应和非热增宽效应最强；3）在MUSE分辨率下，强度功率谱指数与从完整3D密度场计算的谱指数高度一致，表明通过对I₀谱的分析可以推断环内密度扰动的谱信息。

Conclusion: 研究表明，通过对MUSE探测器获得的Fe IX 171 Å谱线强度图（I₀）的功率谱分析，可以可靠地推演日冕环内密度扰动的功率谱，从而验证相混合和湍流级联的存在。这意味着MUSE的高分辨率光谱观测能力将有助于揭示太阳日冕中这些关键动力学过程。

Abstract: We investigate numerically the time evolution of velocity and magnetic field fluctuations in a coronal loop, focusing on the dynamics due to both phase mixing and turbulent cascade. The intensity, doppler velocity and non-thermal broadening are synthesized from numerical results in order to establish if the upcoming Multi-slit Solar Explorer (MUSE) mission could reveal the presence of those phenomena in the solar corona through its unprecedented high-resolution spectroscopic observations. The loop is represented by a cylindrical pressure-balanced magnetic structure with a transverse density and magnetic field inhomogeneity. The initial perturbation is a superposition of a torsional Alfvén wave and a transverse turbulent component with different tunable weights. In order to reconstruct plasma emission features we calculate moments of the Fe IX 171 Å spectral line. 2D maps obtained by integrating the emission along the assumed line of sight are calculated for the emission intensity $I_0$, the Doppler shift $I_1$ and the non-thermal broadening $I_2$, for several values of the model parameters. Finally, we simulate MUSE spectrograph by considering a resolution of $312$ km $\times$ $312$ km. We observe how intensity maps show the formation of longitudinal threads. The generation of small-scale fluctuations mainly takes place in the inhomogeneity region at the loop boundary, where the effects of phase mixing and non-thermal broadening are stronger. 1D power spectra of intensity and Doppler shift maps are calculated and compared with the corresponding spectra of density and line-of-sight velocity component. The agreement observed between the spectral indexes of the intensity power spectra at MUSE resolution and the one computed from the full 3D density field indicates that spectra of $I_0$ can be used to infer information on the spectrum of density inside a loop.
