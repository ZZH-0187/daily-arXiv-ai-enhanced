### [2] [Physics of Circular Polarized Ion-Scale Waves in Hybrid Simulations of Alfvénic Fluctuations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14151)
*Hai Yang Harry Qian,Trevor A. Bowen,Carlos A. Gonzalez,Nikos Sioulas,Alfred Mallet,Kristopher G. Klein,Daniel Verscharen,Stuart D. Bale*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该论文通过2.5D混合模拟研究离子回旋波和快磁声/哨声波的产生机制区别：前者是线性、核心驱动的波动，后者是强阻尼、非线性产生的波动。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 离子回旋波和快磁声/哨声波作为离子动力学尺度的基本电磁模式，其产生机制和在等离子体演化中的作用尚不清楚，需要深入研究。

Method: 使用2.5D混合模拟宽带阿尔芬波动，质子速度分布建模为双麦克斯韦分量（热核心+漂移束），结合小波波识别、双麦克斯韦VDF拟合和PLUME线性色散求解器分析。

Result: ICW表现为线性模，当核心温度各向异性积累时增长，主要由核心驱动；FMW线性阻尼很强但实际观察到，表明由非线性过程产生；两类波的能流都主要向前传播。

Conclusion: ICW是线性、核心驱动的波，能将能量传递给等离子体；FMW是强阻尼、非线性产生的波，虽然存在线性阻尼但仍能持续存在。

Abstract: Ion cyclotron waves (ICW) and fast magnetosonic/whistler waves (FMW) are fundamental electromagnetic modes at ion kinetic scales, yet their generation mechanisms and roles in plasma evolution remain poorly understood. We analyze a 2.5D hybrid simulation of broadband Alfvénic fluctuations, where the proton velocity distribution is modeled as a sum of two bi-Maxwellian components: a thermal core and a drifting beam. Using wavelet-based wave identification, bi-Maxwellian VDF fitting, and the PLUME linear dispersion solver, we find that ICW behave as linear modes. Growth is intermittent, occurring when core temperature anisotropy builds up, and is driven mainly by the core (the beam contributes negligibly). Poynting flux analysis shows that ICW are predominantly forward-propagating, with a net energy flux ratio of $+1$ across all frequencies, consistent with the initial condition. FMW present a stark contrast: PLUME solutions often yield very small (near-zero) linear growth/damping rates. The species decomposition breaks down when $|γ/ω_r| \gtrsim 0.368$, indicating that linear theory predicts these waves to be strongly damped and not describable by linear eigenmodes. Nevertheless, FMW are clearly observed in the wavelet helicity spectrogram, indicating that they are generated by nonlinear processes (e.g., parametric decay or phase steepening) and persist despite linear damping. The net energy flux ratio for FMW is close to $+1$ at low frequencies but decreases at higher frequencies, yet never reaches zero (net energy flow remains forward). These results demonstrate that ICW are linear, core-driven waves that transfer energy to the plasma, while FMW are heavily damped, nonlinearly generated waves.

*Ji Qiang*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统空间电荷力随束流能量增加而受到γ⁻²抑制，但这种抑制可能不适用于电磁力的所有分量。需要研究横向电流在高能高亮度束流中是否会产生显著影响，特别是在三维会聚或发散束中。

Method: 提出了一个广义三维空间电荷求解器，考虑横向电流的贡献。为高斯密度分布的三维会聚或发散束提供了半解析解，并开发了适用于任意密度分布的数值解。

Result: 发现横向电流产生的附加力没有明显的γ依赖关系，在特定高能应用中可能变得显著。确定了这些力与传统空间电荷力相当的参数区域。在FCC-ee、ILC和LCLS-II-HE的强会聚电子束中，这些额外力对最终束流质量影响不显著。

While conventional space-charge forces scale inversely with the square of the relativistic Lorentz factor ($γ$) as beam energy increases, this suppression may not apply to all components of the electromagnetic force. In this paper, we present a generalized space-charge solver that accounts for contributions from transverse currents in three-dimensional (3D) converging or diverging beams. We demonstrate that these additional forces from transverse currents lack an explicit dependence on $γ$, potentially rendering them significant in specific high-energy applications. We provide a semi-analytical solution for a 3D converging or diverging beam with the Gaussian density distribution and identify the regimes in which these transverse current forces become comparable to conventional space-charge forces. Furthermore, we present a numerical solution for the generalized model applicable to arbitrary density distributions. Finally, we evaluate the impact of these forces on strongly converging electron beams within the interaction regions of the FCC-ee and ILC, as well as the second bunch compressor of LCLS-II-HE; our results indicate that these extra forces do not significantly alter the final electron beam quality in these specific cases.

  <summary>Details</summary>
