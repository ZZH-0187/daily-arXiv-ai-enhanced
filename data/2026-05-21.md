### [1] [Production of Nuclear Battery $β^{-}$ Emitters Driven by Fusion Neutrons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20260)
*J. F. Parisi*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 文章探讨利用氘氚聚变中子高效生产核电池放射性同位素，分析了14 MeV中子辐照靶材的潜力，发现147Pm、63Ni、39Ar、137Cs等是前景好的候选核素，并通过OpenMC模拟证明氚自持设计每年可生产超过1吨147Pm，产量比现有方法提高数个数量级。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 目前核电池所需放射性同位素缺乏可扩展的生产途径，而未来氘氚聚变中子的可用性可能大幅提高核电池同位素的制造速率。

Method: 通过模拟14 MeV氘氚聚变中子对靶材的辐照，评估生产核电池放射性同位素的能力，并使用OpenMC对托卡马克装置中的富集148Nd包层进行模拟分析。

Result: 研究发现147Pm、63Ni、39Ar、137Cs等是前景看好的放射性同位素候选核素。富集148Nd包层的氚自持设计每年可生产超过1吨147Pm，相当于约100亿居里/年的147Pm产量。

Conclusion: 氘氚聚变中子辐照技术能够实现核电池放射性同位素生产的革命性突破，产量比现有方法提高多个数量级，并可同时实现氚燃料循环的闭合。

Abstract: Nuclear batteries require radioisotopes with specific combinations of half-life, decay mode, and radiation properties, yet most candidate fuels lack scalable production routes. We show how the future availability of deuterium-tritium (D-T) fusion neutrons could enable manufacturing nuclear battery radioisotopes at many orders of magnitude higher rate than at present. We assess the capability of 14 MeV D-T fusion neutrons to produce nuclear battery radioisotopes by simulating feedstock material irradiation with neutrons. Promising radioisotope candidates include ${}^{147}$Pm, ${}^{63}$Ni, ${}^{39}$Ar, and ${}^{137}$Cs. Some feedstocks allow a radioisotope to be produced at scale while also closing the tritium fuel cycle, resulting in hundreds to over one thousand kilograms of high specific activity radioisotope per gigawatt thermal year of D-T fusion irradiation. We perform OpenMC simulations of an enriched ${}^{148}$Nd blanket for a tokamak, demonstrating that tritium self-sufficient designs can produce over one ton of ${}^{147}$Pm per gigawatt thermal year, equivalent to $\sim$one billion Curies per year of ${}^{147}$Pm. Operation of such a blanket would represent an unprecedented increase of nuclear battery radioisotope production capability.

### [2] [NeSST: A Python Tool for Neutron Spectra and Synthetic Diagnostics in Inertial Confinement Fusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20432)
*Aidan Crilly*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: NeSST 是一个开源的 Python 工具包，用于快速生成 ICF 内爆中的初级与单次散射中子能谱，内置核反应数据库、面密度不对称性建模、离子速度相关的散射核以及中子飞行时间信号合成模块。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 惯性约束聚变（ICF）内爆实验依赖中子能谱的精确分析，用以诊断反应过程、燃料面密度分布与不对称性，然而现有的能谱计算工具往往缺乏对初级及单次散射过程的系统模拟，限制了非均匀性等复杂因素的定量评估。

Method: 基于 ENDF 核反应数据库（包含微分与总截面数据）计算初级与单次散射中子能谱；利用勒让德模式展开描述面密度不对称性；采用预计算的离子速度相关散射核处理背向边缘形状；提供合成中子飞行时间模块，通过包含仪器响应函数、闪烁体灵敏度与束流衰减的全正向模型进行能谱-信号转换。

Result: NeSST 实现了对 DT、DD、TT 反应初级能谱与单次散射能谱的快速计算；可处理多种弹性与非弹性散射过程；通过 Legendre 模式展开能表征面密度不对称性；散射核方法能够解析离子速度对背向边缘形状的影响；合成 nToF 模块可模拟实验信号生成流程。

Conclusion: NeSST 的开发为 ICF 内爆实验的聚变中子能谱模拟提供了关键计算工具，其通用设计可有效支持不同实验条件的诊断需求，有助于深入理解内爆动力学与非均匀性影响。

Abstract: We present the Neutron Scattered Spectra Tool (NeSST), an open-source Python package for rapidly constructing primary and singly scattered neutron spectra from inertial confinement fusion (ICF) implosions. NeSST evaluates primary spectra for deuterium-tritium (DT), deuterium-deuterium (DD) and tritium-tritium (TT) reactions. Differential and total nuclear cross sections are read directly from Evaluated Nuclear Data File (ENDF) libraries. This enables elastic ($n$D, $n$T) and inelastic [D$(n,2n)$p, T$(n,2n)$D] scattering from DT fuel, as well as scattering from additional ablator materials such as $^{12}$C, to be treated within a common framework. Relativistic corrections to elastic scattering kinematics are included. Areal density asymmetries are incorporated through a Legendre mode expansion of the neutron-averaged projected areal density, allowing the spectral signatures of implosion non-uniformities to be computed and fitted. The effect of scattering ion velocities on the neutron backscatter edge shape is handled through pre-computed ion-velocity-dependent scattering kernels. A synthetic neutron time-of-flight (nToF) module converts energy spectra into detector signals with a full forward model that includes configurable instrument response functions (IRFs), energy-dependent scintillator sensitivity, and beamline attenuation. The code is publicly available at https://github.com/aidancrilly/NeSST

### [3] [Photodiode based multi-modal diagnostic for low-energy neutral beam injection in the LTX-$β$ spherical tokamak](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20993)
*A. Maan,Tosh Le,D. P. Boyle,R. Majeski,S. Banerjee,G. J. Wilkie,M. Lampert,C. Lopez Perez,R. Shousha,W. Capecchi,H. Gajani*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 开发了一种基于光电二极管的紧凑诊断阵列，用于研究低能中性束注入，该阵列在LTX-β球形托卡马克中结合了软X射线、莱曼-α和宽波段光电二极管行，具有近乎重合的切向视角，能够同时测量束诱发辐射，并观察到与锂处理历史相关的毫秒级信号变化。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在LTX-β球形托卡马克中研究低能中性束注入时，需要一种能够同时测量束诱发软X射线辐射、中性氢线辐射以及宽波段辐射的诊断工具，以分析束加热和燃料供给的平衡。

Method: 设计了一种真空内诊断阵列，包含滤波的软X射线、窄带莱曼-α和未滤波的AXUV光电二极管行，这些探测器具有部分重叠且几乎重合的切向等离子体视角，覆盖中性束路径。

Result: 初步测量显示，在12-20 keV氢束运行中，三种模式均检测到束同步的探测器响应；未滤波的AXUV信号表现出毫秒级的上升和下降时间，且随视线和锂处理历史变化；与经典慢化时间估计的比较表明，电荷交换对测量衰减有显著贡献。

Conclusion: 该诊断阵列可用于构建前向模型，以估计小型托卡马克中束加热和燃料供给的时间分辨平衡，为中性束注入研究提供重要工具。

Abstract: We present a compact photodiode-based diagnostic array developed to study low-energy neutral beam injection in the LTX-$β$ spherical tokamak. The in-vacuum diagnostic combines filtered soft-x-ray (SXR), narrowband Lyman-$α$, and unfiltered AXUV photodiode rows with partly overlapping, nearly coincident tangential views of the plasma, including the neutral beam path. This geometry provides simultaneous sensitivity to beam-induced SXR emission; neutral-hydrogen line radiation associated with recycling, fast neutrals and fueling; and broadband emission that can include direct neutral impacts from fast-ion charge-exchange losses. Initial measurements from 12-20 keV hydrogen beam operation show beam-synchronous detector responses in all three modalities. The unfiltered AXUV signals exhibit millisecond-scale rise and fall times that are much slower than the detector response, that vary across sightlines, and depend on lithium-conditioning history. Comparison with classical slowing-down time estimates indicates that charge exchange with background neutrals contributes appreciably to the measured decay. The diagnostic can potentially be used to constrain a forward model to estimate the time-resolved balance of beam heating and fueling for small tokamaks.

### [4] [Vortex Dipole Evolution in Viscoelastic Media: Effects of Asymmetry, Coupling, and Transverse Shear Waves](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21340)
*Vipul B Rohit,Vikram Dharodi,Sharad K Yadav*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 研究了粘弹性流体中Lamb-Oseen涡旋偶极子的动力学，重点分析了不对称性、耦合强度和横剪切波对强耦合尘埃等离子体的影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 尘埃等离子体为强耦合粘弹性行为提供了自然的实现环境，其中横剪切模式在不可压缩极限下占主导地位。研究波-涡旋耦合在复杂流体中的动力学机制，对理解强耦合等离子体和其他粘弹介质中的输运过程和结构形成具有意义。

Method: 采用不可压缩广义流体动力学模型进行数值模拟，分别研究对称和非对称涡旋偶极子（通过改变涡核尺寸、环流强度和分离距离实现）。分析了不同耦合强度下横剪切波的影响。

Result: 对称偶极子保持平移运动，速度随初始分离距离增大而减小，符合无粘预测；非对称配置导致旋转运动，弱涡旋围绕强涡旋轨道运动。粘弹性引入的横剪切波强度和传播速度随耦合增强而增加：弱耦合影响小，中度耦合改变传播并诱发变形，强耦合显著增强涡旋-涡旋相互作用，加速应变诱导变形并导致弱涡旋快速耗散。演化过程满足守恒定理。

Conclusion: 横剪切波在强耦合体系中显著增强涡旋相互作用，加速弱涡旋耗散。这些结果为理解复杂流体中波-涡旋耦合机制及其在强耦合等离子体和其他粘弹介质中对输运过程和结构形成的影响提供了重要见解。

Abstract: The dynamics of a Lamb-Oseen vortex dipole in a viscoelastic fluid are investigated, with emphasis on asymmetry, coupling strength, and transverse shear waves relevant to strongly coupled dusty plasmas. Dusty plasmas provide a natural realization of strongly coupled VE behavior, where transverse shear modes dominate in the incompressible limit. Numerical simulations are carried out using the incompressible generalized hydrodynamic model for both symmetric and asymmetric dipoles, with variations in vortex core size, circulation strength, and separation distance. In the symmetric case, dipoles exhibit sustained translational motion, with propagation speed decreasing as the initial separation distance increases, consistent with inviscid predictions. In contrast, asymmetric configurations-arising from unequal core radii or circulation strengths-lead to rotational motion due to imbalance in induced velocities, with the weaker vortex orbiting the stronger one. Viscoelasticity introduces transverse shear waves whose strength and propagation speed increase with coupling. In weakly coupled regimes, their influence is minor, while in moderately coupled regimes they modify propagation and induce deformation. In strongly coupled regimes, transverse shear waves significantly enhance vortex-vortex interaction, accelerating strain-induced deformation and leading to rapid dissipation of the weaker vortex. The evolution also satisfies the conservation theorem, where the contributions from convective, radiative, and dissipative processes dynamically compensate each other, maintaining global balance throughout the dynamics. These results provide insight into wave-vortex coupling in complex fluids, with implications for transport processes and structure formation in strongly coupled plasmas and other viscoelastic media.
