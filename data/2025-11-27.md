### [1] [Isotope Production in Muon-Catalyzed-Fusion Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20951)
*J. F. Parisi,A. Rutkowski*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该论文提出利用μ介子催化聚变(μCF)产生的高通量高能中子来生产有价值的同位素，可以显著改善μCF的经济前景。μCF系统可以在不达到能量收支平衡的情况下，通过同位素嬗变产生经济效益。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 提高μ介子催化聚变的经济性，通过利用其产生的中子束进行同位素生产来获得经济效益，在实现能量收支平衡之前就使μCF系统具有可行性。

Method: 使用μ介子催化聚变产生的高通量高能中子进行同位素嬗变，特别是设计一个每年产生10^8个μ介子的系统来生产^225Ac等有价值同位素。

Result: 一个μ介子产生率为10^8个/秒的μCF系统每年可生产0.5毫克的^225Ac，这是当前全球供应量的10倍，仅需10克^226Ra原料。

Conclusion: μCF系统在实现净能量产生之前就具有作为中子驱动同位素生产系统的可行性，应加速开发用于同位素生产的μCF系统。

Abstract: Producing valuable isotopes with high-flux high-energy neutrons generated by muon-catalyzed fusion ($μ$CF) reactions could substantially improve the economic prospects for muon-catalyzed fusion. Because no external heating is required for $μ$CF, heat flux constraints are significantly relaxed compared with fusion systems requiring external heating. This could allow $μ$CF to attain much higher neutron flux without breaching material heat flux limits. If muon production rates can be increased, $μ$CF systems employing transmutation could be viable well before energy breakeven is possible. For $μ$CF systems transmuting valuable isotopes, the required number of catalyzed fusion events per muon and muon energy generation cost can be relaxed by several orders of magnitude, making $μ$CF an attractive intense neutron source. We show an example $μ$CF system with a modest muon rate of $10^8$ muons / second could produce up to 0.5 mg of $^{225}$Ac per year - ten times current global supply - with a 10 gram $^{226}$Ra feedstock. As higher muon rate beams become available, many other radioisotope transmutation pathways become viable. These findings motivate the accelerated development of $μ$CF systems for neutron-driven isotope production before net energy generation is possible.

### [2] [Analytical Approximations for Beamstrahlung at Very High Energy Electron-Positron Colliders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20912)
*Dongxing He,Arianna Formenti,Spencer Gessner,Michael Peskin*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 该论文研究TeV能量下电子-正电子对撞中的同步辐射现象，发现在10 TeV对撞机设计中量子参数较大时，beamstrahlung的描述会简化，并提供了电子和光子波束束辐射谱的通用函数近似。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在TeV能量级电子-正电子对撞中，beamstrahlung（硬同步辐射）对高能电子、正电子和光子的能谱具有关键影响，并为所有其他QED过程计算提供初始条件。

Method: 在量子参数较大的极限条件下简化beamstrahlung描述，通过通用函数表示电子和光子的beamstrahlung谱，并提供这些函数的近似表达式。

Result: 得到了beamstrahlung谱的简化描述和通用函数，这些函数对于研究极高能量下的束束相互作用具有实用价值。

Conclusion: 在10 TeV对撞机设计的大量子参数极限下，beamstrahlung现象的描述可以简化，研究结果为高能束束相互作用的通用研究提供了有用工具。

Abstract: Among the many effects that occur in beam-beam electron-positron collisions at TeV energies, emission of hard synchrotron radiation, or beamstrahlung, has special importance. Beamstrahlung determines the energy spectrum of the most energetic electrons, positrons, and photons and supplies the initial condition for the calculation of all other QED processes. In this paper, we show that the description of beamstrahlung simplifies in the limit of large quantum parameter $Υ$, which is realized in 10 TeV collider designs. The beamstrahlung spectra for electrons and photons are given in terms of universal functions. We supply approximations to these functions that will be useful for more general studies of the beam-beam interaction at very high energies.

</details>
