### [1] [Wake Perturbations in Laser- and Beam-Driven Plasma Wakefield Accelerators: A Symmetry-Based Multipole Classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18845)
*Andrei C. Berceanu,Alessio Del Dotto*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 论文通过理想化等离子体尾波加速器的对称性群，提供了一个统一框架来分类和分析束品质现象，将横向扰动按方位角多极阶m排序，揭示不同加速机制中相似物理的数学统一，并为优化设计和未来研究提供指导。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 激光驱动（LWFA）和束驱动（PWFA）等离子体尾波加速器中的束品质问题缺乏统一的理论框架，不同加速机制中出现的各种束品质现象（如软管不稳定性、脉冲前沿倾斜抖动、光斑不对称发射度增长等）需要系统性分析，以揭示其内在联系并指导未来加速器设计。

Method: 基于理想化吹出尾波对称性群的分类方法：包括轴对称性SO(2)φ、绝热纵向平移和传播方向宇称。横向扰动按整数方位角多极阶m（SO(2)φ的不可约表示）进行分类，最低阶束品质可观测量耦合到特定多极：束质心(m=1)、跨平面发射度耦合(m=2)。同时采用辛类比将横向匹配与纵向束加载联系起来。

Result: 建立了一个基于对称性群的分类框架：1）将各种束品质现象统一分类到不同的m通道中；2）揭示了LWFA和PWFA中常见现象的数学统一性，它们主要占据两个最低非平凡m通道(m=1和m=2)；3）将正电子见证问题重新组织到同一框架中，每个已知缓解策略都放弃均匀密度吹出的一个特定特征；4）提出了m=3响应通道的可能性；5）建立了与等离子体加速器对称等变贝叶斯优化的联系。

Conclusion: 该论文提出了一种基于对称性群的统一理论框架来理解等离子体尾波加速器中的束品质物理，将各种束品质现象分类到不同的方位角多极阶通道中，揭示了不同加速机制（LWFA和PWFA）中相似物理的数学统一性，并为未来等离子体加速器的优化和控制提供了理论基础。

Abstract: We review beam-quality physics in laser-driven (LWFA) and beam-driven (PWFA) plasma wakefield accelerators through the symmetry group of the idealised blowout wake -- axisymmetry $\mathrm{SO}(2)_φ$, adiabatic longitudinal translation, and propagation-direction parity. Transverse perturbations of the wake are classified by an integer azimuthal multipole order $m$ labelling the irreducible representations of $\mathrm{SO}(2)_φ$, with the lowest beam-quality observables coupling at a specific multipole: the bunch centroid at $m=1$, cross-plane emittance coupling at $m=2$. A symplectic analogy relates transverse matching to longitudinal beam loading. Several phenomena common to LWFA and PWFA -- hose instabilities, pulse-front-tilt jitter, spot-asymmetry emittance growth, polarisation-dependent centroid motion, resonant cross-plane mixing -- populate the two lowest non-trivial $m$-channels and admit a unified discussion. The positron-witness problem reorganises in the same language: each known mitigation abandons one specific feature of the uniform-density blowout, drawn from a finite set. The classification also raises the possibility of an $m=3$ response channel whose magnitude remains open. We note the connection to symmetry-equivariant Bayesian optimisation of plasma accelerators.

### [2] [Bivariate incomplete-Bessel kernels for the first nonlinear Vlasov-Maxwell response](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19261)
*Roberto Ricci*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 采用不完整Bessel函数方法简化磁化等离子体非线性Vlasov方程的求解，避免了传统谐波展开的复杂性，并引入了新的双变量轨道解析函数。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 解决传统双回旋谐波展开方法在表示磁化等离子体弱非线性响应时的复杂性：产生冗长的Bessel函数和嵌套共振分母，使得理论推导和数值计算变得繁琐。

Method: 将线性问题中开发的"不完整Bessel函数G_μ(z,ψ)"方法直接应用于第一阶非线性Vlasov-Maxwell方程。通过保持拉莫尔相位不展开，并在外层特征积分中使用新的双变量轨道解析函数G_{μ,ν}^{(r)}(z,ψ;w,χ)来表示非线性分布函数。

Result: 推导出以双变量轨道解析函数表示的非线性分布函数，获得了计算电流投影所需的恒等式，并通过展开恢复了经典双谐波Liu-Tripathi公式的扩展形式，同时还指出了如何从双变量角收缩中获得非线性磁化率张量。

Conclusion: 该论文提出了一种处理磁化等离子体弱非线性问题的创新方法，使用不完整的Bessel函数表示代替传统的双回旋谐波展开，简化了非线性动力学方程的形式推导和数值实现。

Abstract: The weakly nonlinear response of a homogeneous magnetised plasma is usually written as a double cyclotron-harmonic expansion. This representation is explicit, but the repeated use of the Jacobi-Anger expansion produces long sums of Bessel functions and nested resonance denominators. In the linear problem we recently obtained an alternative formulation by keeping the Larmor phase unexpanded and by evaluating the characteristic integral in terms of the newly introduced incomplete-Bessel function $G_μ(z,ψ)$. In this article we apply the same idea directly to the first nonlinear Vlasov-Maxwell equation. The nonlinear source contains the linear response of an inner mode. When this linear response is written in incomplete-Bessel form, the outer characteristic integral produces a bivariate orbit-resolvent $G_{μ,ν}^{(r)}(z,ψ;w,χ)$. This function emerges therefore as the natural orbit integral generated by the first nonlinear characteristic problem. We derive the nonlinear distribution function in terms of these bivariate functions, collect the identities needed for the current projection, recover an extension of the classical double-harmonic Liu-Tripathi formula by expansion, and indicate how the nonlinear susceptibility tensor is obtained from bivariate angular contractions.
