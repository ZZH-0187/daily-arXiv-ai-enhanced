### [1] [An explicit multiscale pseudo orbit-averaging time integration algorithm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00121)
*Maxwell Rosen,Manaurer Francisquez,Gregory Wayne Hammett*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 提出了一种显式多尺度算法，用于求解含有可被分离和缩放快慢动力学的微分方程。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为解决含有高频模式的微分方程计算效率问题，特别是等离子体磁镜中快速粒子穿越与慢速碰撞区域并存的情况。

Method: 引入分阶段时间积分器，在已知动力学尺度边界时，一个阶段求解未修改方程，另一个阶段冻结部分相空间并减缓快速动力学的演化。

Result: 应用于磁镜等离子体简化动力学模型，通过两个代表性模型问题将磁镜动力学分解为更简单、计算成本低的形式，实现了约ω/ν_c倍的加速，实际案例中达到30,000倍加速。

Conclusion: 该方法通过分离和缩放快慢动力学，有效提高了含有高频模式微分方程的求解效率，在等离子体磁镜模型中展现了显著的计算加速效果。

Abstract: We present an explicit multiscale algorithm for solving differential equations for problems with high-frequency modes that can be averaged over by separating and scaling the fast and slow dynamics within a single equation. We introduce a phased time integrator for cases where the boundaries of dynamical scales are known: one phase solves the unmodified equation, while the other freezes part of phase-space and slows down the evolution of the fast dynamics. This algorithm is applied to reduced kinetic models of plasmas in magnetic mirrors, which feature a distinct boundary between a region dominated by rapid particle transit and a region characterized by slow collisions. Two representative model problems are presented that decompose the dynamics of the magnetic mirror into a simpler, computationally inexpensive form. The model problems demonstrate a speedup by a factor of order $ω/ ν_c$, where $ω$ is the fast oscillation frequency and $ν_c$ is the slow damping rate. This is a 30,000$\times$ speedup for a case of practical interest.

### [2] [Generalized multi-dimensional conservation laws for stimulated Raman and Brillouin scattering in a density gradient](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00295)
*Vijay Patel,Sarah Chase,Frank S. Tsung,John P. Palastro,Denise E. Hinkel,Warren B. Mori*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 使用诺特定理推导了密度梯度中受激拉曼散射（SRS）和布里渊后向散射（SBS）的广义局域和多维守恒定律，包括作用量、能量、动量和角动量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 旨在扩展当前对SRS和SBS的理解，超越一维的Manley-Rowe关系，在多维情况下建立更一般的守恒定律框架，特别是考虑密度梯度效应。

Method: 在傍轴射线近似下，首先找到一个拉格朗日密度，该密度能重现无阻尼情况下密度梯度中SRS和SBS的包络方程。然后，应用诺特定理，利用拉格朗日密度的对称性推导出局域守恒定律。

Result: 成功推导出了作用量、能量、动量和角动量的局域和多维守恒定律。这些多维守恒定律可简化为已知的一维Manley-Rowe关系以及频率和波数匹配条件。此外，还得到了轨道角动量及由频率和波数移动引起的能量和动量贡献的新守恒量。

Conclusion: 诺特定理为SRS和SBS在密度梯度中的多维度守恒定律提供了一个强大而系统的框架，所推导的守恒定律不仅概括了现有理论，还揭示了新的物理量（如轨道角动量）的守恒性。

Abstract: Generalized local and multi-dimensional conservation laws of action, energy, momentum, and angular momentum are derived for stimulated Raman (SRS) and Brillouin backscattering (SBS) in a density gradient within the paraxial ray approximation. A Lagrangian density is found that reproduces the well known envelope equations for SRS and SBS in density gradients in the absence of damping. Using Noether's theorem, the symmetries of the Lagrangian density are used to obtain local conservation laws for quantities that can easily be identified as the action, energy, and momentum. These multi-dimensional conservation laws reduce to the well known one dimensional Manley-Rowe relations, and frequency and wavenumber matching conditions. Additional symmetries of the action lead to conversation laws for new quantities that are identified as orbital angular momentum and contributions to the energy and momentum of the wave from frequency and wavenumber shifts.

### [3] [Generating intense attosecond pulses and vectorizing polarization states from laser-plasma interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00622)
*Panfei Geng,Yipeng Wu,Zhixin Fan,Min Chen,Longqing Yi,Xiaohui Yuan,Zhengming Sheng,Warren B. Mori,Chan Joshi,Jie Zhang*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该论文研究了通过相对论激光-等离子体相互作用生成高阶谐波矢量光束，探索了在极紫外和软X射线波段产生结构化偏振与轨道角动量光束的物理机制和控制方法，实现了可生成螺旋波前且偏振态可控的孤立阿秒脉冲。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当前在可见光和红外波段已能稳定产生具有空间结构化偏振和自旋 轨道角动量的矢量光束，但将其扩展到极紫外和软X射线区域，特别是在相对论强度下，仍然是一个重大挑战。研究者希望填补这一空白，为超快和强场光-物质相互作用研究提供新的工具。

Method: 结合理论分析与三维粒子云模拟，研究了驱动激光与等离子体相互作用产生高阶谐波过程中偏振与轨道角动量的传递和转换机制。提出了通过调控驱动场的拓扑荷来控制谐波偏振与轨道角动量的确定性方法。

Result: 研究证明，驱动场的拓扑电荷可以确定性控制发射谐波的偏振拓扑结构和轨道角动量。利用矢量光束的固有特性，通过少周期驱动脉冲或对多周期脉冲施加矢量偏振选通，能够产生具有螺旋波前和空间定制偏振态的强孤立阿秒脉冲。

Conclusion: 该工作确立了在极紫外和软X射线区域产生高强度结构化光源的途径，为具有工程化角动量的超快和强场光-物质相互作用研究开辟了新机遇。

Abstract: Vector beams with spatially structured polarization and intertwined spin-orbital angular momentum (SAM-OAM) provide powerful degrees of freedom for tailoring light-matter interactions. While such structured beams are well established in the visible and infrared regimes, extending them to the extreme-ultraviolet (EUV) and soft X-ray (SXR) domains at relativistic intensities remains a major challenge. Here, we investigate the generation of higher-order harmonic vector beams driven by relativistic laser-plasma interactions. Combining theoretical analysis with three-dimensional particle-in-cell simulations, we elucidate the underlying physical mechanisms governing the transfer and conversion of polarization and orbital angular momentum during harmonic generation. We demonstrate that both the polarization topology and OAM of the emitted harmonics can be deterministically controlled by the topological charges of the driving field. Owing to the intrinsic properties of vector beams, either few-cycle driving pulses or vector polarization gating applied to multi-cycle pulses enable the production of intense isolated attosecond pulses featuring spiral wavefronts and spatially tailored polarization states. These results establish a pathway toward high-intensity structured light sources in the EUV and SXR regimes and open new opportunities for ultrafast and strong-field light-matter interaction studies with engineered angular momentum.

### [4] [Cyclic reformation of subcritical perpendicular fast magnetosonic shocks due to oblique Whistler waves](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00633)
*ME Dieckmann,L Palodhi,M Francois,D Folini,R Walder*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 使用二维PIC模拟研究亚临界垂直快速磁声速激波（传播速度1.7倍于快速磁声速）的稳定性，重点关注斜向低混杂梯度漂移不稳定性在激波斜坡中的作用机制


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究特定磁化等离子体配置中亚临界激波的稳定性机制，尤其是解释在无外部扰动情况下激波自发重构的物理成因

Method: 二维粒子云（PIC）模拟，电子与完全电离氮等离子体，均匀磁场相对模拟平面法向倾斜45度，该配置产生维持激波磁斜坡的抗磁电流

Result: 抗磁电流在斜坡中驱动斜向低混杂梯度漂移不稳定性；该不稳定性通过反向传播的斜向Whistler波与正向传播的离子声波的反应耦合产生；Whistler波调制激波磁场，静电离子密度调制迫使激波塌陷为磁活塞并随后重构

Conclusion: 激波重构由内部斜向Whistler波驱动而非外部扰动，揭示了亚临界垂直快速磁声速激波稳定性的新机制

Abstract: The stability of subcritical perpendicular fast magnetosonic shocks, which are propagating at 1.7 times the fast magnetosonic speed, is investigated using two-dimensional PIC simulations. The plasma, composed of electrons and fully ionized nitrogen, is permeated by a uniform magnetic field oriented at 45 degrees to the simulation plane normal. This configuration results in a diamagnetic current that sustains the shocks magnetic ramp and is partially resolved within the simulation plane. The diamagnetic current drives an oblique lower-hybrid gradient drift instability within the ramp. This instability has been observed in magnetic reconnection experiments and studied in the framework of a Harris-type sheath in previous studies. It arises from a reactive coupling between the oblique Whistler wave, which is propagating backward in the electron rest frame, and the forward-propagating ion acoustic wave. Our simulations show that the magnetic component of this wave modulates the shocks magnetic field, while the electrostatic ion density modulation forces the shock to collapse into a magnetic piston and then reform. The reformation is not forced by an external perturbation as in previous simulations but by the oblique Whistler wave.

### [5] [Ultrafast Kilowatt-Range Microwave Pulsing for Enhanced CO2 Conversion in Atmospheric-Pressure Plasmas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01136)
*S. Soldatov,L. Silberer,C. K. Kiefer,G. Link,A. Navarrete,J. Jelonnek*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 超快微波功率脉冲能增强大气压等离子体反应器的CO2转化效能，研究探讨了将这种方法从数百瓦扩展到千瓦级的可行性，比较了两种千瓦级反应器的性能，发现脉冲操作在转化率和能效方面有一定提升，但效果受反应器设计和后处理方式影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 验证超快微波功率脉冲策略在千瓦级功率下对CO2转化的增强效果是否具有可扩展性，特别是比较不同反应器配置（喷嘴淬火 vs. 管式缓慢冷却）对性能的影响。

Method: 在两种千瓦级微波等离子体反应器（Surfaguide系统和腔体基等离子体火炬）中进行实验，使用微波功率脉冲（峰值约4 kW，脉宽从亚微秒到微秒级），对比连续波操作，分析CO2转化率、能效以及瞬时反射微波功率以推断电子密度动态。

Result: 在Surfaguide基反应器中，脉冲操作相比连续波操作在CO2转化率上提升了<40%，能效提升了<20%；但在使用快速喷嘴淬火的IPP火炬中，这种提升基本被抑制。两种千瓦级反应器均未观察到等离子体重燃机制，导致等离子体温度调制较弱。

Conclusion: 超快微波功率脉冲在千瓦级尺度上对CO2转化有一定增强效果，但其效果受反应器设计和后处理冷却方式影响显著。其中，缓慢冷却的Surfaguide系统在脉冲操作下表现更好，而快速淬火则会抑制增强效果，表明脉冲策略的实际应用需结合反应器具体设计进行优化。

Abstract: Ultrafast microwave power pulsation is demonstrated as an effective strategy to enhance CO2 conversion in atmospheric-pressure plasma reactors. While initial experiments at several hundred watts in a compact coaxial plasma torch showed improved performance, the present study investigates the scalability of this approach to kilowatt-range microwave power. Conversion and energy efficiency were examined in two reactor configurations: a Surfaguide-based system (KIT) and a cavity-based plasma torch (IPP), and benchmarked against the compact coaxial torch. Both kilowatt-scale setups share similar microwave coupling schemes, power levels, reactor tubes, and gas injection geometries, but differ in afterglow treatment. The torch at IPP employs rapid nozzle-based quenching, whereas the Surfaguide-based reactor relies on slower cooling along an extended quartz tube. Stable plasma operation was achieved at pulsation peak powers of ~4 kW and pulse durations from sub-microseconds to microseconds, with stability limited to inter-pulse times of ~10 us (cavity-based torch) and ~12 us (Surfaguide-based reactor). In contrast to the coaxial torch, no plasma reignition regime was observed in either kilowatt-scale reactor, resulting in weaker plasma temperature modulation. Notably, the period-averaged gas temperature in the Surfaguide-based reactor exceeded that under continuous-wave operation. Under these conditions, relative enhancements of <40% in CO2 conversion and <20% in energy efficiency were measured compared with continuous-wave operation. These improvements were largely suppressed in the torch at IPP, presumably due to rapid afterglow quenching. Finally, analysis of the instantaneous reflected microwave power provided qualitative insights into electron density dynamics during the power-OFF and power-ON phases.
