### [1] [Self-consistent treatment of Intra Beam Scattering, betatron coupling, and vertical dispersion in fourth generation light sources](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01892)
*Sébastien Joly,Jonas Kallestrup,Félix Soubelet*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 该文建立了一个ODE框架来模拟在同步辐射、量子激发、耦合、垂直色散和束内散射共同作用下的三维束发射度演化，特别关注耦合对阻尼分配数的影响及其对平衡发射度的作用，并以BESSY III为例分析了不同垂直发射度生成机制（横向反馈、耦合、垂直色散）的权衡。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 第四代光源的X射线亮度强烈依赖于电子束流和横向发射度。在低中能区，低发射度与高束流会导致高相空间密度，进而受束内散射限制。通常通过耦合增加垂直发射度来缓解束内散射，但耦合引起的阻尼分配数重分布、垂直色散及其对同步辐射与束内散射平衡的影响常被忽视，需要建立自洽模型来准确描述这些效应。

Method: 作者建立了一个基于常微分方程的自洽框架，同时考虑同步辐射、量子激发、耦合、垂直色散和束内散射，允许现实的阻尼分配数。该模型系统地纳入了耦合引起的同步辐射阻尼率和束内散射增长率的改变，能描述三维束发射度的稳态和时间演化。

Result: 应用于BESSY III晶格表明，阻尼分配重分布和光学修改显著影响平衡发射度。对通过横向反馈激励、耦合和垂直色散产生垂直发射度的系统比较显示，水平发射度的降低与运行约束之间存在权衡。

Conclusion: 建立的ODE框架能更真实地描述耦合、垂直色散和阻尼分配数对发射度演化的影响。实际光源设计中需综合考虑不同垂直发射度生成机制对水平和垂直发射度的平衡以及运行约束，以获得最优性能。

Abstract: The X-ray brightness delivered by fourth-generation light sources strongly depends on the electron beam current and transverse emittance. Reaching higher brilliance and lower emittances are increasingly limited by intra beam scattering, particularly at low and medium beam energies, where low emittances combined with high beam currents result in large phase-space densities. Increasing the vertical emittance through betatron coupling is commonly employed to mitigate intra beam scattering by relaxing the phase-space density. However, the redistribution of damping partition numbers due to coupling, the presence of vertical dispersion, and consequently their impact on the balance between synchrotron radiation and intra beam scattering are often neglected. In this work, we develop a self-consistent Ordinary Differential Equations-based framework to describe both the steady-state and time evolution of three-dimensional beam emittances in the simultaneous presence of synchrotron radiation, quantum excitation, betatron coupling, vertical dispersion, and intra beam scattering; allowing for realistic damping partition numbers. The model consistently accounts for the modification of synchrotron radiation damping rates and intra beam scattering growth rates arising from betatron coupling. Application to the BESSY III lattice demonstrates that damping partition redistribution and optics modifications significantly influence the equilibrium emittances. A systematic comparison of vertical emittance generation via a transverse feedback-generated excitation, betatron coupling, and vertical dispersion highlights the trade-offs between horizontal emittance reduction and operational constraints.

### [3] [Triggering physical plasmoids in forming current sheets: conditions and diagnostics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02065)
*Hubert Baty*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 研究了在电阻磁流体动力学框架下，动态形成电流层中触发撕裂模不稳定性的条件。使用谱方法模拟Orszag-Tang涡，结合电流密度和涡度谱诊断，确立了物理性撕裂模触发的三个关键条件，并解决了数值噪声在触发过程中的作用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 解决García Morillo & Alexakis (2025)提出的明显悖论，明确区分物理性撕裂模与数值赝像，阐明在谱模拟中触发撕裂模不稳定性的精确条件，澄清数值噪声的作用。

Method: 采用电阻磁流体动力学框架，使用伪谱法代码模拟Lundquist数S~10^5的Orszag-Tang涡。利用电流密度功率谱E_J(k)和涡度谱E_ω(k)评估模拟收敛性，通过扰动实验和连续噪声注入验证触发条件。

Result: 在谱模拟中物理性撕裂模触发需同时满足三个条件：1）在电流密度最大时刻附近施加扰动；2）扰动幅度高于临界阈值εc~10^-5；3）扰动频谱包含不稳定波数。连续噪声注入在振幅低1-2个数量级时产生类似结果，增长率与撕裂模数量与Comisso2017理论吻合良好。

Conclusion: 研究成功解决了之前的悖论，证明了谱方法中物理性撕裂模的触发条件，明确了数值噪声在触发过程中的作用，为撕裂模不稳定性的数值研究提供了可靠的诊断标准和触发机制。

Abstract: We investigate the conditions for triggering the plasmoid instability in a dynamically forming current sheet in the resistive magnetohydrodynamic framework, using a pseudo-spectral code applied to the Orszag-Tang vortex at Lundquist number $S \sim 10^5$. Following García Morillo \& Alexakis (2025), we use the power spectrum of the current density $E_J(k)$, complemented by the vorticity spectrum $E_ω(k)$, to assess the convergence of our simulations, and show that this diagnostic remains valid even in the presence of physical plasmoids, allowing us to unambiguously distinguish them from spurious ones. We then show that physical plasmoids can be triggered in a well-resolved spectral simulation when three conditions are simultaneously met: a perturbation applied near the time of maximum current density, with amplitude above a critical threshold $\varepsilon_c \sim 10^{-5}$ for our numerical scheme, and with spectral content containing the unstable wavenumbers. These conditions are confirmed using continuous noise injection, which yields similar results at amplitudes one to two orders of magnitude lower. The resulting growth rates and plasmoid numbers are in good agreement with the theory of \citet{Comisso2017}. These results resolve the apparent paradox raised by García Morillo \& Alexakis (2025) and also clarify the role of numerical noise in the triggering of the plasmoid instability.

### [4] [Simulations of internal kink modes and sawtooth crashes for SPARC baseline-like scenarios using the M3D-C1 code](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02172)
*W. H. Wang,C. Clauser,C. Liu,N. Ferraro,R. A. Tinguely*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 首次对SPARC托卡马克进行低n不稳定性研究，发现主导的内部缩缩模，观察到不同的锯齿状崩溃行为，为理解MHD不稳定性对粒子热输运的影响提供了基础。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 分析SPARC托卡马克中MHD不稳定性对于评估其性能及未来聚变电站设计至关重要，特别是了解低n模不稳定性对等离子体约束和输运的影响。

Method: 使用SPARC基准设计点进行模拟，采用高保真3D扩展MHD代码M3D-C1进行线性和非线性模拟，同时开发简化的1D本征值求解器进行对比分析。

Result: 识别出在q=1面处主导的n=1内部缩缩模；线性增长率对keV级温度剖面和q0值敏感；非线性模拟中观察到中等和强烈两种锯齿状崩溃，可解释为Kadomtsev和Wesson模型的混合；实际锯齿崩溃可能在q0降至远低于1之前就发生。

Conclusion: 这项工作为理解MHD不稳定性下的粒子和热输运提供了基础，对准确评估SPARC托卡马克和未来聚变电站的性能至关重要，同时提供了一个研究周期性锯齿状振荡的机会。

Abstract: A relaxed baseline case, based on the SPARC Primary Reference Discharge (PRD) design point, is used to conduct a thorough investigation for the most unstable low-$n$ MHD instabilities for the first time. The simulations use the high-fidelity 3D extended-MHD code M3D-C1. The linear simulation, by scanning over the resistivity, identifies a dominant internal kink mode at the $q=1$ surface with a toroidal mode number $n=1$. Both the current and the pressure profiles are strongly affecting the kink instability in the baseline case. The linear growth rate is sensitive to the keV-level temperature profile and the on-axis $q_0$ around unity. A simplified 1D eigenvalue solver shows a good qualitative agreement for the observed pressure effects. In 3D nonlinear simulations, the marginally unstable case gives a moderate sawtooth crash soon after $q_0$ drops below unity, likely because of the lack of stabilizing effects in our simulations, such as heating and energetic particles. When both the current and the pressure drives exist (the baseline case), a strong sawtooth is observed, which features a magnetic reconnection event and a hollowed pressure profile. This can be explained by mixing both the Kadomtsev and Wesson models. The actual sawtooth crash may occur in SPARC before $q_0$ drops far below unity due to the sensitive changes of the instability around $q_0\sim 1$. The sawtooth-like oscillations shown in low-$β$ simulations also provides an opportunity to investigate periodic sawtoothing timescales in SPARC. This work forms a basis for understanding particle and heat transport under the influence of MHD instabilities, which can be essential for properly assessing the performance of the SPARC tokamak and future fusion pilot plants.

</details>
