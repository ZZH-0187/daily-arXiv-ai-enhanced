### [1] [Fermilab PIP II machine protection system digitized data noise elimination scheme and its FPGA implementation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11415)
*J. Wu,A. Warner*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 这篇论文提出了一种针对PIP-II机器保护系统中光束损失信号的数字噪声抑制方法，采用三个FPGA实时处理模块来抑制宽频带噪声，特别是60Hz谐波干扰。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在Fermilab的PIP-II机器保护系统中，光束损失信号受到高频噪声和60Hz交流电源设备低频噪声的污染，这些噪声与光束损失信号频率重叠，需要比标准滤波更先进的数字处理技术。

Method: 在FPGA上模拟和测试了三个实时功能模块：1）双时间常数放电积分器滤波器，2）去纹波基线提取和存储模块，3）快速恢复放电积分器。非线性IIR积分器滤波器去除高频噪声并输入基线提取器；在检测到突发光束损失时切换到更长的时间常数防止基线失真；去纹波块通过多个60Hz周期的平均计算有效基线并存储在FPGA RAM中；该基线从原始数据中减去后由快速恢复块积分，该块在使用后快速重置。

Result: 所有模块都达到了预期性能。

Conclusion: 提出的数字处理方案有效地抑制了PIP-II光束损失监测系统中的噪声干扰，特别是60Hz及其谐波，提高了系统的可靠性和准确性。

Abstract: In Fermilab's PIP-II machine protection system, beam loss signals from various detectors are digitized at 125 MS/s. Noise from both high-frequency sources and low-frequency 60 Hz AC power equipment can contaminate the data. To suppress noise across these ranges especially 60 Hz and its harmonics, which overlap with beam loss signal frequencies advanced digital processing beyond standard filtering is required. Several real-time functional blocks were simulated and tested on an FPGA: (1) a dual time-constant discharging integrator filter, (2) a de-ripple baseline extraction and storage block, and (3) a fast-recovery discharging integrator. The nonlinear IIR integrator filter removes high-frequency noise and feeds into the baseline extractor. Upon detecting abrupt beam loss, it switches to a longer time constant to prevent baseline distortion. The de-ripple block calculates a valid baseline by averaging over multiple 60 Hz periods, storing results in a 4096-word FPGA RAM. This baseline is subtracted from raw data before integration by the fast-recovery block, which resets quickly after use. All blocks achieved expected performance.

### [2] [Intrabeam Scattering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11952)
*Andrzej Wolski*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 本文回顾了束内散射的理论模型和影响，重点介绍了Piwinski公式及其在不同加速器参数下的实验对比。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 束内散射会导致束流横向和纵向发射度随时间无限制增长，这对于需要维持低发射度的加速器性能至关重要。

Method: 分析了束内散射的多种理论模型，特别介绍了用于计算发射度增长率的Piwinski公式，并在不同参数条件下的加速器中进行了实验测量对比。

Result: 通过理论模型预测与实验测量的对比，验证了束内散射对发射度增长的影响，为不同加速器参数条件下的束流动力学分析提供了参考。

Conclusion: 本文系统地总结了束内散射的理论分析和实验验证，为理解和缓解加速器中的束内散射效应提供了重要基础。

Abstract: Intrabeam scattering refers to the effects of the Coulomb interaction acting between pairs of charged particles within a bunch in an accelerator. One of the main consequences of intrabeam scattering is a change in the emittances of a bunch: in some circumstances (in particular, in hadron storage rings operating above transition), the transverse and longitudinal emittances may grow over time without limit. This may restrict the performance of machines for which maintaining low beam emittance is an important requirement. In this note, we describe some of the models used to analyse the effects of intrabeam scattering, and present in particular the Piwinski formulae for the emittance growth rates. We compare the predicted changes in emittance with measurements in a number of machines operating in different parameter regimes.

### [3] [Investigation of Toroidal Rotation Effects on Spherical Torus Equilibria using the Fast Spectral Solver VEQ-R](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11422)
*Xingyu Li,Huasheng Xie,Lai Wei,Zhengxiong Wang*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 开发了一个高效的光谱求解器VEQ-R，用于计算具有任意环向流的托卡马克等离子体固定边界平衡，相比标准简化模型能更准确地捕捉强旋转下的复杂几何响应。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 标准的简化模型往往难以充分描述托卡马克等离子体在强环向旋转下的复杂几何响应，特别是在高马赫数（$M \sim 1.0$）情况下，需要更高效的求解器来准确捕捉动态形状变化和通量面畸变。

Method: 采用12参数偏移切比雪夫光谱展开来显式解析高阶形状剖面（如动态伸长率和三角形度）的径向变化，结合紧凑变分公式和新颖的'矩阵核'加速技术，将问题转化为预计算的代数矩阵运算。

Result: 求解器在大约5毫秒内收敛，与高分辨率基准相比保持出色的几何保真度，在速度和精度之间取得平衡。分析显示旋转引起的通量压缩导致核心安全因子$q_0$单调下降，将其危险地推近1。

Conclusion: VEQ-R作为一个近似但稳健的求解器，能够有效捕捉结构变形机制，在挑战性的超声区域（$M \sim 1.0$）准确捕捉差分通量表面畸变（非刚性效应），为解决复杂等离子体平衡问题提供了高效实用的工具。

Abstract: Standard reduced models often fail to adequately describe the complex geometric response of tokamak plasmas to strong toroidal rotation. In this work, we present VEQ-R, a computationally efficient spectral solver designed to calculate fixed-boundary equilibria with arbitrary toroidal flow. In contrast to computationally intensive grid-based codes, our model employs a 12-parameter shifted Chebyshev spectral expansion to explicitly resolve radial variations in high-order shaping profiles--such as dynamic elongation and triangularity. This capability allows the solver to accurately capture differential flux surface distortions (non-rigid effects) even in challenging sonic regimes ($M \sim 1.0$). By synergizing this compact variational formulation with a novel ``Matrix-Kernel'' acceleration technique, we transform the problem into pre-computed algebraic matrix operations. This approach achieves convergence in approximately 5 ms, maintaining exceptional geometric fidelity compared to high-resolution benchmarks while balancing speed and accuracy. Our analysis reveals that rotation-induced flux compression leads to a monotonic decrease in the core safety factor $q_0$, pushing it dangerously close to unity--a structural deformation mechanism effectively captured by this approximate yet robust solver.

### [4] [Tuning Optical Properties of FTO via Carbonaceous Al2O3 Microdot Deposition by DC plasma sputtering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11970)
*Sarah Salah,Ahmed Atlam,Nagat Elkahwagy,Abdelhamid Elshaer,Mohammed Shihab*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 通过直流等离子体溅射在FTO上沉积碳掺杂Al2O3微点，有效降低了FTO的高反射率，在可见光范围内反射率最低，可用于太阳能电池和光电器件的抗反射涂层。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: FTO作为透明导电氧化物在光伏和光电器件中具有重要作用，但其高反射率限制了光捕获效率，因此需要开发有效的抗反射涂层来提高器件性能。

Method: 使用简单的直流等离子体溅射方法，在Ar、O2和Ar-O2混合气氛控制下，在FTO衬底上沉积碳掺杂的Al2O3微点结构。通过调整气体成分控制微点的尺寸和分布，形成Volmer-Weber离散生长模式。

Result: Ar-O2混合气氛下制备的涂层在可见光范围内反射率最低，优于裸FTO和其他条件。结构分析确认了γ-Al2O3的形成和碳的掺入，SEM显示了形貌驱动的光学行为。

Conclusion: 研究建立了溅射参数、表面形貌和光学性能之间的明确联系，为下一代太阳能电池和光电器件提供了可扩展的抗反射、光捕获涂层制备方法。

Abstract: Fluorine-doped tin oxide (FTO) is a key transparent conductive oxide for photovoltaic and optoelectronic devices, yet its high reflectance limits light-trapping efficiency. This work demonstrates a simple DC plasma sputtering approach to deposit carbonaceous Al2O3 microdots on FTO under controlled Ar, O2, and Ar-O2 atmospheres. For plasma discharge in the normal mode, with plasma density 10^-9 cm^-3 and temperature of 2 eV, Volmer-Weber growth produced discrete microdots whose size and distribution were tuned by gas composition: dense, uniform dots in Ar (approximately 0.89 um radius), agglomerated structures in O2, and intermediate morphologies in mixed atmospheres. Structural analysis confirmed gamma-Al2O3 formation with carbon incorporation, while SEM revealed morphology-driven optical behavior. UV-Vis measurements showed that Ar-O2 coatings achieved the lowest reflectance across the visible range, outperforming bare FTO and other conditions. These findings establish a clear link between sputtering parameters, surface morphology, and optical performance, offering a scalable route to anti-reflective, light-trapping coatings for next-generation solar cells and optoelectronic devices.
