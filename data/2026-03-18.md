Method: 通过理论推导，建立了考虑Cairns分布（非热分布）的等离子体鞘层模型，推导了相应的物理参数表达式。

We show that these properties of the plasma sheath depend significantly on the a-parameter in the non-thermal a-distribution, and so they are generally different from those of the plasma sheath if the primary electrons were assumed to be a Maxwellian distribution.

Finally, strong and weak scaling tests on Leonardo demonstrate efficiency of $70 \%$ and $78 \%$ up to 64 and 1024 GPUs, respectively.
