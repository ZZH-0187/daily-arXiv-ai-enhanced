### [1] [Critical transverse compressive stresses of straight and bent CORC$^\circledR$ wires with and without impregnation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11923)
*X. Xu,F. Wan,S. Cohan,V. V. Kashikhin*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 评估了CORC®超导导线在横向压缩下的临界性能，分析了弯曲和浸渍处理的影响


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: CORC®导线虽然具有良好的单向拉伸性能，但在加速器磁体中承受的主要应力是横向压缩，可能存在性能退化风险，需要评估其在横向压缩下的临界应力

Method: 开发了液氮环境中的实验系统，测试了五种配置：未浸渍的直导线、未浸渍的弯曲导线、浸渍Stycast 2850 FT的直导线、浸渍Stycast 2850 FT的弯曲导线、浸渍石蜡的弯曲导线，测量了导致临界电流下降3%和5%的横向压力

Result: 获得了不同配置下CORC®导线的横向压缩临界性能数据，分析了导线弯曲和浸渍处理对临界横向压力的影响

Conclusion: 研究结果为评估加速器磁体超导体中的横向应力水平提供了重要参考，揭示了弯曲和浸渍处理对CORC®导线横向压缩性能的影响

Abstract: CORC$^\circledR$ wires are a promising superconductor for accelerator magnet applications. While their excellent uniaxial tensile properties have been well established, potential degradation under transverse compression remains a significant concern for accelerator magnets, in which transverse compression is a primary stress experienced by superconductors. To evaluate the critical transverse compressive stresses of ReBCO conductors, we developed an experimental system that enables testing of samples both with and without impregnation in liquid nitrogen. Furthermore, because bending strain induced during coil winding may influence the critical compressive response of CORC$^\circledR$ wires, the apparatus was also modified to allow testing under the bending condition. In this study tests were conducted for five configurations: (1) straight wires without impregnation, (2) bent wires without impregnation, (3) straight wires with Stycast 2850 FT impregnation, (4) bent wires with Stycast 2850 FT impregnation, and (5) bent wires impregnated with paraffin wax. The transverse pressures corresponding to 3% and 5% reductions in the critical current are reported. The effects of wire bending and impregnation on the critical transverse pressure are analyzed, and the implications for transverse stress levels in accelerator magnet conductors are discussed.

### [2] [Investigations and Considerations of Oxygen Diffusion Profiles in Superconducting Mid-T RF Cavities Utilizing EXAFS Spectroscopy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12964)
*Niels Eckert,Marc Wenskat,Wolfgang Hillert*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 这篇论文通过EXAFS光谱分析三种不同处理的SRF腔体中的氧扩散剖面，但由于噪声影响，定量结果几乎不可用，定性结果则与现有模型一致，未见新的物理解释。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究旨在分析SRF腔体中氧扩散剖面，试图建立微观晶格与腔体性能之间的物理关联，这也是加速器物理学家长期关注的问题。

Method: 在DELTA设施上采用EXAFS光谱测量两种中温烘烤样品和一个标准EuXFEL配方样品中的氧扩散剖面。

Result: 受严重噪声影响，定量结果几乎无法使用；定性分析表明氧扩散剖面与现有模型一致，未发现偏离或反驳先前研究的结果。

Conclusion: 实验分析虽然精确，但受噪声限制；建议未来在PETRA III设施上重复实验，并考虑进行理论误差估计和可能的EXAFS模拟来改进方法。

Abstract: A screening of oxygen profiles in mid-T treated SRF cavities is crucial, in order to infer physical correlations between the microscopic cavity lattice and cavity performance - a problem concerning acceleration physicists for years. This thesis provides an analysis of oxygen diffusion profiles for three differently treated samples: Two mid-T baked and the third with the standard EuXFEL recipe. The measurement method utilizes EXAFS spectroscopy and was carried out at the DELTA facility in Dortmund. The result suffers heavily under noise, making the quantity of the result barely useable. Qualitatively, no deviations of current models regarding the profiles, could be proven, and no results of previous studies were contradicted. The experimental analysis is described in precision, interpretations of the possible are undertaken and theoretical considerations regarding error estimation and possible EXAFS simulations for future attempts are provided. A repetition at the PETRA III facility is indicated.

### [3] [Nonlinear Energy Transfer Analysis in Developing Plasma Turbulence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12523)
*Sandip Das,Lavkesh Lachhvani,Kunal Singha,Rosh Roy,Tanmay Karmakar,Daniel Raju,Prabal Chattopadhyay*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究详细调查了等离子体中通过二次耦合过程进行的非线性波相互作用引起的模式间能量转移，特别关注了瑞利-泰勒模式和漂移波模式之间的相互作用，并探索了两种计算方法在模拟和实验数据中的应用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的线性谱方法无法捕捉等离子体中由非线性相互作用引起的能量转移现象，而IMPED装置测量的等离子体密度波动显示出瑞利-泰勒和漂移波不稳定模态之间的非线性模式相互作用特征，需要深入研究这些模式间的能量转移过程。

Method: 探索了Ritz方法和Kim方法这两种单场湍流模型计算方法，并将它们应用于模拟数据和IMPED实验数据，特别关注数据的统计特性（如峰度）和空间平稳性对方法有效性的影响。

Result: 研究发现，这些方法的有效性和适用性取决于数据的统计性质（特别是高阶矩如峰度）以及空间平稳性。通过在不同径向位置进行能量转移分析，揭示了RT模式向较低频率DW模式转移能量的过程，证明了该方法能够量化等离子体湍流中的谱能量传输。

Conclusion: 该方法能够有效量化等离子体湍流中的非线性能量转移，特别是从RT模式向DW模式的能量转移，为理解等离子体湍流动力学提供了重要分析工具，其应用效果取决于数据本身的统计特性。

Abstract: Energy transfer among various spectral components of fluctuating physical parameters in plasma occurs due to the nonlinear interactions, but these effects are typically not captured by the traditional linear spectral methods. Plasma density fluctuations measured in the Inverse Mirror Plasma Experimental Device (IMPED) have signatures of nonlinear mode interactions among various instability modes, i.e. Rayleigh-Taylor (RT) and Drift-Wave (DW) modes. In this paper, the energy transfer among these modes as a result of nonlinear wave interactions (through the quadratic coupling processes) have been investigated in detail. The existing computational methods for single field turbulence model such as Ritz method and Kim method have been explored to understand the turbulence dynamics. Both methods are applied and validated in simulation as well as experimental data from IMPED for developing plasma turbulence. We find that the validity and applicability of the methods depend on the statistical nature of the data, particularly higher-order moments such as kurtosis, and on spatial stationarity. Energy transfer analysis at different radial locations using these methods reveals the transfer of energy from RT modes to a comparatively low-frequency DW mode, demonstrating the capability of the method to quantify spectral energy transport in the plasma turbulence.

</details>
