### [1] [Plasma morphology under nanosecond repetitively pulsed discharges with air cross-flow: A simulation study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31773)
*Gihun Shim,Praise Noah Johnson,Taaresh Sanjeev Taneja,Suo Yang*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究开发了高精度等离子体-流体耦合模拟框架，系统研究了空气横流中纳秒重复脉冲放电的等离子体形态演化机制，揭示了不同流速下的等离子体保留特性和热积累差异，以及后续放电的"回弹"机制成因。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究动机构要

Method: 该研究在OpenFOAM中开发了高保真等离子体-流体耦合模拟框架，集成了漂移-扩散-对流输运、静电势、光电离、混合平均输运、双极扩散和局部平均电子能量模型等关键物理机制。

Result: 模拟研究显示：较低横流速度（40 m/s）促进等离子体在先前形成的通道内保留，导致显著热积累；而较高横流速度（100 m/s）使等离子体快速对流下游，减少脉冲间保留并减弱热积累。研究进一步揭示了"回弹"机制：先前通道电导率降低后，后续放电通过建立电极间最短的新路径来寻找最小电阻。

Conclusion: 该研究成功开发了综合等离子体-流体耦合模拟框架，系统揭示了空气横流中纳秒重复脉冲放电的形态演化机制，为理解和控制等离子体辅助流动应用提供了重要理论工具和物理洞察。

Abstract: Nanosecond repetitively pulsed discharges in air cross-flows exhibit distinct plasma morphologies that can expand the spatial extent of bulk plasma regions and increase the residence time of reactive radicals within the flow field. This work develops a high-fidelity plasma-fluid simulation framework within OpenFOAM that couples drift-diffusion-convection transport, electrostatic potential, photoionization, mixture-averaged transport, ambipolar diffusion, and a local mean electron energy formulation. Using this framework, we systematically investigate plasma morphologies under air cross-flow velocities of 40 and 100 m/s at a repetition frequency of 50 kHz. The simulations show that lower cross-flow velocities promote plasma retention within previously formed channels, resulting in substantial thermal accumulation. In contrast, at higher cross-flow velocities, plasma is rapidly convected downstream, reducing retention between pulses and weakening thermal accumulation. The simulations further reveal the mechanisms responsible for snap-back, in which a subsequent discharge shifts from a previously formed channel to a shorter and more direct path between the electrodes as the conductivity of the previous channel decreases. As a strong electric field develops between the electrodes, electrons are accelerated and intense Joule heating occurs near the anode tip. This process increases the local electron energy, enhances electron production, and generates a stronger discharge current. As the conductivity of the previous channel decreases, the discharge consequently establishes a new path of least resistance directly between the electrodes.

Motivation: I型边界局部模式（ELMs）对聚变试验电厂构成重大风险，需要寻找无大ELMs且能在高燃料密度下运行的H模式工作区。

Method: 通过实验观察EDA和QCE H模式的访问条件，结合湍流参数分析、剥落-气球模稳定性计算以及BOUT++数值模拟来研究这些模式的物理机制。

Abstract: We review the Enhanced D-alpha (EDA) and Quasi-continuous Exhaust (QCE) H-modes that have recently been achieved in the DIII-D tokamak. These scenarios are free of large type-I edge-localized modes (ELMs) and achieved at high fueling compatible with divertor detachment. This makes them promising operational regimes as ELMs are a major risk for fusion pilot plants (FPPs). BOUT++ simulations qualitatively reproduce the pressure collapse characteristics of the different regimes and suggest an important role of n ~ 50 resistive ballooning modes localized near the separatrix at the X-point during EDA and QCE H-modes. This work provides a foundation and guidance for further research on the access conditions and physics mechanisms of EDA and QCE H-modes necessary for the extrapolation of these regimes towards an FPP.

Abstract: We apply Bayesian inference to the analysis of collective Thomson scattering (CTS) spectra, a diagnostic technique for determining local plasma parameters. In particular, the combined analysis provides a clear constraint on the ion charge state, which is not determined by either separate analysis alone. These findings demonstrate the importance of Bayesian modeling that accounts for the two-component nature of plasmas, consisting of ions and electrons.

### [4] [Plasma transport by carrier electrons: delocalization length for heat-flux](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32085)
*C. A. Walsh*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该论文提出了改进的非局域热流度量：去局域化长度λ_d，包含了电子-电子碰撞对热流载流电子的局域化效应。同时推导了去局域化霍尔参数ψ_d，显示磁化比热导率磁化(ψ_κ)更快。建议使用修正的Knudsen数Kn_d替代传统Knudsen数来统一表示电离度和磁化下的非局域性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统Knudsen数使用电子-离子平均自由程，不能公平地表示跨电离度和磁化的非局域性。需要开发能够考虑电子-电子碰撞局域化效应和磁化影响的改进非局域性度量。

Method: 在全电子-电子碰撞算子和收敛的电子分布谐波展开(达f_6)的线性机制下，统一了三个磁化热流分量的动力学理解。推导了去局域化长度λ_d、去局域化霍尔参数ψ_d，并提出了改进的Knudsen数Kn_d。

Result: 发现Righi-Leduc热流比其垂直分量更容易去局域化，因为其载流子是更高能量的电子。磁化以比热导率磁化更快的速率局域化载流电子。提出的新度量能够更准确地描述电离和磁化条件下的非局域效应。

Conclusion: 该方法为一致处理动力学修正的感应方程奠定了基础，提供了改进的非局域热流度量，能够更好地表征电子-电子碰撞局域化和磁化效应。建议使用Kn_d=8.28λ_{d⊥}/L_T替代传统Knudsen数。

Abstract: The delocalization length $λ_d=λ_{ei} Z/\sqrt{Z+4.45}$ is an improved heat-flux non-locality metric accounting for the localizing effect of electron-electron collisions on the electron population carrying heat-flux. Magnetization also localizes the carrier electrons; the delocalization Hall parameter $ψ_d=13.5 χZ/(Z+4.62)$ shows that this occurs at a faster rate than magnetization of thermal conductivity ($ψ_κ= 9.94χ(Z+0.17)/(Z+5.88)$). The traditional Knudsen number, which uses the electron-ion mean-free path, does not fairly represent non-locality across ionization and magnetization; $Kn_d = 8.28λ_{d\bot}/L_T$ should instead be used. The kinetic understanding of the three magnetized heat-flux components is unified in the linear regime with a full electron-electron collision operator and a converged harmonic expansion of the electron distribution (up to $f_6$). Righi-Leduc heat-flux is found to delocalize easier than its perpendicular counterpart, due to its carriers being higher energy electrons. This approach lays the groundwork for a consistent treatment of the kinetically-modified induction equation.

### [5] [Predicting Negative Triangularity Edge Confinement with a Neutral-Coupled Full-f Gyrokinetic Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32100)

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 负三角位形通过抑制湍流输运改善约束性能的机制研究。研究表明：负三角位形中稳定的带状流动和流体-中性粒子耦合对约束改进起关键作用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究负三角位形(NT)相对正三角位形(PT)改善约束性能的物理机制。DIII-D实验观察到NT配置具有更好的约束性能，需要从第一性原理的动力学模拟角度理解其内在机制，特别是湍流抑制和中性粒子相互作用在其中的作用。

Method: 使用Gkeyll全局全f回旋动力学代码，结合简化中性流体模型，模拟DIII-D内壁限制放电。模拟涵盖约束区边缘、刮削层和鞘边界，允许边缘电场自洽演化。相比自适应再循环源模型，简化中性流体模型能更好地平衡粒子与能量，且计算成本增加较小。

In NT, suppressed turbulent fluxes coincide with a broad, stationary zonal flow inside the last closed flux surface, whereas PT develops transient, fragmented shear layers. Spectral analysis indicates that modified fluctuation phase relationships and enhanced zonal shear both contribute to transport suppression despite broadly comparable fluctuation amplitudes. These results identify kinetic and turbulent mechanisms associated with improved NT confinement within an experimentally established ELM-free regime and demonstrate the importance of coupling gyrokinetic plasma dynamics with neutral interactions across the confined plasma and SOL.
