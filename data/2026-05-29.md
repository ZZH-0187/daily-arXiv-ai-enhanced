### [1] [Propagation of waves in weakly ionized two-fluid plasmas. II. Nonlinear Alfvénic waves](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30057)
*David Martínez-Gómez*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 本文研究弱电离等离子体中Alfvén波的传播及其非线性效应，分析了霍尔电流和离子-中性粒子弹性碰撞对线性和圆偏振横波的联合影响，推导了阻尼率和加热率的解析表达式，并通过数值模拟研究了非线性的密度扰动和体流生成。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 太阳和恒星大气层的弱电离等离子体中存在密度扰动和体流，通常被认为是非线性Alfvén波的ponderomotive力所致。这些波的耗散会导致等离子体加热。本研究旨在探究霍尔电流和离子-中性粒子碰撞对横波传播的非线性效应，特别是对ponderomotive力和能量耗散的影响。

Method: 采用双流体模型，研究了霍尔电流和离子-中性粒子弹性碰撞对线性和圆偏振横波传播的联合影响。推导了阻尼率和加热率的解析表达式，并通过数值模拟研究了ponderomotive力引起的非线性密度扰动和体流，以及离子-中性粒子相互作用导致的能量耗散。

Result: 研究发现：1）圆偏振本征模的非线性扰动不会表现出线性本征模通常引起的振荡运动，但保留了非振荡的体流；2）在弱耦合条件下，中性流体的非线性动力学主要由波能量耗散驱动，而ponderomotive力仅直接作用于带电流体，导致纵向运动与密度、温度扰动的幅度不同。

Conclusion: 霍尔电流和离子-中性粒子弹性碰撞对弱电离等离子体中横波的传播、阻尼和加热有重要影响。圆偏振波与线性偏振波在非线性效应上存在显著差异，特别是在扰动特性上。在弱耦合条件下，带电流体和中性流体的动力学行为受不同机制主导，这对理解太阳和恒星大气中的波动现象具有重要意义。

Abstract: Weakly ionized plasmas can be found in the lower layers of the solar and stellar atmospheres and in structures such as prominences and spicules. A variety of density perturbations and bulk flows detected in these environments have been explained as the result of the ponderomotive force generated by nonlinear Alfvénic waves. In addition, the dissipation of the energy carried by these waves leads to heating of the plasma. Here, we use a two-fluid model to study the combined influence of Hall's current and elastic collisions between ions and neutrals on the propagation of linearly and circularly polarized transverse waves in weakly ionized plasmas. We derive analytical expressions for the damping and heating rates, showing their dependence on the strength of the collisional coupling and on the polarization state. We also perform numerical simulations to investigate the nonlinear generation of density perturbations and bulk flows related to the ponderomotive force and the energy dissipation by the ion-neutral interaction. We find that the nonlinear perturbations associated with the circularly polarized eigenmodes do not show the oscillatory motions typically caused by linearly polarized eigenmodes, but they retain the non-oscillatory bulk flows. We also briefly discuss how in weak coupling conditions the nonlinear dynamics of the neutral fluid is mainly driven by the wave energy dissipation while the ponderomotive force only directly acts on the charged fluid, resulting in different amplitudes of the longitudinal motions and the perturbations of density and temperature.

### [2] [Advanced Externally Seeded FEL Schemes for High-Repetition-Rate Operation at SHINE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30041)
*Hanxiang Yang,Nanshun Huang,Zheng Qi,Tao Liu,Haixiao Deng,Bo Liu*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 本文提出了三种MHz重复频率、高次谐波（超过30阶）的种子自由电子激光方案，基于自调制和直接放大技术，旨在解决目前高重频种子激光器能量调制受限的问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的外种子自由电子激光（FEL）在向更短波长和MHz重复频率扩展时受到高重复频率种子激光器能量调制能力的限制。需要通过技术创新来突破这一瓶颈，实现MHz级别的高次谐波辐射。

Method: 在上海软X射线FEL装置上进行了自放大和直接放大实验，显著降低了高增益谐波产生（HGHG）对峰值功率的要求。提出了三种兼容的高重复频率种子FEL配置：自调制级联HGHG、自调制EEHG和直接放大驱动的EEHG，采用通用的调制器-弯转结构保持各模式的兼容性。

Result: 数值模拟表明，这些方案能够在MHz重复频率下实现超过30阶的谐波产生，为高频重复率操作提供了灵活的技术路径。

Conclusion: 研究提出的三种方案和通用调制器布局为SHINE装置未来的优化和实验实施提供了可行的技术路线，推动了MHz级别、高次谐波种子自由电子激光的发展。

Abstract: Externally seeded free-electron lasers (FELs) are promising approaches for generating fully coherent soft-X-ray radiation. Their extension to shorter wavelengths and MHz-level repetition rates is, however, constrained by the limited availability of high-repetition-rate seed lasers with sufficient energy modulation. Recent self-amplification and direct-amplification experiments at the Shanghai Soft X-ray FEL facility have significantly relaxed the peak-power requirement for high-gain harmonic generation (HGHG) and opened a practical path toward echo-enabled harmonic generation (EEHG). Using the SHINE bypass line, three compatible high-repetition-rate seeded-FEL configurations are explored: self-modulation cascaded HGHG, self-modulation EEHG, and direct-amplification-driven EEHG. Numerical simulations indicate that these schemes can provide flexible routes toward MHz-level operation with harmonic generation beyond the 30th order. A common modulator-chicane layout is proposed to preserve compatibility among the candidate modes and to support future optimization and experimental implementation at SHINE.
