Abstract: Achieving high-quality electron beams from laser wakefield accelerators critically relies on density tailoring to control electron dynamics during injection, acceleration, and extraction. ### [3] [Nitrogen-induced ELM suppression and confinement improvement in the EAST tokamak with a full metal wall](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26403)

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 氮气注入在EAST装置上实现的ELM-free H模态，通过边缘DTEM调节梯度，抑制ELM并提高约束性能


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为了在具有金属壁的未来稳态聚变堆中实现高约束的同时保护等离子体面向组件，需要理解和建立无边缘局域模(ELM)的先进运行方案

Method: 在EAST装置上进行氮气注入实验，利用O模极向相关反射计和AXUV诊断识别边缘相干模(ECM)，结合CGYRO线性回旋动理学模拟分析模的不稳定性

Result: 氮气注入后完全抑制大ELM爆发，H98因子从0.9提升到1.2；在pedestal根部(psi_N≈0.99)发现频率20-50kHz、极向波数k_θ≈0.54cm⁻¹的相干模；模拟确认该模为耗散捕获电子模(DTEM)，通过调控边界的能量和粒子输运维持稳态ELM-free状态

Abstract: This paper reports the achievement of an ELM-free H-mode regime with confinement improvement enabled by nitrogen (N2) seeding on the Experimental Advanced Superconducting Tokamak (EAST) with a full metal wall. Following N2 injection, large Edge-Localized Mode (ELM) bursts are completely suppressed, while global energy confinement is significantly enhanced, with the H98 factor increasing from approximately 0.9 to 1.2. A distinct edge coherent mode (ECM), localized at the pedestal foot (psi_N ~ 0.99), is identified using O-mode Poloidal Correlation Reflectometry and AXUV diagnostics. This mode operates within a frequency range of 20-50 kHz with a poloidal wavenumber of k_theta ~ 0.54 cm^-1. Linear gyrokinetic simulations performed with the CGYRO code reveal a dominant instability that quantitatively matches the experimental measurements. Detailed scans of parameters identify this mode as a Dissipative Trapped Electron Mode (DTEM). The energy and particle transport driven by this pedestal-foot DTEM effectively regulates the edge gradients, preventing the pedestal from crossing the Peeling-Ballooning stability boundary and sustaining a stationary ELM-free state. These findings provide a physical basis for an integrated scenario to maintain high confinement and protect plasma-facing components in future steady-state fusion reactors.

### [4] [Theory of Relativistic Surface Plasmon Excitation on Smooth Surface by High-Intensity Laser](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26705)
*Bifeng Lei,Bin Qiao,Matt Zepf,Guoxing Xia,Carsten Welsh*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究提出了一种相对论表面等离子体激元（RSP）的经典理论，分析其在光滑等离子体-真空界面被强激光脉冲驱动的激发机理，重点探讨几何形状（平面、圆柱面）对激发条件和场增强的影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究相对论表面等离子体激元在强激光-等离子体相互作用中的激发机制，理解表面几何形状如何影响激发条件、模式选择及场增强，为粒子加速和强场物理应用提供理论指导。

Method: 从麦克斯韦方程与冷流体等离子体响应耦合出发，推导受驱RSP波动方程并解析求解；分析平面和圆柱面几何下的波矢守恒、模式选择及驱动场重叠；利用局部相对论介电模型和粒子模拟验证。

Result: 平面界面 enforce 面内波矢守恒；有限纵向相互作用长度或轴向调制提供有限kz谱；圆柱曲率将连续横向波数替换为离散方位角模式m，部分放宽平面约束；圆柱面可 sustain 轴向加速场，实现高非线性尾波场产生；模式选择规则由驱动极化决定。

Conclusion: RSP激发强度由驱动与表面本征场的重叠决定，受表面几何形状控制；相对论效应显著改变介电响应；圆柱几何提供固有模式选择控制，有望用于粒子加速等应用。

Abstract: We present a classical theory of relativistic surface plasmon (RSP) excitation at a smooth plasma-vacuum interface driven by either a ponderomotive force or an electric field of an intense laser pulse. Starting from Maxwell equations coupled to a cold-fluid plasma response, we derive a general driven wave equation for the RSP and solve it analytically. We show that an infinite planar surface enforces conservation of the in-plane wavevector. A finite longitudinal interaction length or axial modulation supplies a finite kz spectrum, while cylindrical curvature replaces one continuous transverse in-plane wavenumber by a discrete azimuthal mode index m. This partially relaxes the planar in-plane constraint, while axial phase matching remains controlled by the longitudinal spectrum of the drive. The excitation strength is controlled by the overlap between the drive and the surface eigenfield, which is determined by the surface geometry. This provides a general principle for controlling RSP excitation. We also show that relativistic effects can substantially modify the dielectric response and can be preliminarily verified by particle-in-cell simulations. Within the local relativistic dielectric model, the overlap-normalised planar source saturates at large a0, and cylindrical curvature partially alleviates this reduction before strong surface softening develops. The role of surface geometry is analysed. A cylindrical surface can sustain an on-axis accelerating field, enabling highly nonlinear wakefield generation for particle acceleration. In addition, the cylindrical geometry imposes a precise mode-selection rule that provides intrinsic control over RSP excitation. Axisymmetric ponderomotive drive selects fundamental mode m=0. A linearly polarised laser field selects a superposition of m=+1 and m=-1 modes, and a circularly polarised laser field selects a single helical mode.

### [5] [The link between coil non planarity and magnetic surface geometry in QI stellarators: a data driven study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26763)
*Andrea Pavone,Felix Warmer*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 等离子体边界表面的主方向旋转率（Twist Rate）是预测线圈非平面性的最佳单一指标。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 理解等离子体边界形状如何决定线圈组复杂度，这对仿星器设计、工程可行性以及下一代聚变电厂建设至关重要。

Method: 使用Constellaration数据集的7500个准等动力（QI）仿星器等离子体边界，通过SIMSOPT进行约束优化计算线圈配置，定义线圈复杂度指标（扭转、SVD非平面性评分、内侧倾角、谱宽）以及表面和磁几何特征集合（第二基本形式、归一化扭曲、主方向旋转率、表面曲率、磁轴特性），对去均值数据进行单变量和多变量统计分析。

Result: 等离子体边界的主方向旋转率（扭曲率）是线圈非平面性的最佳单一预测指标（Spearman ρ = 0.936, R² = 0.700），使用最多四个表面特征的随机森林模型对同一目标的R²达到0.882。

Conclusion: 等离子体边界表面的局部扭曲及其在表面上的变化速率，是准等动力仿星器线圈非平面性的主要驱动因素。

Abstract: Stellarator fusion devices confine plasma by means of complex, non-planar electromagnetic coils. Understanding how the shape of the plasma boundary determines the required complexity of the coil set is a central open question in stellarator design, with direct implications for engineering feasibility and the prospects of building next-generation fusion power plants. In this work we address this question using a large data-driven study. Starting from the Constellaration dataset of 7 500 quasi-isodynamic (QI) stellarator plasma boundaries, we compute a set of coil configurations using constrained optimisation within SIMSOPT, and define quantitative coil-complexity metrics (torsion, SVD non-planarity score, inboard-side inclination angle, spectral width) together with a rich set of surface and magnetic geometry features (second fundamental form, normalised twist, principal-direction rotation rate, surface curvatures, and magnetic axis properties). Univariate and multivariate statistical analyses, performed on demeaned data to remove dataset-level biases, reveal a strong, central role of the surface geometry: the principal-direction rotation rate of the plasma boundary (twist rate) is the single best predictor of coil non-planarity (Spearman \r{ho} = 0.936, R2 = 0.700), while a Random Forest model using up to four surface features achieves R2 = 0.882 for the same target. These results provide quantitative evidence that the local twist of the boundary surface, and how rapidly it changes across the surface, are the primary drivers of coil non-planarity in quasi-isodynamic stellarators.

</details>
