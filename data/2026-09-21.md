### [1] [Mechanism of Ionization Avalanche in Tokamak Microwave Gas Breakdown](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21429)
*Jinwoo Gwak,Yeongsun Lee,Jeongwon Lee,Won Ik Jeong,Hyun-Tae Kim,Yong-Seok Hwang,Min-Gu Yoo,Yong-Su Na*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该论文开发了一种三维蒙特卡洛模拟来研究电子回旋波驱动的微波击穿，发现平行布朗运动在电离雪崩机制中起关键作用，预测ITER条件下2 mPa D₂预充气压和1 MW EC功率可实现成功击穿。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在反应堆规模托卡马克中，电子回旋波驱动的微波击穿为非感应等离子体启动提供了一条重要路径。然而，传统的建模方法无法自洽处理非线性波粒相互作用、原子碰撞和导向中心输运等复杂物理过程，因此需要开发新的模拟方法来准确预测击穿边界和机制。

Method: 引入了三维蒙特卡洛模拟方法，首次自洽地处理了非线性波粒相互作用、原子碰撞和导向中心输运。该方法能够揭示微波击穿过程中的关键物理机制，特别是平行布朗运动在电离雪崩中的作用。

Result: 模拟揭示了平行布朗运动在电离雪崩机制中的关键作用。预测的击穿边界与KSTAR实验结果相验证。研究得出结论：在ITER相关条件下，使用2 mPa的D₂预充气压和1 MW的注入EC功率，微波气体击穿将能够成功实现。

Conclusion: 这项工作证明了三维蒙特卡洛模拟能够准确描述微波击穿物理，为反应堆规模托卡马克的非感应等离子体启动提供了可靠的理论预测。在ITER条件下，采用适宜的预充气压和EC功率，微波击穿是可行的，这将为聚变反应堆的启动提供重要技术支撑。

Abstract: Microwave breakdown driven by electron cyclotron (EC) waves provides a non-inductive route to plasma initiation in reactor-scale tokamaks. We introduce a three-dimensional Monte Carlo simulation that, for the first time, self-consistently treats nonlinear wave-particle interactions, atomic collisions, and guiding-center transport. The Monte Carlo simulation unveils the key role of parallel Brownian motion in the ionization avalanche mechanism. The predicted breakdown boundary is validated against KSTAR experiments. This work concludes that microwave gas breakdown will be successful under ITER-relevant conditions at a D$_2$ prefill pressure near 2 mPa with 1 MW of injected EC power.

### [2] [Azimuthal mode decomposition Particle in Cell algorithm for cylindrical plasma sources](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21951)
*Matteo Ripoli,Eduardo Ahedo,Mario Merino*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 本文提出一种高效的粒子网格(PIC)数值方法，通过对场进行傅里叶模态分解，将圆柱几何中的D维模拟简化为耦合的(D-1)维问题，实现方位角方向的光谱精度和显著计算加速。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 针对低温等离子体模拟中传统全维粒子网格方法计算成本高昂的问题，本文旨在开发一种既能保持模拟精度又能大幅减少计算消耗的高效数值方法。

Method: 采用傅里叶模态分解方法：在圆柱几何中，对方位角θ方向的场进行模态分解直至选定最大模态数Nm；宏粒子在所有D维中推进，并为每个模态m在(D-1)维网格上进行权重分配；每个模态的电场计算独立进行，简化为求解(Nm+1)个(D-1)维泊松问题。

Result: 该方法在方位角方向实现光谱精度，计算成本与(D-1)维模拟相当；通过基于Penning放电的平面测试案例验证，可将2D问题转化为耦合的1D问题集合，实现光谱分析各方位角模态对径向输运的贡献，相比现有2D粒子网格代码计算时间节省约一个数量级。

Conclusion: 所提出的高效粒子网格数值方法能够对低温等离子体进行全维动力学模拟，在保持方位角方向光谱精度的同时，大幅降低计算成本，为等离子体输运机制的分析提供了新的工具。

Abstract: An efficient Particle-in-Cell numerical approach to perform full-dimensional kinetic simulations of low temperature plasmas is presented. Taking advantage of the cylindrical geometry of most plasma sources, a Fourier mode decomposition of the fields is carried out in the azimuthal ($θ$) direction up to a chosen maximum number of modes $N_m$. Macroparticles are pushed in all $D$ dimensions and weighed, for each mode $m$, onto a $(D-1)$ dimensional grid. The computation of the electric field for each mode is independent and reduces to solving $(N_m+1)$ $(D-1)$ dimensional Poisson problems. The approach brings spectral accuracy in the azimuthal direction, while the computational cost is comparable to that of a simulation with $(D-1)$ dimensions. We verify this approach against a planar test case based on a Penning discharge, widely used for benchmarking and validation purposes in the low-temperature plasma community. Our approach allows us to reduce the 2D problem into a collection of coupled 1D problems and to naturally perform spectral analysis of the different azimuthal modes, recovering the contribution of each mode to radial transport, with a computational time saving of one order of magnitude with respect to state of the art 2D particle-in-cell codes.

### [3] [Approximate solution of an adapted Bethe transport equation for electron scattering in foils](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21634)
*J. M. Corstens*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 提出了用于箔中电子散射的新型半解析模型，适用于厚度至20个无量纲厚度、电子束动能高达100 keV的薄箔；通过Hankel变换求解改进的Bethe输运方程，并与现有模型比较验证了在20-100 keV范围内的优越性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有电子散射模型（如Kawrakow模型采用小角度近似）在20-100 keV能区存在局限性，尤其对薄箔样品，需要发展更精确的半解析模型来准确预测电子束的角分布和能量损失效应。

Method: 通过Hankel变换求解经过Wentzel微分截面修改的Bethe输运方程；采用解析求解或近似处理积分；与Goudsmit-Saunderson解和Kawrakow模型（小角度近似）进行对比验证。

Result: 模型在20-100 keV能区内表现优于Kawrakow模型，但在高于100 keV时情况相反；能够模拟角分布束流和能量损失效应；验证了无量纲厚度λ=5,10,20时在金薄样品中的准确性。

Conclusion: 该半解析模型为20-100 keV能区的薄箔电子散射提供了有效工具，特别适用于需要考虑角分布和能量损失的应用场景，填补了该能区现有模型的不足。

Abstract: A new semi-analytical model for electron scattering in foils is presented valid for thin foils and electron-beam kinetic energies up to roughly $100$ keV and from 20 keV at a dimensionless foil thickness of $20$, and higher energies for thinner foils. To perform calculations, an approximate solution is constructed by Hankel transforms of an adapted Bethe transport equation for electron scattering. The Wentzel differential cross section is used in the Bethe transport Eq. and resulting integrals are solved analytically or approximated. These approximate solutions of the transport Eq.'s were compared to the Goudsmit-Saunderson solutions in gold, for thin samples with dimensionless thicknesses $λ=5,10,20$. We also compare to the Kawrakow \cite{Kawr} model, which uses the small angle approximation. We show that in the regime of $20$ to $100$ keV beam energies, our model performs better than Kawrakow's model and this is opposite above about 100 keV. Exactly, some angularly distributed beams and the influence of energy loss of the beam can be simulated with our model.

### [4] [Moderator Modeling for High Intensity Slow Positron Sources](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22036)
*Sophie Crisp,Ryland Goldman,Spencer Gessner*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 该研究通过蒙特卡洛模拟和扩散模型，探索如何通过改变慢化体的几何结构、材料和入射快正电子能量来提高线性加速器基慢正电子束的产生效率，发现低于300keV时固体氖比钨更高效，在MeV尺度时多箔钨结构更优，结合减速腔和低温慢化体可显著提升整体效率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 线性加速器基慢正电子源受限于源强度和亮度。传统设计中，钨慢化体因受热导致缺陷相关损失，低温慢化体（如固体氖）在暴露于高功率快正电子时会熔化。需要更有效地从快正电子产生慢正电子。

Method: 采用蒙特卡洛模拟结合扩散模型，研究慢化体几何结构、材料和入射快正电子能量对慢正电子产额的影响。比较单钨箔和多箔结构配置与固体氖慢化体在反射和透射几何结构中的性能。

Result: 快正电子能量低于300keV时，固体氖因其更大的扩散长度，比钨的效率高一个数量级；在MeV尺度能量下，多箔钨结构通过最大化散射获得更高效率。在模拟中包含靶代用品可使低能量效率提高四倍，因为部分初始反射的正电子会返回慢化体。

Conclusion: 线性加速器源要实现从开始到结束的显著效率提升，可能需要将减速腔与解耦的低温慢化体相结合。

Abstract: Slow positron beams enable diverse applications, from surface-sensitive materials studies to positronium physics, but progress is limited by source intensity and brightness. In linac-based sources, mono-energetic, slow positrons are produced by moderating the broad, divergent distribution of fast positrons produced by high-energy electrons incident on a high-Z target. This process is intrinsically inefficient. Conventional linac-based designs place the moderator close to the target. Tungsten moderators become less efficient when heated by high-power, fast positrons, leading to defect-related losses. Cryogenic moderators, like solid neon, melt when exposed to high-power, fast positrons. Following the method of O'Rourke et al., we use Monte Carlo simulations combined with a diffusion model to investigate how moderator geometry, material, and incident fast positron energy affect slow positron production. We compare single tungsten foil and multi-foil configurations with solid neon moderators in reflection and transmission geometries. We find that for fast positron energies below 300 keV, solid neon offers order-of-magnitude higher efficiency than tungsten due to its larger diffusion length, whereas at MeV-scale energies multi-foil tungsten has higher efficiency due to the maximization of scattering. We also find that including a target proxy in simulation increases low-energy efficiency up to fourfold by allowing a fraction of initially reflected positrons to return to the moderator. We conclude that substantial gains in start-to-end efficiency at linac sources will likely require combining a decelerating cavity with a decoupled cryogenic moderator.

</details>
