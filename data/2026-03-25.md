### [1] [Statistical Flux Freezing with Magnetic Path-lines in Turbulence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22526)
*Amir Jafari*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 统计磁冻结理论：在粗糙磁湍流场中，经典确定性磁冻结失效，需基于磁路径线的概率表述


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 经典磁冻结定理基于速度场和磁场足够光滑的假设，而湍流等离子体中这些场具有空间粗糙性，使传统定理失效。先前研究关注瞬时磁场线，但磁场线缺乏自然的时间演化和身份保持。需要寻找更本质的动力学轨迹来建立湍流中的磁输运理论。

Method: 提出使用磁路径线（magnetic path lines）代替传统磁场线。磁路径线即使在理想极限下也保持内禀随机性，允许通过向后平流的路径线表面上的统计平均来守恒磁通量。这种基于磁路径线的方法提供了时空中的时间演化动力学轨迹，并保留了身份连续性。

Result: 建立了统计形态的Alfvén磁冻结定理，证明了在足够粗糙的湍流磁场中，经典的确定性磁冻结形式无法成立。与先前基于磁场线的随机磁冻结方法相比，路径线方法提供了更简单、更透明的随机磁输运分析框架。

Conclusion: 磁路径线是描述湍流等离子体中随机磁输运的更合适概念框架，它自然地导致了统计意义的磁冻结定理，揭示了经典确定性磁冻结在粗糙湍流场中的局限性。

Abstract: Magnetic flux freezing states that, in ideal magnetohydrodynamics, magnetic flux is transported by the flow and magnetic field lines remain frozen into the plasma. In turbulent plasmas, however, the velocity and magnetic fields are spatially rough, invalidating the regularity assumptions underlying the classical theorem. Previous work has shown that Lagrangian trajectories in such rough flows can become nonunique in the limit of small magnetic diffusivity, leading to stochastic formulations of magnetic flux freezing based on magnetic field lines. Field lines, however, are instantaneous geometric objects that do not possess a natural time evolution and do not preserve identity in turbulent flows. We instead use magnetic path lines, which remain intrinsically stochastic in the ideal limit, implying that magnetic flux is conserved only in a statistical sense over ensembles of backward-advected path-line surfaces. This yields a statistical formulation of Alfven's theorem in terms of magnetic path lines and shows that the classical deterministic form of flux freezing cannot hold in sufficiently rough turbulent magnetic fields. While closely related in physical content to earlier stochastic flux-freezing approaches based on magnetic field lines, the path-line formulation provides time-evolving dynamical trajectories that retain identity in spacetime and offer a simpler and more transparent framework for analyzing stochastic magnetic transport.

### [2] [Wave-particle equilibria with heavy ions in weakly collisional space plasmas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22613)
*Nicolás Villarroel-Sepúlveda,Daniel Verscharen,Pablo S. Moya,Rodrigo A. López,Kristopher G. Klein*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 研究波粒相互作用如何塑造次要离子的速度分布函数，以及这些离子的统计特性如何影响电磁波的色散关系。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 空间等离子体是弱碰撞的，其特征时间尺度远大于拉莫尔回旋或波粒相互作用的时间尺度。因此，波活动很可能驱动了观测到的空间等离子体速度分布中的非热特征，如温度各向异性、束流和偏度。研究次要离子与波动之间的相互作用对于理解等离子体动力学和电磁波行为具有重要意义。

Method: 使用Boris算法推导重离子在电磁波中的运动，将波视为具有太阳风参数的两离子成分等离子体中电磁波的完全动力学色散关系的解。使用ALPS代码基于离子实际分布的线性Vlasov-Maxwell色散关系。测试粒子初始处于热平衡状态，其分布通过与波相互作用而演化。通过使用演化后的分布求解色散关系来研究系统行为。

Result: 研究结果表明，系统会演化到一个稳定的波粒平衡状态，这种状态的特征是波与粒子之间的相互作用和能量传输达到最小化。

Conclusion: 这项工作证明了波粒相互作用在塑造次要离子速度分布中的关键作用，并展示了如何通过使用演化分布求解色散关系来分析系统的动态平衡。波粒平衡态的实现对于理解空间等离子体中能量传输和分布演化的基本机制提供了重要见解。

Abstract: Space plasmas are weakly collisional since characteristic time scales related to Coulomb collisions are much larger than those of Larmor gyration or wave--particle interactions. Thus, wave activity is likely to drive some of the non-thermal features that are observed in space plasma velocity distributions, such as temperature anisotropy, beams, and skewness. Therefore, we study how wave--particle interactions shape the velocity distribution functions of minor ions, and how these ions and their statistical properties modify the dispersion relation of electromagnetic waves. To achieve this, we derive the motion of heavy ions in electromagnetic waves using the Boris algorithm. We take the waves to be solutions of the fully kinetic dispersion relation of electromagnetic waves in two-ion component plasmas with parameters representative of the solar wind. We use the Arbitrary Linear Plasma Solver (ALPS) code to derive the linear Vlasov--Maxwell dispersion relation based on the actual distribution of the ions. The test-particles are initially in thermal equilibrium, and their distribution evolves due to interactions with the waves. By solving the dispersion relation using the evolved distributions, we show that the system evolves into a steady wave--particle equilibrium, which is characterized by a minimization of the interaction and energy transfer between wave and particles.

### [4] [Anisotropic truncation for turbulent transport in the Hasegawa-Wakatani system](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22995)

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究通过限制极向波数（保持径向分辨率）构建降阶模型的可行性，探究需要多少极向模式才能准确捕捉湍流粒子通量和带状流主导状态的转变，并分析不同尺度在级联过程中的作用机制。

Method: 开发基于各向异性截断傅立叶空间的降阶模型，在固定梯度模型和通量驱动模型两种设置下与直接数值模拟对比。以湍流粒子通量和湍流向带状流主导状态的转变为主要验证标准。分析不同极向尺度在湍流级联中的能量传输规律。

Result: 为合理再现DNS结果，固定梯度模型需要至少4个围绕最不稳定模式的极向模；通量驱动模型需要约10个模式才能准确恢复粒子通量的概率分布函数。在湍流态中，大极向尺度发生径向波数的逆能量级联，小极向尺度发生径向波数的正向涡旋级联。带状流形成时，从注入尺度附近获取能量并传递给更大极向尺度，呈现各向异性能量逆传递机制。

Conclusion: 各向异性截断的降阶模型能有效模拟Hasegawa-Wakatani系统的湍流和带状流行为，但对极向模式数量有要求：至少4个模式可捕捉主要特征，约10个模式能更完整恢复统计特性。揭示了带状流作为能量传输通道的角色——从注入尺度附近吸收能量并输运至更大尺度，而正向涡旋级联则保持各向同性特征。

Abstract: Reduced models based on an anisotropic truncation of the Fourier space, retaining only a few poloidal wave-numbers while keeping the full radial resolution, are developed and applied to the Hasegawa-Wakatani system. The turbulent particle flux, and the transition from the quasi-two dimensional turbulence to the zonal flow (ZF) dominated state, are used as the main criteria for validation. Then, similar reduced models are developed in a flux-driven formulation and compared to the DNS, focusing on two cases far from the non-linear threshold of the transition from turbulence to zonal dominated states of the fixed gradient formulation. In both fixed gradient and flux driven cases, it is found that at least 4 poloidal modes, distributed around the most unstable mode, are needed to reproduce the DNS results reasonably. Moreover, when they form, ZFs feed on poloidal scales that are around and slightly smaller than the injection scale, while giving their energy to the larger poloidal scales. In that case, there is an anisotropic inverse energy transfer, akin to inverse cascade, from the energy injection to the large poloidal scales through ZFs, while the forward enstrophy cascade seems to stay isotropic.
