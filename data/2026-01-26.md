### [1] [Improved Kelbg Potentials for $Z>1$ and Application to Carbon Plasmas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16794)
*Heather D. Whitley,Michael S. Murillo,John I. Castor,Liam G. Stanton,Lorin X. Benedict,Philip A. Sterne,James N. Glosli,Frank R. Graziani*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究推导了电子-离子衍射势的一般形式，将其拟合到改进的Kelbg势，并通过分子动力学模拟计算碳等离子体的内能和压力，与路径积分蒙特卡罗和密度泛函理论结果进行对比，验证了该势在温稠密物质和高能量密度等离子体状态方程研究中的适用性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为了在温稠密物质和高能量密度等离子体研究中开发准确的电子-离子相互作用势，需要验证改进的Kelbg势在包含压力电离效应时对于重元素（如碳）的适用性，并与氢元素的研究结果进行比较，以确定该势的普适性和局限性。

Method: 1. 从量子对密度矩阵推导电子-离子衍射势的一般形式；2. 将衍射势拟合到改进的Kelbg势（原子序数Z≤54）；3. 应用经典分子动力学结合改进的Kelbg势和各种形式的泡利势计算碳等离子体的内能和压力；4. 与路径积分蒙特卡罗和密度泛函理论的状态方程模型结果进行对比验证。

Result: 1. 改进的Kelbg势能够较好地再现碳等离子体的内能和压力；2. 考虑压力电离效应后，碳等离子体的适用区域与先前氢元素的研究结果基本一致；3. 验证了该势在温稠密物质和高能量密度等离子体状态方程研究中的有效性。

Conclusion: 改进的Kelbg势在考虑压力电离效应后，对于碳和氢等离子体都具有较好的适用性，能够用于温稠密物质和高能量密度等离子体的状态方程研究，但使用时需要注意其适用范围和局限性。

Abstract: In this work, we present a general form for the electron-ion diffractive potential derived from the quantum pair density matrix and fit to the improved Kelbg potential for atomic numbers up to $Z = 54$. We apply classical molecular dynamics using the improved Kelbg potential for carbon with various forms of the Pauli potential to compute internal energies and pressures for hot, dense plasma conditions. Our results are compared to an equation of state model based on path integral Monte Carlo and density functional theory simulations to examine the extent to which the improved Kelbg potential reproduces the internal energy and pressure of carbon plasmas. The regions of validity for carbon agree generally with those derived previously for hydrogen once pressure ionization effects are incorporated. Based on our carbon results and previously published hydrogen studies, we discuss the general applicability and limitations of these potentials for equation of state studies in warm dense matter and high energy density plasmas.

### [2] [RF Applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16760)
*Thomas Geoffrey Lucas*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 本章介绍了加速器中射频系统的基本原理，重点讨论射频腔体设计需要多学科协作，以瑞士自由电子激光器(SwissFEL)为例说明


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 射频系统在粒子加速器中至关重要，但设计工作需要射频工程师、束流物理学家和机械工程师的跨学科合作，缺乏对相关原理和协作方式的系统介绍。本章旨在填补这一空白。

Method: 从基本原理出发，重点阐述射频腔体的设计原则，强调不同专业领域间的协作模式，并以瑞士Paul Scherrer研究所的SwissFEL X射线自由电子激光器作为具体案例分析。

Result: 构建了加速器射频系统的完整知识框架，明确了各专业角色在设计中的分工与协作关系，通过SwissFEL实例证明了多学科协作对实现高性能射频系统的重要性。

Conclusion: 成功的射频系统设计本质上是跨学科的工程挑战，需要集成电磁学、束流动力学和机械工程等多领域知识，SwissFEL案例为此提供了实用范例，强调协作在加速器技术发展中的核心地位。

Abstract: Radiofrequency (RF) systems play a critical role in particle accelerators by enabling the acceleration, manipulation, and diagnosis of charged particle beams. At the heart of many of these systems lies the RF cavity, whose effective design requires close collaboration among RF designers, beam physicists, and mechanical engineers. This chapter presents the fundamental principles of RF systems, with particular emphasis on RF cavities, and underscores the interdisciplinary effort involved in their development. The SwissFEL X-ray free-electron laser at the Paul Scherrer Institut serves as a key example to illustrate these concepts.
