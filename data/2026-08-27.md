TL;DR: 本文提出了一种针对10-moment MHD方程组的能量守恒有限差分格式，能够在宽范围的等离子体β值（10^{-10}到10^{10}}）下工作，通过非线性滤波和能量转移机制保持总能量守恒。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统CGL MHD模型在大压力各向异性或高等离子体β下会失去双曲性，难以开发近似黎曼求解器；而10-moment MHD方程的特征模分析也很困难，同样阻碍了低耗散黎曼求解器的发展。需要一种能在宽参数范围内稳定工作的数值方法。

Method: 将传统MHD的能量守恒有限差分方法扩展到10-moment MHD模型。对所有六个独立压力张量分量应用非线性滤波，并将滤波耗散的动能和磁能明确转移到对角压力分量中（基于等分假设），以保持总能量平衡的一致性。

Result: 通过七个测试问题验证了方案：在各项同性极限、回旋极限和无各项同性/回旋化情况下的测试。结果显示方案具有预期的空间收敛性和总能量行为，能够重现线性增长率，并产生与理论预期和先前模拟定性一致的压力张量结构。

Conclusion: 该方案为在广泛分离的等离子体β范围内进行碰撞等离子体的大规模模拟提供了一个有前景的框架，并为太阳风湍流和磁重联等应用开辟了道路。

Abstract: Pressure anisotropy and off-diagonal pressure stresses are ubiquitous and play important roles in collisionless/weakly collisional plasmas. The Chew-Goldberger-Low (CGL) MHD model is often used; however, it can lose hyperbolicity when the pressure anisotropy or plasma beta becomes large, making it hard to develop approximate Riemann solvers. An alternative approach is to use the 10-moment MHD equations, but their eigenmode analysis is also difficult, which similarly hinders the development of less-diffusive Riemann solvers. This paper presents a new energy-consistent finite difference scheme for 10-moment MHD designed to operate over a broad range of plasma beta. The proposed scheme extends the 10-moment MHD model using the energy-consistent finite-difference approach developed for conventional MHD. Nonlinear filtering is applied to all six independent components of the pressure tensor, and the kinetic and magnetic energies dissipated by the filtering are explicitly transferred to the diagonal pressure components under an equipartition assumption to maintain consistency with the total energy balance. The proposed scheme is validated against seven test problems in the isotropic limit, the gyrotropic limit, and without isotropization/gyrotropization. The results demonstrate the expected spatial convergence and total energy behavior, reproduce the linear growth rate, and yield pressure tensor structures qualitatively consistent with theoretical expectations and previous simulations, spanning plasma beta values from $10^{-10}$ to $10^{10}$. The proposed scheme provides a promising framework for large-scale simulations of collisionless plasmas across widely separated plasma beta regimes and opens a path toward applications such as solar wind turbulence and plasmoid-mediated reconnection.
