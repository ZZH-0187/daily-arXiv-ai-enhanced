*Anjor Kanekar*

Abstract: We present GANDALF, a JAX-based spectral solver for Kinetic Reduced MHD (KRMHD) turbulence designed to lower infrastructure barriers to plasma turbulence research. Existing production codes require specialized HPC infrastructure and compilation expertise, limiting participation to well-resourced institutions. GANDALF addresses this barrier by leveraging JAX's hardware abstraction to run transparently on laptops, desktop GPUs, and Apple Silicon without modification, enabling single-command installation via pip. We employ Fourier spectral methods for spatial discretization and Hermite spectral basis for velocity space, combined with an exponential integrating factor method that exactly propagates linear Alfvén waves, eliminating associated numerical stiffness. Verification demonstrates research-grade accuracy: linear Alfvén waves achieve machine precision (~10^{-15} relative error), the Orszag-Tang vortex conserves energy to 10^{-6} over two Alfvén times, and driven turbulence reproduces the expected k_perp^{-5/3} cascade spectrum. GANDALF enables rapid prototyping, parameter surveys, and educational applications on commodity hardware. The code complements rather than replaces established solvers like AstroGK and Viriato, prioritizing accessibility for researchers without HPC resources. By removing infrastructure barriers while maintaining spectral accuracy, GANDALF broadens participation in fundamental plasma turbulence research, particularly benefiting students, small research groups, and institutions in developing regions.

### [2] [Thermodynamically Consistent Vibrational-Electron Heating: Generalized Derivation for Excited State Populations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21944)
*Bernard Parent,Felipe Martin Rodriguez Fuentes*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 摘要分析了电子温度预测的重要性，提出了振动-电子(V-e)加热的热力学一致模型的推广，移除了对基态分子碰撞的依赖假设，使模型在振动激发态对电子冷却有显著贡献的等离子体流中仍然有效。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 准确预测非平衡等离子体流中的电子温度在多个应用领域都至关重要，包括高超音速飞行和等离子体辅助燃烧。在之前的模型中，假设电子能量损失主要来自与基态分子的碰撞，这在振动激发态对电子冷却有显著贡献的等离子体流中是不够准确的。

Method: 本研究对之前提出的热力学一致的振动-电子(V-e)加热模型进行了严格推广。虽然原模型假设电子能量损失由基态分子碰撞主导，但新推导证明加热-冷却比$\exp(\theta_{\rm v}/T_{\rm e}-\theta_{\rm v}/T_{\rm v})$（其中$\theta_{\rm v}$是特征振动温度）在包括电子与振动激发态相互作用时仍然有效。

Result: 结果展示，新的推广模型移除了之前对基态主导的约束，从而将模型的有效性扩展到振动激发态对电子冷却有显著贡献的等离子体流中。

Conclusion: 通过严格推广，振动-电子(V-e)加热模型的适用范围更广，能更准确地预测在多种等离子体流中的电子温度，这对相关应用具有重要意义。

Abstract: Accurate prediction of electron temperature ($T_{\rm e}$) in non-equilibrium plasma flows is critical for applications ranging from hypersonic flight to plasma-assisted combustion. We recently proposed a thermodynamically consistent model for vibrational-electron (V-e) heating [Phys. Fluids 37, 096141 (2025)] which enforces convergence of $T_{\rm e}$ to the vibrational temperature ($T_{\rm v}$) at equilibrium. While the original derivation assumed electron energy loss was dominated by collisions with ground-state molecules, this Letter presents a rigorous generalization of the model. We demonstrate that the heating-to-cooling ratio $\exp(θ_{\rm v}/T_{\rm e}-θ_{\rm v}/T_{\rm v})$ with $θ_{\rm v}$ the characteristic vibrational temperature remains valid even when electron cooling interactions with vibrationally excited states are included. This derivation removes the previous constraint assuming ground-state dominance, thereby extending the model's validity to plasma flows where vibrationally excited populations contribute significantly to electron cooling.

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 旨在了解对称性破缺扰动如何影响剪切阿尔芬波的频谱特性，特别是当扰动导致磁通表面破坏时的变化规律

Result: 随扰动强度增加，磁通表面不断被破坏，剪切阿尔芬波频谱发生变化；对于特定磁通表面，在表面完整时波解相对不变，即使在表面破坏后的大扰动下仍保留原始特征

Abstract: The shear Alfvén spectrum is computed in the presence of symmetry breaking perturbations that introduce chaotic magnetic field trajectories. Quadratic flux minimised surfaces allow the creation of pseudo straight field line coordinates in the chaotic region. With these coordinates, the reduced ideal MHD equations are cast into an eigenvalue problem and solved numerically. The spectrum is computed with varying perturbation strength, showing how shear Alfvén waves change as increasing number of flux surfaces are destroyed. Solutions on specific flux surfaces are shown to remain relatively unchanged while the flux surface remains intact, and retain some original features at large perturbations where the flux surface is destroyed.

### [4] [Solving the six-dimensional Vlasov-Maxwell System with Active Flux and Splitting Methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.22440)

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 发展一种高效的数值方法来解决高维Vlasov方程的模拟问题，同时克服传统有限体积法的高计算复杂度和数值耗散大的缺点。

Method: 采用算子分裂技术将高维问题简化为一系列一维平流方程，结合Active Flux方法在胞界面上构造附加自由度来计算高阶数值通量，形成紧凑格式。

Result: 与基于半拉格朗日方法的有限体积法相比，AF方法具有显著更低的数值耗散和减少的各向异性，在标准测试案例中表现相当甚至更优，且计算成本更低。
