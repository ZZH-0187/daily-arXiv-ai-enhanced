Motivation: 托卡马克装置中环向场线圈和中央螺线管的尺寸设计是聚变反应堆工程的关键挑战。随着对高场设计空间的探索（峰值磁场高达20 T），径向结构成为主要约束，在基础配置下超过20 T时无法找到可行的设计。因此，需要开发分析模型并探索替代策略，以实现紧凑、高效的DEMO级聚变电站。

Method: 研究建立了两种分析模型：1）教学用的薄圆筒模型；2）精化的厚圆筒和绕组包模型。精化模型与六个参考装置以及MADE磁体设计代码进行了验证，并用于探索高场设计空间（面向DEMO级聚变电站：聚变功率2 GW、Q=40、平台时间2小时），将TF线圈导体的峰值磁场推至Bmax=20 T。研究识别了主要的技术杠杆（高强度钢、替代机械架构、降低CS磁通需求）和次要优化措施（导体形状、径向分级）。

Result: 1）精化模型与参考装置和MADE代码吻合良好；2）在基础楔形/316L配置下，当TF导体峰值磁场超过20 T时，径向结构成为主导约束，无法找到可行设计；3）识别了三种主要技术杠杆（高强度钢、替代机械架构、降低CS磁通需求），每种对最小可行主半径的影响量级相当；4）次要优化措施（导体形状、径向分级）能提供额外但较有限的增益；5）结合所有有利杠杆后，紧凑型装置（R0 < 4 m）具备可行性。

Conclusion: 综合分析表明，在结合高强度钢（如CHSN01）、替代机械结构（如支撑式或插入式）以及降低有效中央螺线管磁通需求等有利杠杆后，紧凑型托卡马克装置（主半径R0 < 4 m）在技术上具有可行性。尽管需要承担结合多种新方法所带来的风险，但高温超导体的应用为紧凑型发电托卡马克的实现开辟了潜在路径。

Abstract: Two analytical models for sizing the toroidal field coils and central solenoid of a tokamak are developed within the D0FUS system code: a pedagogical thin-cylinder model and a refined thick-cylinder and winding packs model. The refined model shows good agreement with six reference machines and the MADE magnet design code. When the high-field design space is explored for DEMO-class power plants (2 GW of fusion power, Q = 40, tplateau = 2 h), pushing the peak field at the Toroidal Field (TF) coil conductor up to Bmax = 20 T, the radial build emerges as the dominant constraint: in fact, in the baseline wedging/316L configuration, no viable design can be found beyond 20 T, making alternative strategies necessary. The primary levers identified are high-strength steels (e.g CHSN01), alternative mechanical architectures (bucking, plug), and reductions of the effective Central Solenoid (CS) flux demand (for example through auxiliary heating during ramp-up), each carrying an impact of the same order of magnitude on the minimum feasible major radius. Secondary optimisations (conductor shape, radial grading) are shown to provide additional but more modest gains. When all favourable levers are combined (CHSN01, bucking, etc.), compact machines (R0 < 4 m) appear feasible. This suggests that, provided one accepts the associated risks of combining new approaches (CHSN01, bucking, etc.), high-temperature superconductors could unlock the path to compact electricity generating tokamaks.
