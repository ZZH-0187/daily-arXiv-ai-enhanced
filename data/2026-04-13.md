### [1] [Statistical equilibrium model for stellarators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08878)
*Maximilian Ruth,Joshua W. Burby,Wrick Sengupta,Andrew Brown*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于统计模型的新型等离子体平衡原理，通过考虑磁场的快速涨落来解决传统MHD平衡模型在无对称性环面中的奇异性问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统三维无对称性环面磁约束聚变的MHD平衡模型通常无法支持光滑解，存在共振磁面上的奇异电流片，违反了长度尺度分离假设，导致数值近似在细化时不收敛。需要一种能克服此限制的新平衡原理。

Method: 基于等离子体涨落的统计模型，假设磁场相对于MHD时间尺度具有快速的遍历性涨落。通过对产生的力取平均，推导出依赖于涨落方差统计平均磁场的变分平衡问题。通过渐近分析、数值模拟和Grad-Shafranov类型论证，验证该变分原理对特定涨落统计量支持光滑解。

Result: 所提模型通过选择对标准平衡建模范式最小修改的涨落统计量，可以平滑奇异电流片，平滑长度尺度由磁场涨落决定。这为三维无对称性环面中的等离子体平衡提供了新解决方案。

Conclusion: 统计涨落平衡模型能够克服传统MHD平衡模型在无对称性环面中的奇异性问题，支持光滑解，为聚变装置设计提供了新的理论基础。

Abstract: In three dimensional toroidal domains without symmetry, the standard magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) equilibrium model used for magnetic confinement fusion does not generally support smooth solutions. Instead, solutions have singular plasma currents on resonant magnetic surfaces that violate the MHD assumption of length-scale separation, further leading to the non- or slow convergence of numerical approximations under refinement. In this work, we present an improved equilibrium principle derived from a statistical model for plasma fluctuations. Instead of being static, we assume that the plasma magnetic field is ergodically and rapidly fluctuating relative to the MHD time scale. By averaging the resulting force, we derive a variational equilibrium problem for the statistical mean magnetic field which depends on fluctuation variance. Then, through asymptotics, numerical simulations, and a Grad-Shafranov type argument, we show that the variational principle supports smooth solutions for specific fluctuation statistics chosen to minimally modify the standard equilibrium modeling paradigm. Physically, this model smooths singular current sheets with a length scale determined by the magnetic field fluctuations.

### [2] [A Fully Electromagnetic Hybrid PIC-Fluid Model for Predictive Fusion Neutron Yield in Dense Plasma Focus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09032)
*Yinjian Zhao,Zhe Liu,Qiang Sun,Qianhong Zhou,Guangrui Sun*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究开发了一个全电磁混合仿真框架，用于预测致密等离子体焦点装置中的中子产额。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 磁约束聚变和惯性约束聚变的发展受限于能量损失、等离子体不稳定性以及大型设施的复杂性。致密等离子体焦点作为一种紧凑、脉冲功率驱动的替代方案，其定量预测中子产额面临数值挑战，主要在于需要自洽地解决动力学离子行为、电磁能量耦合和真空场演化。

Method: 开发了一个全电磁混合仿真框架：采用粒子网格法处理动力学离子，电子作为准中性流体，在等离子体和真空中求解麦克斯韦方程。广义欧姆定律包含电阻、电子压力梯度和霍尔项，并使用预测-校正法更新电流密度。该方法应用于非中空的180 kA DPF几何结构。

Result: 模拟的离子密度、离子温度和轴向电场再现了鞘层形成、轴向行进、径向压缩和箍缩后膨胀过程。外鞘前沿位置与全动力学基准在可比区间内误差小于10%。通过简化拟合D-D聚变截面，预测的总中子产额为0.296×10^7，与相似电流下报道的全动力学结果量级相当，比早期混合结果高出近两个数量级。

Conclusion: 该研究提出的全电磁混合框架能够有效预测致密等离子体焦点装置的中子产额，与全动力学模拟结果具有较好的一致性，为解决等离子体焦点设备中的聚变反应预测提供了一种更高效的计算方法。

Abstract: While magnetic confinement fusion (MCF) and inertial confinement fusion (ICF) remain the primary routes toward controlled fusion, progress is still constrained by energy loss, plasma instabilities, and the cost and complexity of large-scale facilities. The Dense Plasma Focus (DPF) device presents a compact, pulsed-power-driven alternative for producing fusion-relevant conditions and neutron emissions. However, the quantitative prediction of neutron yield in DPF devices poses a significant numerical challenge, primarily due to the imperative of self-consistently resolving kinetic ion behavior, electromagnetic energy coupling, and vacuum field evolution. This complexity often impedes a definitive understanding of the underlying neutron production mechanisms. To address this, we develop a fully electromagnetic hybrid simulation framework: ions are advanced kinetically with particle-in-cell, electrons are a quasi-neutral fluid, and Maxwell's equations are solved in both plasma and vacuum. The generalized Ohm law includes resistive, electron pressure-gradient, and Hall terms, with a predictor-corrector update for current density. We apply the model to a non-hollow 180 kA DPF geometry similar to the LLNL configuration. The simulated ion density, ion temperature, and axial electric field reproduce sheath formation, axial rundown, radial compression, and post-pinch expansion. The outer sheath front position agrees with fully kinetic benchmarks within 10\% over the available comparison interval. With a compact fit to the D-D fusion cross section, the predicted total neutron yield is 0.296e7, comparable in order of magnitude to reported fully kinetic results at similar currents and nearly two orders of magnitude higher than earlier hybrid results.

### [3] [Short-Time Plasma Evolution: Flow Generation and Magnetogenesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09194)
*Zain H. Saleem,Hamid Saleem*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 开发了一个自洽的双流体解析框架，用于描述等离子体在短时间尺度内的演化，阐明了流动和磁场耦合产生的基本机制。发现离子动量与质量守恒的一致性要求总压力满足拉普拉斯方程，这导致了一类精确解析解可用于描述压力梯度同时驱动等离子体流动并通过Biermann型机制产生磁场。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 等离子体中流动和磁场的耦合产生是一个普遍存在于激光产生等离子体和天体物理系统中的基本物理过程。然而，现有的理论框架往往不能统一描述短时间尺度内压力驱动的磁生成和等离子体流动。

Method: 建立了一个自洽的双流体解析模型，特别注重离子动量守恒和质量守恒的一致性要求。通过分析发现总压力必须满足拉普拉斯方程的约束条件，基于此推导出了一类精确的解析解。

Result: 获得了压力梯度同时驱动等离子体流动和通过Biermann型机制产生磁场的解析解。使用典型参数计算显示，得到的磁场强度和流动速度与激光等离子体和天体物理系统的实验观测结果一致。

Conclusion: 该双流体框架为短时间尺度内的压力驱动磁生成和等离子体流动提供了统一的理论描述，揭示了等离子体演化中流动与磁场耦合产生的基本机制，为理解激光等离子体和天体物理系统中的相关现象提供了理论基础。

Abstract: We develop a self-consistent analytical two-fluid framework for plasma evolution in the short-time regime, elucidating the fundamental mechanism underlying the coupled generation of flow and magnetic fields. We show that consistency between ion momentum and mass conservation imposes a structural constraint on the system: the total pressure must satisfy the Laplace equation, $\nabla^2 P = 0$. This constraint enables a class of exact analytical solutions in which pressure gradients simultaneously drive plasma flow and generate magnetic fields through a Biermann-type mechanism. Using representative parameters, we obtain magnetic-field strengths and flow velocities consistent with both laser-produced plasmas and large-scale astrophysical systems. This framework provides a unified description of pressure-driven magnetogenesis and plasma flow in the short-time regime.

</details>
