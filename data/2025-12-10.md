### [1] [ALS Storage Ring RF Control System Upgrade Plan and Status](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07996)
*Najm Us Saqib,Angel Jurado,Esteban Andrade,Qiang Du,Jeong Han Lee,Miroslaw Dach,Benjamin Flugstad*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: ALS加速器射频控制系统升级：用Allen-Bradley PLC替代老化的Horner PLC，提升可维护性和可靠性


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有Horner PLC控制器和远程I/O模块已服役至寿命末期，需要升级以提高可维护性、可靠性和长期支持能力

Method: 采用Allen-Bradley PLC平台进行全面系统升级，包括详细的项目规划、系统设计和实施

Result: 论文展示了升级项目的规划、设计和当前实施进展状态

Conclusion: ALS加速器的射频控制系统正在进行现代化升级，以确保持续可靠运行

Abstract: The Advanced Light Source (ALS) at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, a third-generation synchrotron light source operational since 1992, is undergoing a comprehensive upgrade of its storage ring RF control system. The legacy Horner PLC controllers and remote I/O modules, now at end-of-life, are being replaced with an Allen-Bradley PLC platform to improve maintainability, reliability, and long-term support. This paper presents the planning, design, and current status of the upgrade project.

### [2] [Modeling of van-der-Meer scan at NICA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08605)
*Anton Babaev*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 本文讨论了束流密度受沙漏效应畸变时van-der-Meer方法的适用性，特别是在Nuclotron离子对撞设施中，并修正了理论形式，提出了估算该方法偏差的通用方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究沙漏效应对van-der-Meer绝对亮度校准方法的适用性影响，因为Nuclotron离子对撞设施(NICA)在设计时就存在显著的沙漏效应。

Method: 修正了van-der-Meer方法的理论形式以考虑沙漏效应，提出了一种估算光束不可因式分解时该方法偏差的通用方法。

Result: 研究显示了沙漏效应对亮度测量和校准的重要性，表明在存在沙漏效应时直接应用传统van-der-Meer方法会导致偏差。

Conclusion: 在存在显著沙漏效应的对撞机(如NICA)中，必须修正van-der-Meer方法才能获得准确的亮度校准，本文提出的框架可用于估算和校正这种效应导致的偏差。

Abstract: Van-der-Meer method is used in hadron colliders for absolute luminosity calibration. In the letter the applicability of the method is discussed when particles desnsity is distorted by hour-glass effect next to interaction point. The study is motivated by close commisioning of Nuclotron based Ion Collider fAcility where hour-glass effected is significant by design. The theoretical van-der-Meer formalism is revised for this case. Importance of hour-glass effect for luminosity measurements and calibration is demonstrated. Also, the general approach to the estimation of van-der-Meer method bias when it is used for non-factorizable beams is proposed.

</details>
