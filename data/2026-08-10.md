### [1] [The Equations of Reduced Magnetohydrodynamics in Dipole Coordinates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06617)
*Ian M. DesJardin,Christopher Bard,Natalia Y. Buzulukova,John C. Dorelli*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究通过多尺度分析推导了偶极坐标系中低β等离子体的简化磁流体动力学方程，揭示了场向电流与等离子体涡旋的耦合关系，为磁层-电离层耦合问题提供了新工具，并使用计算机代数系统简化复杂坐标计算。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 简化磁流体动力学方程能够以计算可行的方式分离阿尔文能量传递和湍流动力学，但需要针对特定几何配置进行推导。研究旨在为地球磁层-电离层耦合问题提供一个能够将磁层中动态演化的场向电流与低高度磁流体湍流发展自洽联系的框架，特别是在地磁暴和亚暴期间。

Method: 研究者通过对偶极坐标系中低β等离子体进行多尺度分析，推导了简化磁流体动力学方程。应用克雷斯定理推导了磁流体湍流时间尺度下背景条件必须满足的平衡条件。使用了计算机代数系统来处理复杂坐标系中的代数计算。

Result: 研究推导出了偶极坐标系中低β等离子体的简化磁流体动力学方程，发现了等离子体沿磁通管流动与阿尔文速度变化之间的联系，这可能驱动非线性波效应。最终方程展示了场向电流与等离子体涡旋之间的密切耦合，包括非均匀磁通管面积和实际等离子体密度分布的影响。

Conclusion: 该研究推导了偶极坐标系中低β等离子体的简化磁流体动力学方程，揭示了场向电流与等离子体涡旋的密切耦合关系。工作为地球磁层-电离层耦合问题提供了新工具，能够以自洽方式将磁层中动态演化的场向电流与低高度的磁流体动力学湍流发展联系起来。此外，研究展示了计算机代数系统在复杂坐标系中进行多尺度微扰分析的方法，为复杂几何条件下的可重复、可验证研究提供了模板。

Abstract: The equations of reduced magnetohydrodynamics (RMHD) isolate the Alfvénic energy transfer and turbulence dynamics from MHD in a computationally tractable way. In this study, we derive the equations of RMHD for a low $β$ plasma in a dipole coordinate system aligned with the background magnetic field using a multiscale analysis. The Kreiss theorem is used to derive the equilibrium conditions that the background conditions must satisfy on the time scale of MHD turbulence. From these, we find a connection between plasma flow along flux tubes and changes in the Alfvén speed that may drive nonlinear wave effects. The final equations demonstrate an intimate coupling between field aligned currents and plasma vorticity including the effects of nonuniform flux tube area and realistic plasma density profiles. This work has immediate application to the magnetosphere-ionosphere coupling problem in the Earth's magnetosphere as it provides a way to link dynamically evolving field aligned currents from the magnetosphere, especially important during geomagnetic storms and substorms, with the development of magnetohydrodynamic turbulence at low altitudes in a self-consistent manner. This also clarifies the role of Alfvén waves as a transfer mechanism of energy under these inhomogeneous circumstances while remaining simple enough to make predictions. Furthermore, this study makes use of a novel methodology, a computer algebra system, in performing the brunt of algebraic work under complicated coordinate systems. We hope this approach serves as a template for performing reproducible and verifiable multiscale perturbation analysis under arbitrarily complex geometries.

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: TEK代码首次实现了ITPA-EP选定的简化托卡马克构型中高能粒子驱动的TAEs非线性基准测试，填补了该案例非线性基准的空白。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: ITPA-EP组选择的简化托卡马克构型用于代码基准测试，虽然线性基准已建立，但非线性基准尚缺乏。作者旨在填补这一空白。

Method: 使用新型回旋运动代码TEK，采用混合变量pullback方法缓解电磁消除问题，对所有粒子种类在回旋运动模型下同等处理，进行单n和多n模式非线性模拟。

Result: 非线性结果与阿尔芬本征模驱动的带状场拍频理论良好一致，验证了非线性模拟的正确性，提供了饱和水平和EP输运数据。

Linear benchmark has been well established by other codes, whereas nonlinear benchmark for this case is lacking. This paper presents, besides the linear benchmark, nonlinear results for both single-n and multiple-n simulations (n is the toroidal mode number). The nonlinear results are in good agreement with an analytical theory on zonal field beat-driven by Alfven eigenmodes, partially verifying correctness of the nonlinear simulations. The saturation level and the resulting EP transport are examined. This provides data for future inter-code nonlinear benchmarking. In TEK, all species (electrons, thermal ions, EPs) are treated on the same footing using the gyrokinetic model (with electrons in the zero Larmor radius limit). The electromagnetic cancellation problem is mitigated by using the mixed-variable pullback method. Numerical details related to electromagnetic gyrokinetic simulation are discussed.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 使用陀螺动力学模拟方法，包括线性模拟和电子尺度/多尺度非线性模拟。通过线性敏感性分析研究模式对磁场压缩分量和碰撞频率的依赖性。

Result: 发现了一种主要由负电子密度梯度驱动的捕获电子电子尺度撕裂奇偶性模式，该模式的增长率对磁场压缩分量敏感，与标准微撕裂模式不同。线性分析显示模式在较低碰撞率下最不稳定，非线性模拟表明这些模式能产生与实验相当的兆瓦级功率输运。

Abstract: Gyrokinetic simulations of an NSTX spherical-tokamak plasma reveal novel negative-density-gradient (NDG) drift waves at electron-gyroradius-scale that drive turbulent transport comparable to the experimental power flow. Although thermal transport is mainly carried by transverse magnetic, $δA_{\parallel}$, fluctuations, the growth rate is sensitive to compressional magnetic fluctuations, $δB_{\parallel}$, differentiating these from standard microtearing modes (MTMs). Linear sensitivity analysis also reveals that the modes are most unstable at lower collisionality, consistent with the trapped-electron character. Electron-scale and multi-scale nonlinear simulations show that these modes can account for several megawatts of experimental power, with the latter suggesting that experimental gradients may lie at a bifurcation between distinct turbulence regimes.

### [5] [Autonomous discovery of accelerator commissioning algorithms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07138)
*Thorsten Hellert*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 该论文提出了一个闭环研究框架，其中语言模型代理编写调试代码，在模拟中测试，并从结果中改进算法，从而自动化调试过程。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当前的模拟调试虽然减少了现代光源设计调试的风险，但调试程序仍完全由人类专家设计，且经过晶格变化后需要大量人力重新开发，限制了其在早期设计迭代中的使用。

Method: 创建了一个闭环研究框架，让语言模型代理自动编写调试代码，在模拟环境中进行测试，并根据测试结果改进算法。应用在ALS-U累加环模型的RF束流捕获任务中。

Result: 该框架显著改进了已有的专家调试程序，并能从极少的起始代码构建出有效的程序。更具能力的模型能从更少的初始代码成功。扩展到多目标优化时，生成了16个非支配算法，涵盖了快速束流捕获和校正预置机器误差之间的不同权衡方案。

Conclusion: 该研究将调试研究从评估人类设计的程序转向了代理直接参与发现加速器算法的新模式，为调试过程自动化开辟了新途径。

Abstract: Simulated commissioning has become essential for de-risking modern light-source design and commissioning, but the procedures being simulated are still designed entirely by human experts. Their labor-intensive redevelopment after lattice changes makes such studies hard to repeat and limits their use during early design iteration. This Letter demonstrates a closed research loop in which a language-model agent writes commissioning code, tests it in simulation, and improves the algorithm from the results. Applied to RF beam capture in the ALS-U accumulator-ring model, the loop substantially improves a working expert procedure and can construct a working one from a minimal starting point, with more capable models succeeding from less initial code. Extending the same framework to multiple objectives produces 16 non-dominated algorithms spanning physically distinct trade-offs between rapid beam capture and correction of seeded machine errors. This reframes commissioning studies from evaluating human-designed procedures toward a mode in which agents participate directly in discovering accelerator algorithms.
