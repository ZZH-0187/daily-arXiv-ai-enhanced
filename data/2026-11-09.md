### [1] [Different exact-law formulations to calculate the energy cascade rate in magnetohydrodynamic turbulence with and without mean magnetic fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09437)
*Ramesh Sasmal,Supratik Banerjee*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 使用1024³网格的高分辨率直接数值模拟，首次系统比较了三种均匀MHD湍流总能量串级的精确律公式：Yaglom-Monin、Banerjee-Galtier和两点关联函数公式，发现它们在不同磁场强度下均表现一致，验证了后两者在空间等离子体湍流中的实用性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在远大于离子惯性长度和离子回旋尺度的空间等离子体湍流中，通常用不可压缩磁流体力学描述。目前有多个能量串级精确律公式，但缺乏系统比较。研究旨在验证不同公式在估计能量串级率方面的一致性和适用性，特别是在各向异性条件下。

Method: 采用1024³网格点的高分辨率直接数值模拟，系统比较了三种均匀MHD湍流（各向同性/非各向同性）的总能量串级精确律：Yaglom-Monin公式、Banerjee-Galtier公式和两点关联函数公式。在不同背景磁场强度（零、中等、强）下进行分析，并通过角度平均考察能量转移率的一致性。

Result: 三种公式的角度平均能量转移率在不同磁场强度下均表现出极好的一致性。研究证实了Banerjee-Galtier公式和两点关联函数公式在空间等离子体湍流中的实用性。同时发现各向同性的Politano-Pouquet公式不能提供真正的方向性转移率，在各向异性条件下会导致不准确的估计。

Conclusion: 研究首次系统验证了三种MHD湍流能量串级精确律的一致性和适用性。Banerjee-Galtier公式和两点关联函数公式可作为多航天器测量中能量串级率估计的有效工具。同时指出，仅增强平均磁场强度并不能获得还原MHD的串级率，强调了不同非线性相互作用通道在维持惯性尺度能量串级中的协同作用。

Abstract: At scales much larger than the ion inertial length and ion-gyroscale, space-plasma turbulence is often described within the framework of incompressible magnetohydrodynamics (MHD). Using high-resolution direct numerical simulations with $1024^3$ grid points, we perform the first systematic numerical comparison of three exact-law formulations for the total energy cascade in homogeneous, but not necessarily isotropic, MHD turbulence: the Yaglom-Monin (YM), Banerjee-Galtier (BG), and two-point correlator formulations. Despite directional variations, the angle-averaged energy transfer rates for all three formulations show excellent agreement for zero, moderate, and strong background magnetic fields. These results clearly suggest the usefulness of alternative BG and correlator formulations for estimating the energy cascade rates in turbulent space plasmas when the required spatial derivatives are accessible through multi-spacecraft measurements. We also argue that the isotropic Politano-Pouquet (PP) formulation does not provide a true directional transfer rate and therefore its application as a single-direction energy transfer rate estimator leads to inaccurate values, especially under anisotropic conditions. Decomposing the exact formulations into RMHD and non-RMHD contributions, we also show that the RMHD cascade rates cannot be obtained merely by increasing the strength of the mean magnetic field in incompressible magnetohydrodynamic turbulence. Finally, a term-by-term analysis of different formulations reveals how different nonlinear interaction channels coexist and collaborate with each other to maintain the same energy cascade rate across the inertial scales.

### [2] [Are the tearing and the Weibel instabilities the same?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09639)
*K. M. Schoeffler,H. Aravindakshan,F. Pucci,M. E. Innocenti*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 研究人员通过模拟展示了撕裂不稳定性在薄电流层中会平滑过渡到韦伯尔不稳定性，当电流层厚度小于离子拉莫尔半径时，两种不稳定性的机制本质相通。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 本文旨在探究撕裂不稳定性和韦伯尔不稳定性之间的关系。尽管传统上认为它们是两种不同的等离子体不稳定性现象——撕裂不稳定性依赖磁场梯度释放自由能，而韦伯尔不稳定性则来自温度各向异性——但研究者注意到磁场梯度需要电流存在，而电流引起的等离子体漂移会产生有效的温度各向异性，这可能是两种不稳定性的连接点。

Method: 研究采用半隐式粒子网格（PIC）模拟方法。通过改变电流层的厚度参数，系统地研究了从厚电流层到薄电流层的过渡情况。特别关注了当电流层厚度小于离子拉莫尔半径时的情况，此时撕裂不稳定性的标准假设不再适用。

Result: 模拟结果显示存在明显的平滑过渡：对于厚电流层，系统表现出典型的撕裂不稳定性特征，温度各向异性影响很小；对于薄电流层（厚度小于离子拉莫尔半径），系统更符合韦伯尔不稳定性的物理机制。有趣的是，在薄电流层中被识别为"撕裂不稳定性"的模式，其增长率与离子回旋理论预测的增长率惊人一致，而离子回旋理论本质上是韦伯尔不稳定性的磁化对应物。

Conclusion: 研究证明撕裂不稳定性和韦伯尔不稳定性本质上是相互关联的，特别是在薄电流层（厚度小于离子拉莫尔半径）的情况下。两种不稳定性共享相同的物理根源：由电流引起的有效温度各向异性。这一发现为统一理解等离子体中的各种宏观和微观不稳定性提供了新的视角。

Abstract: The tearing instability, which extracts free energy from a magnetic field gradient, and the Weibel instability, which draws free energy from temperature anisotropies, appear at first glance to be fundamentally distinct. However, the magnetic field gradient in the tearing instability requires a current, and the resulting plasma drift leads to an effective temperature anisotropy, so that the plasma is hotter along the direction of the current flow. This anisotropy can play an essential role in driving the instability. We demonstrate, using semi-implicit particle-in-cell simulations, a smooth transition between the tearing regime for thick current sheets, where the anisotropy has little influence, and a thin-current-sheet regime better described by Weibel physics. We find that for current sheet thicknesses less than the ion Larmor radius, where standard assumptions of the tearing instability break down, the growth rate of the mode commonly identified as the tearing instability agrees remarkably well with the growth rate predicted by ion-cyclotron theory, which is essentially the magnetized counterpart of the Weibel instability.

### [3] [Stable, High-Charge, Laser-Driven VHEE Beams for Preclinical Applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09329)
*Chaitanya Varma,Camilla Giaccaglia,Julien Gautier,Jean-Philippe Goddet,Amar Tafzi,Pascal Rousseau,Alessandro Flacco*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 激光等离子体加速器生成高达559 pC电荷的极高峰值剂量率电子束，用于医学放射治疗研究


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 激光驱动的等离子体加速器（LPA）在产生用于医学应用的高能电子束方面具有潜力，但提高单脉冲电荷同时保持指向稳定性仍具挑战性，需要探索适用于临床前VHEE（极高能电子）辐照的LPA操作机制

Method: 使用80 TW激光系统进行实验，通过粒子模拟（PIC）分析加速机制，测量电子束的指向漂移、光谱重复性，并进行VHEE辐照实验，测量剂量分布

Result: 实现了单脉冲总电荷达（559±56）pC的激光加速电子束，光谱主峰在40-60 MeV，高能尾部延伸至约200 MeV，指向漂移在空气中可低至0.7 mrad，有效脉冲剂量率达10^12 Gy/s，平均剂量率达数十Gy/min

Conclusion: 研究建立了一种适用于临床前VHEE辐照的LPA操作机制，展示了强束载荷下仍能维持显著加速电荷的可行方法，为医学应用提供了关键的实验基础

Abstract: Laser-driven plasma accelerators (LPAs) are promising sources of very-high-energy-electron (VHEE) beams for medical applications, but increasing charge per pulse while maintaining pointing stability remains challenging. We demonstrate laser-accelerated beams with total charge per pulse reaching (559 $\pm$ 56) pC using an 80 TW laser system. The spectra have a dominant component at 40-60 MeV and a high-energy tail extending to approximately 200 MeV. Pointing jitter measured in air at the irradiation target plane reaches as low as 0.7 mrad. The beams were used for VHEE irradiation experiments involving a 10 mm region of interest through a 40 mm depth at 400 $\pm$ 20 mGy/pulse. Spectral reproducibility is quantified over fixed-setting shot sequences. Particle-in-cell simulations produce a peak-and-tail spectrum through acceleration in successive bubbles associated with a strongly modified plasma wake, providing a plausible mechanism for maintaining substantial accelerated charge despite strong beam loading. Dose-weighted time-of-flight calculations indicate an effective pulse dose rate of $10^{12}$ Gy/s and average dose rates of tens of Gy/min. These results establish an LPA operating regime relevant to preclinical VHEE irradiation.

### [4] [Secondary neutron beam production at Jefferson Lab](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09525)
*A. Fulci,P. Ambrozewicz,M. Battaglieri,M. Bondi,R. De Vita,M. Filippini,G. Foti,S. Grazzi,G. Mandaglio,T. Nagorna,A. Pilloni,M. Spreafico,S. Vallarino*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 研究用于杰斐逊实验室Hall A束流捕集器的11 GeV电子束二次中子产额及其传输，探索与主实验并行运行的潜力


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索利用现有电子束流产生高能中子源的机会，为核物理、材料辐照等研究提供独特的能量覆盖（热中子到GeV级）实验条件

Method: 分析三种配置：当前设置、规划的BDX设施、优化的BDX布局（带专用中子引出管道）；研究不同材料的辐射体对中子产额的增强效果

Result: 中子能谱覆盖热中子到数GeV范围，特别在1 GeV以上具有独特优势；优化布局和材料选择可显著提高中子通量

Conclusion: 11 GeV电子束可产生宽能谱的二次中子束，通过优化配置和辐射体设计能显著提升中子产量，为下游实验区提供有吸引力的次级束流实验机会

Abstract: We investigate secondary neutron production by an 11 GeV electron beam in the Hall A beam dump at Jefferson Lab and their transport to a downstream experimental area. This secondary beam could operate opportunistically with the primary electron-scattering programme. The neutron energy spectrum extends from thermal energies to several GeVs, offering a distinctive opportunity for irradiation at energy grater than 1 GeV. We considered three configurations: the current setup, the planned BDX facility, and an optimized BDX layout with a dedicated neutron extraction pipe. We also studied how radiators made of different materials and placed upstream of the beam dump increase neutron production.
