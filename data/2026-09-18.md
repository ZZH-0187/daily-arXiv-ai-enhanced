Each gram of stored ${}^{232}$U produces 10 mg (8.2 Ci) of ${}^{228}$Th per year. Subsequent ${}^{228}$Th(n,$γ$)${}^{229}$Th converts 0.07 to 0.34 g of ${}^{229}$Th per gram of ${}^{228}$Th. These pathways scale from kilowatt to megawatt D-T sources and could secure millions of ${}^{212}$Pb and ${}^{225}$Ac dose-equivalents per year.

### [2] [Efficient laser ion acceleration in near-critical density plasmas in the picosecond pulse regime](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19571)

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该论文提出一个扩展TNSA理论的分析模型，解释了当靶材等离子体密度接近相对论临界密度时，激光-离子转换效率显著提高的物理机制，预测最大转换效率可达37%。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 千焦级短脉冲激光是产生相对论离子束的优秀工具，但实现高的激光到离子转换效率仍是一个开放挑战。最近的实验显示，当靶材平均等离子体密度接近相对论临界极限时，转换效率显著提高，需要理论解释这一现象。

Method: 扩展传统的法向鞘层加速（TNSA）理论，纳入激光对相对论临界密度靶的穿透效应，建立分析模型描述激光能量通过加热膨胀电子鞘层向离子前沿传输的物理过程。模型重点优化激光传输和靶面密度，并通过2D、3D粒子网格模拟验证理论预测。

Result: 模型预测最大离子转换效率可达37%，比经典TNSA显著提高。理论关键标度关系与粒子网格模拟结果和已发表的实验数据一致。碳氢等离子体的2D、3D模拟证实了加速过程的鲁棒性。

Conclusion: 相对论临界密度靶材通过增强激光穿透和电子鞘层加热，显著改善了激光-离子转换效率和离子截止能量。这为最大化离子产额提供了一条有效路径，相关模型和模拟结果为优化实验设计提供了理论指导。

Abstract: Kilojoule-class short-pulse (< 10 ps) lasers are excellent tools for generating relativistic ion beams, but achieving high laser-to-ion conversion efficiency remains an open challenge. Recent experiments show a significant increase in conversion efficiency when leveraging targets with an average plasma density approaching the relativistic-critical limit. We present an analytical model that explains this increase by extending the theory of target-normal sheath acceleration (TNSA) to include laser penetration of relativistic-critical density targets, enabling energy transfer to the ion front by heating the expanding electron sheath. This physical process increases the sheath field strength and significantly improves ion cutoff energy and laser-to-ion conversion efficiency relative to classical TNSA. The model predicts a maximum ion conversion efficiency of 37% by optimizing laser transmission and target areal density. Key scalings of the model agree with particle-in-cell (PIC) simulations and published experimental data. A series of 2D and 3D simulations of hydrocarbon plasmas confirm the robustness of the acceleration process, demonstrating a path for maximizing ion yields using relativistic-critical targets.

TL;DR: 提出一种无矩阵的残差最小化方法，用于在有限网格上准备导心分布函数。该方法在物理雅可比度量下最小化固定的保守离散残差，使用Polak-Ribiere+迭代和精确二次线性搜索。通过JAX/XLA评估算子及其伴随算子而不组装四维矩阵。

Motivation: 解决在有限网格上准备导心分布函数时，传统方法需要组装四维矩阵导致的巨大计算成本问题。目标是在不显式构建大矩阵的情况下，实现分布函数的数值准备和优化。

Abstract: We present a matrix-free residual-minimization method for preparing guiding-center distribution functions on finite grids. Non-negative projection is treated as an optional constrained extension: it leaves the residual operator unchanged but changes the feasible zero-residual set and endpoint selection. Results are finite-grid, finite-time evidence and do not establish a continuous equilibrium, grid convergence, strict positivity, or a transport prediction.
