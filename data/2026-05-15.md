### [1] [A Hybrid Scheme to Achieve Highest Implosion Performance on the OMEGA Laser](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14129)
*P. S. Farmakis,L. Ceurvorst,V. Gopalaswamy,D. Cao,K. S. Anderson,A. Lees,D. Patel,R. Betti*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该论文研究了混合激波驱动方案，结合了直接和间接驱动的优点，使用X射线的均匀性和直接照明的效率来提升聚变性能。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 长期以来，结合直接驱动和间接驱动的混合激光聚变方案被视为最优选择，因为它结合了X射线的均匀性和直接照明的效率。目前需要一种能够抑制激光印记引起的流体不稳定性、保持靶壳完整性的方案，以实现低绝热线、高会聚度的内爆。

Method: 提出了混合激波驱动方案，使用镀金X射线转换外壳产生X射线驱动初始激波进入标准直接驱动靶丸，然后使用直接照明进行内爆。通过2D模拟验证了该方案在OMEGA激光器上的可行性。

Result: 该方案有效抑制了激光印记引发的流体不稳定性，保持了内爆过程中靶壳的完整性。预计将显著提升聚变产量，使OMEGA上的劳森参数记录提高约85%，同时有效消除了对激光平滑的要求。

Conclusion: 混合激波驱动方案为高性能内爆提供了稳健的平台，为先进高增益惯性聚变能靶的研发铺平了道路。

Abstract: Merging direct and indirect-drive has long been viewed as an optimal hybrid laser-fusion scheme that combines the uniformity of x rays with the efficiency of direct illumination. We present the first integrated 2D simulations of hybrid shock drive (HSD) targets for the OMEGA laser. The HSD scheme [L. Ceurvorst et al., Phys. Rev. E 101 063207 (2020)] uses x rays from a thin Au-coated x-ray converter outer shell to drive the initial shock into a standard direct-drive capsule. Direct illumination is used to implode the target after the first shock. The design effectively suppresses laser-imprint seeding of hydrodynamic instabilities, maintaining shell integrity during the implosion. This scheme will enable fielding low-adiabat, high-convergence implosions on OMEGA with expected performance greatly exceeding those of current designs. HSD targets are projected to significantly enhance fusion yields, potentially increasing the record Lawson parameter by $\sim$85\% on OMEGA while effectively eliminating the requirement for laser smoothing. These results position HSD as a robust platform for high-performance implosions, paving the way for advanced high-gain inertial fusion energy targets.

### [2] [Axion magnetohydrodynamics and reconnection-driven axion bursts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15162)
*H. Terças*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该论文建立了超越理想极限的轴子磁流体动力学，考虑轴子惯性和非理想等离子体的基本物理。研究发现磁场重联会激发混合阿尔文-轴子模式，在磁主导环境中实现磁场与轴子之间的相干能量交换，为中子星和磁星中的瞬态轴子爆发提供了理论机制。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的轴子研究主要关注静态磁场中的产生机制，而在真实的天体物理环境中，等离子体通常处于非理想状态，特别是在磁场重联等动态过程中。本研究旨在探索非理想等离子体环境下轴子的产生机制，特别是在中子星和磁星等高能天体物理环境中。

Method: 作者从第一性原理出发，建立了包含轴子惯性和非理想等离子体物理的轴子磁流体动力学框架。在该框架下，重点分析了磁场冻结失效的区域（其中E·B ≠ 0）如何成为轴子辐射的局部源。通过理论推导，展示了磁场重联如何激发混合阿尔文-轴子模式。

Result: 研究发现，在磁场主导的环境中，磁场重联过程能够实现磁场能量与轴子能量之间的相干交换。具体而言，磁耗散区域（E·B ≠ 0）会作为局部源产生轴子辐射。在中子星和磁星中，这种机制将普遍导致重联驱动的阿尔文波耗散产生的瞬态轴子爆发。

Conclusion: 该工作提出了磁场重联驱动轴子产生的新机制，为轴子探测提供了与静态磁场方法互补的新途径。通过推导轴子-光子耦合的特征敏感性，该研究将理论机制与观测可能性联系起来，拓展了轴子探测的实验窗口。

Abstract: We formulate axion magnetohydrodynamics beyond the ideal limit, retaining axion inertia and the essential physics of non-ideal plasmas from first principles. In this framework, regions where magnetic flux freezing breaks down acquire a new physical role: whenever $\mathbf{E} \cdot\ \mathbf{B} \neq 0$, magnetic dissipation acts as a localized source of axion radiation. We show that magnetic reconnection naturally excites mixed Alfvén-axion modes, enabling coherent energy exchange between magnetic fields and axions in magnetically dominated environments. In neutron stars and magnetars, this mechanism leads generically to transient axion bursts powered by reconnection--driven Alfvénic dissipation. We connect this production process to observational prospects and derive a characteristic sensitivity to the axion--photon coupling, complementary to searches based on static magnetic fields.
