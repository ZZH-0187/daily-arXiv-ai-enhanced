### [1] [Radiation safety considerations for ultrafast lasers beyond laser machining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25585)
*Simon Bohlen,Julian Holland,Rudolf Weber*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 目前德国辐射防护法规将超快激光的辐射安全阈值通用化，但研究发现X射线生成条件具有高度特异性，主要出现在材料加工场景；非加工环境的实验室条件下，辐射剂量可忽略不计。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 德国辐射防护法规要求辐照度超过$1 \times 10^{13}~W/cm^2$的超快激光系统必须申报或审批，但该阈值未区分材料加工与其他应用场景。研究旨在验证辐射风险是否在所有超快激光应用中普遍存在，为法规提供更精准的科学依据。

Method: 通过评估现有辐射研究在非加工环境中的适用性，并在典型实验室条件下进行实验，测量超快激光与欠密度气体、静止固体靶等作用时的X射线剂量。

Result: 实验结果表明，在非材料加工的代表性实验室条件下（如激光与气体或静止固体靶相互作用），X射线产生剂量可忽略甚至为零；X射线生成需要特定条件，这些条件通常仅在材料加工过程中满足。

Conclusion: 现行法规将材料加工特有的辐射风险泛化到所有超快激光应用，未能准确反映实际物理条件；建议辐射防护标准应区分不同应用场景，而非采用统一阈值。

Abstract: The interaction of ultrafast lasers with plasmas has been studied for many years, primarily with respect to fundamental emission mechanisms. Only in recent years has ionizing radiation emerged as a safety concern in ultrafast laser-based material processing, where high pulse energies, repetition rates, and average powers, combined with continuous material supply, can lead to sustained X-ray emission. These processing-specific findings have informed German radiation protection legislation, which mandates notification or approval for laser systems exceeding irradiances of $1 \times 10^{13}~W/cm^2$. However, this threshold does not distinguish between material processing and other ultrafast laser applications. In this work, we show that the conditions required for X-ray generation are highly specific and are typically only met during material processing. We assess the applicability of existing radiation studies to non-processing environments and present experimental results demonstrating negligible or no dose production under representative laboratory conditions, such as ultrafast laser interactions with underdense gas or stationary solid targets. We conclude that current legislation generalizes a processing-specific hazard to all ultrafast laser applications and does not adequately reflect the relevant physical conditions.

### [2] [On the double-adiabatic equations in the relativistic regime](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25594)
*Francisco Ley,Aaron Tran,Ellen G. Zweibel*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 本研究将双绝热演化方程推广到相对论和极端相对论领域，建立了磁化、无碰撞等离子体的解析动力学模型，并获得了适用于天体物理环境的相对论双绝热方程。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统双绝热方程主要适用于非相对论等离子体，而天体物理环境中广泛存在相对论性粒子（如宇宙射线、电子-正电子对）。需要建立适用于相对论和极端相对论条件的动力学描述，以完善对脉冲星风云、天体物理喷流、黑洞吸积流等天体物理等离子体的理论研究。

Method: 1. 解析求解相对论、时间依赖的漂移动力学方程；2. 推导初始相对论麦克斯韦分布下各向异性麦克斯韦-于特纳分布的自然扩展；3. 计算时间依赖解的矩，在极端相对论情形下获得垂直与平行压力的演化解析表达式；4. 数值求解相对论情形的矩方程，获得该领域的双绝热方程一般表达式；5. 通过剪切和压缩盒子的完全动力学粒子网格模拟验证结果。

Result: 1. 获得了显式依赖磁场和密度变化的解析解；2. 建立了相对论双绝热方程；3. 粒子网格模拟结果与理论预测一致；4. 所获方程可直接应用于宇宙射线、电子-正电子对等相对论性粒子。

Conclusion: 成功将双绝热理论扩展至相对论和极端相对论领域，为天体物理等离子体的动力学建模提供了理论基础，特别适用于存在高能粒子的天体物理环境如脉冲星风云、黑洞吸积流等。

Abstract: We revisit the double adiabatic evolution equations and extend them to the relativistic and ultrarelativistic regimes. We analytically solve the relativistic, time-dependent drift kinetic equation for a homogeneous, magnetized, collisionless plasma and obtain a solution explicitly dependent on the magnetic field and density variations. In the case of an initial relativistic Maxwellian distribution, a natural extension to an anisotropic Maxwell-Jüttner is obtained. We calculate the moments of this time-dependent solution and obtain analytical expressions for the evolution of the perpendicular and parallel pressures in the ultrarelativistic case. We numerically solve the moment equations in the relativistic case and obtain general expressions for the double-adiabatic equations in this regime. We confirm our results using fully kinetic particle-in-cell simulations of shearing and compressing boxes. Our findings can be readily applied to relativistic species including cosmic-rays and electron-positron pairs, present in astrophysical plasmas like pulsar wind nebulae, astrophysical jets, black hole accretion flows, and Van Allen radiation belts.
