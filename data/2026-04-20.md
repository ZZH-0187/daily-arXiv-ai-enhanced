### [1] [Controlling external injection in laser-plasma accelerators with terahertz frequency bunch manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16059)
*Aras Amini,Lewis R. Reid,James K. Jones,Morgan T. Hibberd,Laura Corner,Darren M. Graham,Steven P. Jamison,Graeme Burt,Robert B. Appleby*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 提出利用太赫兹控制的电子束进行外部注入激光等离子体尾场加速的新概念，通过太赫兹频率实现电子束与驱动激光的时域锁定，并在注入前将高质量电子束压缩至亚10飞秒水平。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 激光等离子体尾场加速（LWFA）在紧凑装置中提供超高加速梯度，但其非线性特性限制了高质量束流的产生。传统射频（RF）注入器存在非线性压缩和激光束不同步问题，导致能量抖动和发射度增长。

Method: 采用太赫兹控制的外部电子束注入方案：1) 利用太赫兹频率实现电子束与驱动激光之间的时域锁定；2) 在注入LWFA前将高质量电子束压缩至亚10飞秒持续时间。

Result: 数值模拟表明，该方法可实现GeV级加速，同时保持优异的束流质量和稳定性：能量抖动和能量展宽约0.2%。

Conclusion: 这一概念为稳定的多级激光驱动加速器开辟了新途径，并为自由电子激光（FEL）等下一代应用的发展提供了技术支持。

Abstract: Laser-plasma wakefield acceleration (LWFA) offers ultrahigh accelerating gradients in compact setups, but the complex non-linear nature of the process makes it challenging to generate high-quality beams. Injection of electron bunches from an external source into a plasma accelerator provides a promising route to improved performance; however, electron bunches from conventional radio-frequency (RF)-based injectors suffer from non-linear compression and laser-beam asynchrony, leading to energy jitter and emittance growth. We present a fundamental concept of terahertz-controlled electron bunches for external injection into LWFA. This terahertz-frequency approach provides temporal locking between the electron beam and the drive laser, and enables the compression of high-quality beams to sub-10-fs durations before injection into the LWFA. Numerical simulations demonstrate that GeV-scale acceleration with excellent beam quality and stability -- energy jitter and energy spread around 0.2% -- can be achieved using this method. This concept opens new opportunities for stable, multi-stage laser-driven accelerators and supports the development of next-generation applications such as free-electron lasers (FELs).

### [2] [Ion-motion-driven enhancement of energy coupling and stability in relativistic laser-microchannel interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15545)
*K. Weichman,M. VanDusen-Gross,G. Bruhaug,J. P. Palastro,M. Wei,A. Haid,A. V. Arefiev,H. G. Rinderknecht*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 针对短脉冲激光与微通道靶相互作用，本研究发现通过离子运动可实现自组织状态，增强峰值场强与电荷、光子转换效率，并通过相似性参数指导低强度实验设计下一代高辐射性态激光装置。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 随着激光脉冲持续时间或强度的增加，离子运动在均匀等离子体中会导致不稳定性；而在结构靶中则可能引出更复杂且有益的行为，因此探索激光驱动微通道中离子运动的效应及自组织状态具有重要价值。

Method: 利用三维粒子模拟（3‑D particle‑in‑cell simulations），将脉冲持续时间、光斑尺寸与强度通过相似性参数与通道尺度关联，系统分析激光‑微通道相互作用的定性状态。

Result: 研究表明，存在一个由离子运动促成的自组织状态，可产生更强的峰值场并提高电荷和光子的转换效率，同时相似性参数指出，较低强度的实验结果可以为下一代高强度设施的设计提供参考。

Conclusion: 激光驱动微通道中的离子运动能够实现自组织状态，显著增强场强与辐射转换效率；提出的相似性参数不仅描述了相互作用的基本规律，还为设计未来高场、高辐射性态激光实验提供了有效指导。

Abstract: For sufficiently short relativistic-intensity laser pulses, the disparity in time scales for electron and ion motion causes ions to behave as a fixed, neutralizing background. As the pulse duration or intensity is increased, ion motion becomes important, leading to instability in uniform plasmas but more complex, and potentially desirable behavior in structured targets. In this work, we introduce a new self-organized regime in laser-driven microchannels wherein ion motion facilitates stronger peak fields and high charge and photon conversion efficiency. 3-D particle-in-cell simulations demonstrate that the qualitative laser-microchannel interaction regime is governed by similarity parameters relating the pulse duration, spot size, and intensity to channel scales. The observed similarity suggests that lower-intensity experiments can inform designs for next-generation facilities, where the high-field, highly-radiative properties of the self-organized regime are especially desirable.

### [3] [Mechanism Behind the Recombination Requirement for Benign Termination of Relativistic Electron Beams](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15575)
*George Su,Carl Friedrich Benedikt Zimmermann,Carlos Paz-Soldan,Matthias Hoelzl,Pavel Aleynikov*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 通过结合动力学建模和非线性MHD模拟，提出基于电离复合的机制解释聚变装置中相对论电子束的良性终止条件，首次建立了广泛适用且与实验一致的理论框架。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有研究对托卡马克中相对论电子束的良性终止机制缺乏统一的理论解释，特别是关于边缘撕裂模放大和磁场随机化的物理过程，需要建立可外推到下一代装置的理论模型。

Method: 采用包含中性粒子的动力学建模分析复合效应，结合JOREK代码进行非线性MHD模拟，研究有限数量的中性粒子注入如何通过复合增加体电阻率，从而选择性地放大边缘撕裂模。

Result: 发现复合作用增加的体电阻率（而非自由电子密度）才是决定良性终止的关键因素，这种机制优先放大边缘撕裂模，在相对论电子去约束期间产生更随机的边缘磁场，扩大电子润湿面积。

Conclusion: 首次提供了关于良性终止MHD机制的普遍适用且与实验一致的理论图景，为向下一代装置的外推提供了关键理论基础，确认了电阻率的核心调控作用。

Abstract: We present a first-principles explanation of the recombination requirement for benign termination of relativistic electron (RE) beams in tokamaks. Kinetic modeling including neutrals shows that the injection of neutrals over a finite quantity window, together with recombination, increases bulk resistivity. Nonlinear MHD simulations using the JOREK code demonstrate that this preferentially amplifies edge tearing modes, producing a more stochastic edge magnetic field during RE deconfinement, resulting in a larger RE wetted area. We identify resistivity, not the free electron density, to govern access to benign termination. This provides the first broadly applicable and experimentally consistent picture of the MHD mechanisms behind the benign scenario, critical to its extrapolation to next-step devices.

*Gerd Röpke*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究旨在建立热密等离子体性质的一致描述框架，为电子气和氢等离子体的状态方程以及介电函数等输运性质提供理论基准，特别是在低密度条件下验证数值模拟结果的可靠性。

Method: 1. 基于量子统计推导热力学和输运性质的平衡关联函数表达式；2. 采用解析方法或DFT-MD、PIMC等数值方法求解；3. 利用格林函数方法获得维里展开式。

Result: 1. 获得了均匀电子气和氢等离子体的状态方程结果；2. 推导了包含直流电导率（作为介电函数特例）的输运性质维里展开；3. 为低密度等离子体数值模拟提供了基准数据。

Conclusion: 研究成功建立了等离子体性质的量子统计描述框架，但指出仍需进一步工作以实现热密等离子体性质的一致描述，特别是在扩展维里展开的应用范围和提升理论模型的完备性方面。

Abstract: Expressions for the thermodynamic and transport properties of plasmas are derived from quantum statistics in the form of equilibrium correlation functions. These can be evaluated using analytical methods or numerical approaches such as DFT-MD or PIMC simulations. Virial expansions are obtained using the Green's function method. They provide benchmarks for numerical simulations and are useful in the low-density range. The results for the equation of state are discussed for the uniform electron gas and the hydrogen plasma. Transport properties such as the dielectric function are also of interest. Virial expansions are considered for the electrical direct current conductivity as a special case of the dielectric function. Examples are given and it is explained where further work is needed to obtain a consistent description of the properties of hot and dense plasmas.

### [5] [Experimental evidence for coronal mass ejection suppression in strong stellar magnetic fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16156)
*S. N. Chen,K. Burdonov,W. Yao,J. D. Alvarado-Gómez,C. Argiroffi,J. Béard,S. Bolanõs,R. Bonito,A. Ciardi,O. Cohen,J. J. Drake,S. Orlando,J. Fuchs*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究通过天体物理模拟、高能激光驱动等离子体实验和3D磁流体动力学建模，揭示了强恒星磁场如何完全抑制日冕物质抛射传播的物理机制。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 恒星日冕物质抛射在观测中很少被检测到，这可能是因为较太阳更强的恒星磁场阻止了CME的形成和逃逸。研究旨在验证这一假设。

Method: 结合天体物理模拟、高能激光驱动等离子体的缩比实验以及3D磁流体动力学模型进行综合分析。实验室中模拟了恒星CME条件下的等离子体流动。

Result: 在100 G恒星偶极场中，低等离子体β的CME被磁约束限制。实验表明，在约30 G等效场中，等离子体流可以自由传播，但在300 kG（100 G等效）强场中，流动变得不稳定并完全停止。数值模拟显示流动的突然中断是由扭曲不稳定性引起的。

Conclusion: 强恒星磁场能够完全抑制CME传播，这为恒星观测中缺失CME提供了物理解释，并强调了磁约束在恒星演化和系外行星空间天气中的重要作用。

Abstract: Solar coronal mass ejections (CME) are routinely observed, but as of yet there exist few convincing detections of stellar CMEs. A reason for this could be the stronger magnetic fields of these stars, compared to that of our Sun, would prevent CME to form and escape. Here we combined astrophysical simulations, measurements of scaled high-energy laser-driven plasma flows, and 3D magneto-hydrodynamic modeling to test this hypothesis. Simulations show that in a 100 G stellar dipole field, low-plasma beta CMEs become magnetically confined. In the laboratory, a laser-produced plasma stream scaled to stellar CME conditions propagates freely at low applied magnetic fields (approximately 30 G stellar equivalent) but becomes unstable and halts entirely when the field is increased to 3e5 G (i.e., a 100 G equivalent). Numerical simulations suggest that the sudden disruption of the flow is induced by a kink instability. These results provide the first laboratory-scale evidence that strong stellar magnetic fields can fully suppress CME propagation, offering a physical explanation for their lack in stellar observations and highlighting the role of magnetic confinement in stellar evolution and exoplanet space weather.

</details>
