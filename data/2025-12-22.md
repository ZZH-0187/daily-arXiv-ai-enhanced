### [1] [Asymptotic state of nonlinear Landau damping in one-dimensional plasma](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17269)
*Yifei Ouyang,Ping Zhu,Chung-Sang Ng*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 非线性朗道阻尼渐近态的研究：通过准线性模型和二阶辛积分器，发现碰撞less等离子体的渐近态是多波BGK结构，相空间中表现为多个涡旋结构，对应电场频谱中的多个峰值。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究一维等离子体中非线性朗道阻尼的渐近状态，探索稳态解的特性和相空间结构，揭示非线性演化过程中的物理机制。

Method: 采用准线性模型和二阶辛积分器进行数值模拟，将稳态解的平稳分布函数的色散关系扩展到复平面，并与非线性模拟结果进行对比分析。

Result: 确定碰撞less等离子体的渐近态是多波BGK结构，该结构在相空间中表现为多个涡旋，对应电场频谱中的多个峰值。

Conclusion: 非线性朗道阻尼的渐近状态是复杂的多波BGK结构，相空间中的涡旋结构反映了等离子体在非线性演化过程中的稳定状态特性。

Abstract: In this work, the asymptotic state of nonlinear Landau damping in one-dimensional plasma has been examined using a quasi-linear model and a second-order symplectic integrator. The dispersion relation of the plateau distribution function for the steady-state solution of the quasi-linear mode is extended to the complex plane and compared with the nonlinear simulation. We determine that the asymptotic state of the collisionless plasma is a multi-wave BGK structure. This structure is characterized by multiple vortices in phase space, which correspond to distinct peaks in the frequency-wavenumber (ω, k) spectrum of the electric field

### [2] [Influence of plasma shaping on the parity of core-localized toroidal Alfvén eigenmode in an advanced tokamak configuration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17337)
*Shiwei Xue,Ping Zhu,Haolong Li*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 本文研究了环形阿尔文本征模（TAEs）和高能粒子模（EPMs）在具有反磁剪切的先进托卡马克位形中的激发和特性。模拟发现，当等离子体呈拉长非圆二维形状时，主导TAE/EPM呈现奇宇称和反气球结构；当形状更趋圆形时，宇称转为偶宇称并呈现气球结构。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在托卡马克中，辅助加热和聚变产生的α粒子可能激发TAEs和EPMs，这些模的不稳定性可能影响高能粒子约束。研究不同等离子体形状下这些模的宇称和结构特性，有助于解释实际装置中的观测结果。

Method: 使用NIMROD代码中的混合动力学-MHD模型，模拟了具有反磁剪切核心区域的先进托卡马克位形中TAEs和EPMs的激发，分析了不同等离子体形状（拉长非圆形vs圆形）对模宇称和结构的影响。

Result: 拉长非圆二维形状下：主导TAE/EPM呈现奇宇称和反气球结构。当形状变圆、拉长比减小时：宇称转为偶宇称，结构变为气球结构。

Conclusion: 等离子体形状是决定TAE/EPMs宇称和结构的关键因素，这些发现有助于解释不同等离子体形状的先进托卡马克装置中观测到的主导宇称模式。

Abstract: Toroidal Alfvén eigenmodes (TAEs) and energetic particle modes (EPMs) can both be excited by energetic particles from auxiliary heating and fusion-born alpha particles in a tokamak. Using the hybrid kinetic-MHD model implemented in the NIMROD code, the excitation of these modes and their properties are investigated in an advanced tokamak configuration with reversed magnetic shear in the core region. The dominant TAE/EPM is found to exhibit odd parity with an anti-ballooning structure when the plasma has elongated, non-circular two-dimensional shaping. As the plasma shaping becomes more circular with reduced elongation, the mode parity undergoes a transition to even parity accompanied by a ballooning structure. These results may help explain the dominant parity of TAE/EPMs observed in advanced tokamak configurations with different plasma shaping.

### [3] [H-mode transition in the TJ-II stellarator plasmas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17522)
*T. Estrada,C. Hidalgo*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: TJ-II装置中L-H转换的触发机制与脉动E×B流的关联性比稳态径向电场更强，表明带状流在转换中起关键作用；湍流抑制不仅发生在势垒区域，也出现在E×B剪切较弱或无剪切的更宽径向范围，说明存在局部剪切流之外的湍流抑制机制。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自2008年在TJ-II等离子体中首次观察到H模转换以来，为更好地理解约束转换物理机制开展了大量实验研究。本文旨在综述TJ-II中L-H转换的主要发现，包括径向电场的驱动机制、湍流抑制的可能机制及相关时空尺度的影响。

Method: 通过TJ-II实验装置的系统实验研究，分析L-H转换过程中径向电场、E×B流和湍流的演化特性，重点关注湍流与流之间的捕食-猎物关系及其对转换的介导作用。

Result: TJ-II等离子体中L-H转换的触发与脉动E×B流的发展关联性更强，而非稳态径向电场效应，表明带状流在转换过程中发挥关键作用。首次在TJ-II中发现的湍流与流之间捕食-猎物关系的实验证据支持这一结论。此外，转换时湍流输运的抑制不仅发生在势垒区域，也出现在E×B流剪切较弱甚至为零的更宽径向范围。

Conclusion: TJ-II中L-H转换主要由脉动E×B流（带状流）驱动，而非稳态径向电场效应。湍流抑制机制不仅限于局部剪切流的作用，还存在其他物理机制在更宽的径向范围内发挥作用，这对全面理解约束转换物理具有重要意义。

Abstract: Since the first H-mode transitions were observed in TJ-II plasmas in 2008, an extensive experimental effort has been done aiming a better physics understanding of confinement transitions. In this paper, an overview of the main findings related to the L-H transition in TJ-II is presented including how the radial electric field is driven, which are the possible mechanisms for turbulence suppression, and what are the related temporal and spatial scales which can impact the transition. The trigger of the L-H transition in TJ-II plasmas is found to be more correlated with the development of fluctuating $E\times B$ flows than with steady-state $E_r$ effects, pointing to the role played by zonal flows in mediating the transition. Experimental evidence supporting the predator-prey relationship between turbulence and flows as the basis for the L-H transition, found for the first time in TJ-II, reinforces this conclusion. Besides, the reduction in the turbulent transport at the transition is detected at the barrier region but also in a wider radial range with weak or even zero $E\times B$ flow shear, what points to other mechanisms beyond the turbulence suppression by local sheared flows.

</details>
