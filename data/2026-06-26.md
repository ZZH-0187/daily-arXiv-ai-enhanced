The extracted longitudinal and transverse wake functions and corresponding beam impedances show that the dominant deflecting wakefield can be substantially reduced without significantly degrading the longitudinal wakefield. These results demonstrate the reliability and applicability of the proposed method for complex dielectric-loaded structures.
