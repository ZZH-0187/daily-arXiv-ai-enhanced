### [1] [Dephasingless laser wakefield acceleration in a plasma waveguide](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20298)
*J. P. Palastro,K. G. Miller,C. D. Arrowsmith,R. Almeida,M. R. Edwards,A. L. Elliott,A. Kiewel,A. Konzel,L. S. Mack,D. Ramsey,D. Singh,A. G. R. Thomas,J. Vieira*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 利用等离子体波导中的时空结构化激光脉冲驱动光速尾波场，解决激光尾波场加速器的失相问题，相比飞焦方法减小等离子体体积，实现能量增益与模式数线性增长。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 激光尾波场加速器虽然能提供极大的加速梯度，使电子加速器和光子源更紧凑，但受到失相限制，即捕获电子超过尾波场加速相。飞焦脉冲虽然能通过驱动真空光速尾波场消除失相，但存在光斑尺寸变化、持续时间长或所需等离子体体积大等缺点。

Method: 通过在等离子体波导中叠加适当选择频率的等离子体波导模式，构造具有恒定光斑尺寸和超短持续时间的时空结构化激光脉冲，驱动真空光速尾波场。使用标度定律和准三维粒子-单元模拟进行分析验证。

Result: 与飞焦方法相比，波导方法显著减少了所需等离子体体积。标度定律和准三维粒子-单元模拟表明，单级能量增益与用于构造脉冲的模式数呈线性关系，能够实现比标准激光尾波场加速器更大的能量增益或更短的加速级。

Conclusion: 该研究提出了一种在等离子体波导中利用时空结构化激光脉冲驱动光速速度尾波场的方法，这种方法相比飞焦方法能大幅减小所需等离子体体积，并使得单级能量增益与用于构建脉冲的模式数呈线性关系，为实现更高能量增益或更短加速级提供了可能。

Abstract: Laser wakefield accelerators (LWFAs) provide extremely large accelerating gradients for compact electron accelerators and photon sources but are limited by dephasing, where trapped electrons outrun the accelerating phase of the wakefield. Flying-focus pulses can eliminate dephasing by driving a wake at the vacuum speed of light, but these pulses involve tradeoffs such as varying spot size, long duration, or large plasma volume. Here we show that a spatiotemporally structured laser pulse propagating in a plasma waveguide can drive a wakefield at the vacuum speed of light while maintaining a constant spot size and ultrashort duration. The pulse is formed by superposing plasma-waveguide modes with appropriately selected frequencies. Compared with flying-focus approaches, the waveguide substantially reduces the required plasma volume. Scaling laws and quasi-3D particle-in-cell simulations show that the single-stage energy gain increases linearly with the number of modes used to construct the pulse, enabling larger energy gains or shorter stages than standard LWFA.

### [2] [Bayesian optimization of stellarator alpha-particle confinement using data-informed parameter spaces and dimensionality reduction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19523)
*Matt Landreman,Michael Czekanski,Andrew Giuliani,Byoungchan Jang,Rory Conlin*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 提出了用于恒星器优化的两种新参数空间方法：基于量变换和基于PCA+量变换，将参数自然地约束在[0,1]区间，解决了传统傅里叶参数化中边界设置的难题，并通过贝叶斯优化获得了具有优异快粒子约束的配置。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当前恒星器设计通常通过优化等离子体边界表面的形状进行，参数通常采用傅里叶幅度。但优化算法（如贝叶斯方法）需要参数边界约束，且当各参数缩放相似时效率最高。传统傅里叶参数化存在边界设置困难的问题：宽边界会导致自相交边界和MHD平衡计算失败，而紧边界又限制了表达能力。

Method: 提出了两种新的参数空间构建方法：1）对每个傅里叶自由度应用量变换，将数据分布映射到单位区间上的均匀分布；2）对边界点应用主成分分析（PCA），再进行量变换。两种方法都将变换后的变量作为自由度，自然地约束在[0,1]区间内。通过异步并行化的贝叶斯优化框架，使用导心跟踪在优化循环内部评估α粒子约束。

Result: 提出的两种方法成功实现了在[0,1]区间内自然约束的参数空间，其中PCA方法还具有维度缩减的优势，能在少量参数下保持高表达能力。通过异步并行贝叶斯优化结合导心跟踪，获得了具有优异快粒子约束的恒星器配置，这些配置场可以远离准对称或准等动力学特性。

Conclusion: 本文提出了两种新的参数空间用于恒星器优化，通过量变换或PCA加量变换的方法，将参数自然地约束在[0,1]区间，解决了传统傅里叶参数化中边界设置困难的问题。这些方法不仅提供了自然的边界约束，还能在少量参数下保持高表达能力，在异步并行化的贝叶斯优化框架中证明了其有效性，成功获得了具有优异快粒子约束的恒星器配置。

Abstract: Modern stellarators are typically designed by optimizing the shape of the plasma boundary surface, with the parameters taken to be Fourier amplitudes. Many promising optimization algorithms such as Bayesian methods require bound constraints on the parameters and are most efficient when each parameter is scaled similarly to the others. With the typical Fourier parameterization, it is unclear how to set these bounds: wide constraints lead to self-intersecting boundaries and frequent failures of the MHD equilibrium calculation, while tight bound constraints limit expressiveness. To address these issues, here we propose two new parameter spaces for stellarator optimization. Both begin with a dataset of existing stellarator boundaries. In the first approach, a quantile transformation is applied to each Fourier degree of freedom, mapping the data distribution to a uniform distribution on the unit interval. In the second approach, principal component analysis (PCA) is applied to points on the boundaries, followed by a quantile transformation. For both approaches, the transformed variables become the degrees of freedom, naturally bounded to [0, 1]. The PCA method has the additional benefit of dimensionality reduction, with high expressiveness for a small number of parameters. The methods are demonstrated via Bayesian optimization for good alpha-particle confinement with guiding-center tracing inside the optimization loop, using asynchronous parallelization. These optimizations yield stellarator configurations with excellent fast-particle confinement in fields that can be far from quasisymmetric or quasi-isodynamic.
