### [1] [Plasma oscillations within Israel-Stewart theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14344)
*Lorenzo Gavassino*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 研究发现，在带电粒子动力学理论中，零波数下非流体动力学频率可以通过电阻性磁流体动力学中以色列-斯图尔特弛豫方程与安培-麦克斯韦定律的相互作用产生振荡模式，这种振荡对应等离子体振荡。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统上认为不带电动力学理论中的非流体动力学频率在零波数下是纯虚数，但最近发现在电阻性磁流体动力学中会出现振荡的非流体动力学模式，本文旨在详细分析这一现象。

Method: 首先证明这些振荡模式是德鲁德模型的精确解，对应普通等离子体振荡；然后应用昂萨格-卡西米尔原理解释其振荡性质反映了自由度在PT变换下的不同性质；最后建立理论框架说明在带电粒子动力学理论中每个空间维度最多只能存在一对这样的振荡模式。

Result: 研究发现振荡模式反映了电场和分布函数在宇称-时间反演变换下的反对称性质，分布函数是偶函数而电场是奇函数；每个空间维度最多只能存在一对振荡的非流体动力学模式，其余模式仍位于虚轴上。

Conclusion: 带电粒子动力学理论在零波数下可以存在振荡的非流体动力学模式，但仅限于每个空间维度的一对模式，这为理解等离子体振荡的物理本质提供了新的理论框架。

Abstract: It is well known that, at zero wavenumber, the non-hydrodynamic frequencies of uncharged kinetic theory are purely imaginary. On the other hand, it was recently shown that, in resistive magnetohydrodynamics, the interplay between the Israel-Stewart relaxation equation and the Ampère-Maxwell law can give rise to a pair of oscillating non-hydrodynamic modes. In this work, we analyze this phenomenon in detail. We first demonstrate that these oscillatory modes are exact solutions of the Drude model, corresponding to ordinary plasma oscillations. We then invoke the Onsager-Casimir principle to explain that their oscillatory nature reflects the distinct PT-transformation properties of the degrees of freedom: the distribution function is even, while the electric field is odd. Finally, we establish that, in a kinetic theory of charged particles, there can be at most one such pair of oscillatory modes per spatial dimension, while all other modes still must sit on the imaginary axis.

### [2] [Real-time time-dependent density functional theory for high-energy density physics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14643)
*Alina Kononov,Minh Nguyen,Andrew D. Baczewski*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该论文综述了实时含时密度泛函理论(TDDFT)在高能量密度系统中的应用，包括计算方法教程和未来发展方向的探讨


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 高能量密度系统的电子响应特性对行星结构、聚变靶演化和实验室天体物理诊断至关重要，需要精确的建模框架

Method: 使用实时含时密度泛函理论(TDDFT)，能够准确预测HED材料的动态响应，包括自由-自由、束缚-自由和束缚-束缚贡献，无需特殊态划分

Result: 实时TDDFT提供了一个通用的建模框架，能够在线性响应区域及之外捕获集体和非集体行为，并计算动态结构因子、电导率和阻止本领等相关响应特性

Conclusion: 实时TDDFT是服务高能量密度科学的一种强大计算方法，论文提供了进一步发展的途径

Abstract: Electronic response properties of high-energy density (HED) systems influence planetary structure, drive evolution of fusion targets, and underpin diagnostics in laboratory astrophysics. Real-time time-dependent density functional theory (TDDFT) offers a versatile modeling framework capable of accurately predicting the dynamic response of HED materials -- including free-free, bound-free, and bound-bound contributions without requiring ad hoc state partitioning; capturing both collective and non-collective behavior; and applicable within the linear-response regime and beyond. We review the theoretical formalism of real-time TDDFT as applied to HED systems, provide a practical tutorial for computing relevant response properties (dynamic structure factors, conductivity, and stopping power), and comment on avenues for further development of this powerful computational method in service of HED science.
