### [1] [Self-subsidizing Mercury Remediation with Fusion Reactors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02590)
*J. F. Parisi,J. Azad*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 论文提出利用聚变反应堆的中子将汞不可逆地转化为稳定黄金，实现永久性汞污染修复并创造高经济价值。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 环境关切是低碳能源部署的重大障碍，聚变能通过整合污染修复从根本上改善政策平衡；同时汞转化为黄金的巨大经济价值可以刺激汞回收，实现环境与经济双赢。

Method: 采用高能中子照射（可通过μ子催化聚变和惯性约束聚变实现）引发汞同位素的(n,2n)核反应，最终将其衰变为稳定黄金同位素。

Result: 在高中子通量下，所有汞同位素都可通过核反应转化为黄金；全球可回收汞的总价值约200万亿美元，远超地下黄金储量；协同生产黄金可使聚变电站收入提高三倍。

Conclusion: 聚变能不仅是环境友好的，而且通过连续消耗汞污染物实现

Abstract: Fusion reactors can permanently remediate mercury by using it as a neutron multiplier: each (n,2n) reaction reduces the neutron number towards ${}^{197}$Hg which quickly decays into stable gold, irreversibly removing it from the environment while generating substantial economic value. Fusion energy is therefore not merely environmentally benign, but anti-polluting through the continuous consumption of an environmental pollutant. The history of nuclear fission demonstrates that environmental concerns can be decisive obstacles to low-carbon power deployment, suggesting that integrated pollution remediation fundamentally improves the policy calculus for fusion energy. We show that at high neutron flux (achievable in muon-catalyzed and inertial confinement fusion), nuclear reactions make all mercury isotopes eligible for gold transmutation, incentivizing mercury recovery and valuing the world mercury extractable stock at ${\sim}\$200$ trillion, exceeding all in-ground gold reserves. Co-producing gold alongside electricity can triple a fusion plant's revenue, aligning economic incentives with complete, permanent mercury remediation.

### [2] [Collimation of diamagnetic laser-driven plasma outflows by an ambient magnetic-pressure gradient](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02704)
*Yigeng Tian,Chung Hei Leung,Arijit Bose,Riddhi Bandyopadhyay,Michael A. Shay,William H. Matthaeus*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 磁场增强对激光驱动等离子体射流径向聚焦效應的研究


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 模拟日冕开放磁场中等离子体射流，研究外部磁场对激光驱动等离子体膨胀的聚焦作用

Method: 使用FLASH代码进行磁流体动力学模拟，包含非理想项(电阻率、Biermann电池效应、Nernst对流)，模拟CH靶受351nm激光(5kJ/10ns)驱动，背景磁场0-50T

Result: 膨胀等离子体形成中心低密度抗磁性空腔，被高磁压壳层包围；磁场通量从中心对流到边缘，方位抗磁性电流减小腔内磁场、放大腔外磁场，产生径向磁压力梯度，通过J×B力径向约束流动；聚焦效应随外加磁场增强而加强

Conclusion: 增强外部磁场能有效提高等离子体射流的径向聚焦效果，这是通过降低等离子体β参数和增强J×B约束力实现的

Abstract: We present magnetohydrodynamic simulations of laser driven plasma outflows propagating along an externally applied poloidal magnetic field, designed to mimic coronal open-field plasma jets. Using the FLASH code with non-ideal terms (resistivity, Biermann battery, and Nernst advection) included, we model a CH target driven by a 3$ω$ (351 nm) beam delivering 5 kJ over 10 ns and a uniform background field $\text{B}_0$ = 0 to 50 T. Under these conditions, the expanding plume develops a central low-density diamagnetic cavity bounded by a high-magnetic-pressure shell. Magnetic flux is advected from the plume center to its edge, and azimuthal diamagnetic currents form that decrease fields inside the cavity and amplify fields outside, producing a radial magnetic-pressure gradient that exerts an inward $\text{J}\times \text{B}$ force and radially confines the flow. We show that the collimation strengthens with increasing applied magnetic field, as stronger fields reduce the plasma $β$ and correspondingly enhance the confining $\text{J}\times \text{B}$ force.

### [3] [Finite Ion Temperature Effects on the Merging of Current-Carrying ELM Filaments in the edge region of a tokamak](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03089)
*Souvik Mondal,Nirmal Bisai,Abhijit Sen,Indranil Bandyopadhyay*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 研究表明，有限离子温度显著改变ELM-like细丝的传播与相互作用，延迟合并过程，并将径向动能转为涡旋运动，从而减少径向输运


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: ELM细丝对托卡马克边缘的跨场输运至关重要，但现有模型常采用冷离子近似，而实验数据显示离子温度接近电子温度（Ti~Te），因此需要研究有限离子温度对ELM细丝动力学的影响

Method: 采用归一化的三维流体模型，研究有限离子温度对单向电流携带的ELM-like细丝动力学的影响，包括单细丝动力学和多细丝相互作用的系统分析

Result: 增加离子温度显著改变细丝传播和相互作用：1）延迟细丝合并过程 2）形成不对称电位结构、强极向流和持续性旋转运动 3）将动能源从径向传播重新分配到涡旋动力学 4）存在从径向主导到旋转主导行为的明显转变

Conclusion: 有限离子温度通过将动能转为涡旋运动而减少径向输运并延迟细丝合并，强调在ELM细丝动力学和边缘等离子体输运建模中必须考虑离子温度效应

Abstract: Edge-localized-mode (ELM) filaments are crucial for cross-field transport at the tokamak edge; yet, their dynamics are often analyzed using the cold-ion approximation, despite experimental data indicating that Ti~Te . This study employs a normalized three-dimensional fluid model to investigate the influence of finite ion temperature on the dynamics of unidirectional current-carrying ELM-like filaments. We demonstrate that increasing ion temperature substantially alters filament propagation and interaction, resulting in a delay of filament merging despite an increase in total kinetic energy due to a stronger pressure-gradient drive. The examination of single-filament dynamics indicates that finite ion temperature generates asymmetric potential structures, strong poloidal flows, and persistent rotational motion, which channel kinetic energy from radial propagation into vortical dynamics. A comprehensive examination of the ion-to-electron temperature ratio reveals a distinct transition from radially dominated to rotation-dominated behavior as ion temperature increases. These results provide a unified physical explanation for reduced radial transport and delayed merging in the warm-ion domain, emphasizing the necessity of incorporating ion temperature effects in the modeling of ELM filament dynamics and edge plasma transport.
