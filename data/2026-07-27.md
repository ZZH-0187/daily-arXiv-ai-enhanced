### [1] [A Multi-Species Reactive-Boltzmann Formulation for Self-Consistent Kinetic Simulation of Burning Fusion Plasmas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21723)
*Mark Dunn,Jingwei Hu,Uri Shumlak*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究开发了一个多物种反应-弹性动理学框架，用于评估核聚变等离子体中反应物离子分布偏离麦克斯韦分布的可能性，特别关注聚变产物加热是否会产生超热离子群。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 国家点火装置(NIF)燃烧等离子体实验中，实验数据与辐射流体力学模型之间存在差异，部分原因可能来自反应物离子分布偏离麦克斯韦平衡分布。特别是假设聚变产物与主体等离子体的碰撞弛豫可能产生简化模型未捕捉到的超热离子群。

Method: 开发了一个多物种反应-弹性动理学框架，其中聚变源和汇直接从反应物速度分布函数中通过反应性玻尔兹曼碰撞算子评估。弹性相互作用在小角度碰撞极限下使用Landau和Lenard-Bernstein碰撞算子建模，采用快速傅里叶谱方法离散化积分-微分碰撞算子。

Result: 数值实验在弱耦合、早期时间状态下对空间均匀的二物种和三物种D-D聚变系统进行。研究发现聚变产物加热并没有在反应物离子中产生明显的超热离子群：偏离相应麦克斯韦分布的偏差在Frobenius范数和聚变反应性方面都保持很小。

Conclusion: 聚变产物加热不太可能导致NIF实验中观察到与模型差异的主要驱动因素。反应物离子分布基本保持接近麦克斯韦分布，简化模型中对超热离子的假设可能不成立。

Abstract: Discrepancies between experimental data and radiation-hydrodynamic models of burning plasmas at the National Ignition Facility have been attributed, in part, to possible deviations of reactant ion distributions from Maxwellian equilibrium. In particular, it has been hypothesized that the collisional relaxation of energetic fusion products with the bulk plasma may generate suprathermal ion populations not captured by reduced models. To assess this hypothesis in a fully kinetic setting, we present a multi-species reactive--elastic kinetic framework, in which fusion sources and sinks are evaluated directly from the reactant velocity distribution function with a reactive Boltzmann collision operator. For computational efficiency, elastic interactions are modeled in the small-angle collision limit using the Landau and Lenard--Bernstein collision operators, although the framework admits large-angle elastic scattering generalizations. The integro-differential collision operators are discretized with a fast Fourier spectral method, enabling efficient, high-order accuracy evaluation of reactive processes in three-dimensional velocity space and providing a deterministic alternative to Monte Carlo collision methods. Numerical experiments are performed for spatially homogeneous two- and three-species D--D fusion systems in weakly coupled, early-time regimes. We find that fusion product heating does not generate a significant suprathermal population in the reactant ions: deviation from the corresponding Maxwellian distribution remains small in both the Frobenius norm and the fusion reactivity.

### [2] [Full 3D Maxwell solver for Inertial Electrostatic Confinement devices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22253)
*Victor-Otto de Haan,Niels Geerits*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 介绍了全三维电磁等离子体聚变模拟器的研究结果，该工具能研究结构化电磁势、反常磁矩或电荷团簇结构对聚变产额的影响。模拟器计算的聚变产额与实际聚变装置结果相似，离子能量和密度模拟准确。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 开发一个能够准确模拟三维电磁等离子体聚变过程的工具，研究复杂电磁效应（如结构化电磁势、反常磁矩、电荷团簇结构）对聚变产额的影响，以增进对聚变物理的理解并为实验设计提供指导。

Method: 采用全三维电磁等离子体聚变模拟器，包含完整的麦克斯韦求解器来计算离子属性。该模拟器能够处理复杂的电磁结构和效应，包括结构化电磁势、反常磁矩和电荷团簇结构。

Result: 模拟器计算的聚变产额与实际聚变装置中的结果相当接近，离子能量和密度的模拟也非常准确。由于离子属性由完整的麦克斯韦求解器产生，这部分模拟具有充分的准确性证据。

Conclusion: 该三维电磁等离子体聚变模拟器能够准确模拟聚变过程，为研究复杂电磁效应对聚变产额的影响提供了有效工具。结论建议继续推进这方面的研究工作。

Abstract: Results of a full 3D electromagnetic plasma fusion simulator are presented. It will enable investigations into the influence of structured electromagnetic potentials, anomalous magnetic moments, or charge cluster structures on the fusion yield. The simulator calculates fusion yields similar to those found in real world fusors. In addition, the ion energy and density are accurately simulated. Given that the ion properties arise from a full Maxwell solver there is ample evidence that this part of the simulation is accurate. Concluded with a recommendation for continued research.

### [3] [First Observation of Fishbone-Driven Zonal Flows with Fine Reversed Structure in Tokamak Plasmas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22344)
*Liutian Gao,Yuehao Ma,Huishan Cai,Adi Liu,Bin Zhang,Ming Xu,Haiqing Liu,Liqing Xu,Chu Zhou,Feifei Long,Mingyuan Wang,Xiaoming Zhong,Jinlin Xie,Ge Zhuang,the EAST team*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 首次实验观测EAST托卡马克中鱼骨模驱动的带状流，发现其具有精细径向反转结构，推翻传统理论预测的热粒子排出机制，揭示热离子与电子贡献抵消的新机制。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 鱼骨模（fishbone）不稳定性对托卡马克等离子体约束有重要影响，但传统理论认为其通过热粒子排出机制产生全局流模式。本文旨在通过实验直接观测鱼骨模驱动流，验证理论预测并探索其真实物理机制。

Method: 1) 在EAST托卡马克中进行直接实验观测；2) 测量鱼骨模驱动流的时空演化特性；3) 结合全局非线性回运动力学模拟，定量再现观测结果；4) 分析热离子和电子各自的理论贡献。

Result: 观测到与理论预测相反的精细径向反转流结构（在q=1有理面内）；发现流比鱼骨模增长更快、饱和更早；模拟揭示此结构源于热离子和电子贡献的抵消，这是之前理论框架未能捕捉的机制。

Conclusion: 鱼骨模能产生具有独特径向拓扑的剪切流，为调控湍流、改善芯部约束提供了新途径。研究颠覆了传统理论框架，揭示了热离子-电子抵消机制的重要性。

Abstract: We present the first direct experimental observation of fishbone-driven zonal flows in the core of the EAST tokamak. In contrast to the global pattern predicted by previous models and simulations based on the energetic-particle-expulsion mechanism, the observed flows exhibit a fine-scale, radially reversed structure inside the q = 1 rational surface. The flow rises faster and saturates earlier than the fishbone within a single burst, indicating that a beat-driven nonlinear process dominates the early stage rather than the energetic-particle-expulsion mechanism. Global nonlinear gyrokinetic simulations quantitatively reproduce the observed radial profile and reveal that this structure arises from the cancellation of comparable but opposite contributions from thermal ions and electrons. This cancellation mechanism is not captured in previous theoretical frameworks. These findings establish that the fishbone can generate sheared flows with a distinct radial topology, offering a promising pathway for regulating turbulence and improving core confinement.

### [4] [Robustness of Off-Axis Electron Vortices in Nonuniform Magnetic Fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22315)
*Hui-Dong Huang,Qi Meng,Zhi-Bin Wang,Liang Lu,Jian Chen,Li-Ping Zou*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 论文揭示了离轴电子涡旋在轴对称磁场中的轨道角动量守恒机制


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的旋转对称性只能保护轴上电子涡旋的拓扑电荷，但不能保护离轴涡旋。需要寻找新的守恒量来解释离轴电子涡旋在轴对称磁场中的动力学行为

Method: 识别了SU(1,1)动力学不变量，保证了离轴电子涡旋内在轨道角动量在近轴近似下的守恒，并通过第一性原理模拟验证了这一预测

Result: SU(1,1)动力学不变量的发现解释了离轴电子涡旋轨道角动量的守恒机制，模拟确认了一个离轴电子涡旋穿透Glaser透镜时满足这一守恒规律

Conclusion: 在轴对称非均匀磁场中存在稳健的输运机制，SU(1,1)动力学不变量为离轴电子涡旋的轨道角动量守恒提供了理论基础

Abstract: Rotational symmetry protects the topological charge of on-axis electron vortices but not of off-axis vortices. We identify an additional SU(1,1) dynamical invariant that guarantees conservation of their intrinsic orbital angular momentum within the near-axis approximation. First-principles simulations of an off-axis electron vortex traversing a Glaser lens confirm this prediction, establishing a robust transport mechanism in axisymmetric nonuniform magnetic fields.

### [5] [Generalized Theory of Blowout-Regime Plasma Wakefields via a Lagrangian Formulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22473)
*Y. Kang,P. Manwani,S. S. Baturin,G. Andonian,B. Naranjo,J. B. Rosenzweig*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 该研究扩展了等离子体尾波场加速中的blowout机制理论，超越了传统的轴对称限制，开发了适用于非对称横向发射度扁平束的自洽理论框架。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有等离子体尾波场加速理论主要针对轴对称情况，无法完全描述未来直线对撞机所需的扁平束（具有高度不对称横向发射度）的特性。扁平束对于控制亮度和束辐射至关重要，因此需要超越轴对称极限的理论来描述非对称束驱动的尾波场。

Method: 采用简化的拉格朗日公式化方法，将现有理论从轴对称限制推广到非对称情况，推导出描述非对称束驱动尾波势的耦合方程组。该理论自洽地预测了聚焦场及其沿共动坐标的变化，以及加速场及其横向非均匀性。

Result: 理论预测与粒子胞内模拟结果进行了基准测试，验证了该理论在描述和解释非对称源驱动尾波场方面的准确性，展示了理论预测与模拟结果的良好一致性。

Conclusion: 成功开发了超越轴对称限制的等离子体尾波场加速blowout机制理论，为未来需要非对称扁平束的直线对撞机应用提供了更准确的理论框架，能够自洽描述非对称束驱动的尾波场特性。

Abstract: The blowout regime of plasma wakefield acceleration is a strongly nonlinear plasma response in which an intense relativistic beam or laser pulse expels plasma electrons forming a nearly uniform ion column. Existing self-consistent theories describe this structure primarily in the axisymmetric limit, reducing the bubble dynamics to a single radial degree of freedom. This restriction is inadequate for flat beams with highly asymmetric transverse emittances, as required in future linear colliders to control luminosity and beamstrahlung. Using a reduced Lagrangian formulation, we generalize the existing theory beyond axisymmetry and derive a coupled system of equations governing the wake potential driven by an asymmetric beam. This theory self-consistently predicts the focusing fields and their variation along the co-moving coordinate, as well as the accelerating field and its transverse nonuniformity. The resulting theoretical predictions are benchmarked against particle-in-cell simulations, demonstrating the theory's ability to describe and interpret wakefields driven by nonaxisymmetric sources.

</details>
