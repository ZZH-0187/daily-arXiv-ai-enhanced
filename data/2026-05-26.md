### [3] [Impact of non-equilibrium radiation in a high-enthalpy inductively coupled plasma wind tunnel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25060)
*Sanjeev Kumar,Sung Min Jo,Alessandro Munafò,Marco Panesi*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 本研究开发了一个多物理场框架来量化350 kW ICP风洞中辐射冷却效应，结果显示辐射损失具有显著的压力依赖性，尤其是在高压下成为主要能量耗散机制，大气压下损失可达输入功率的32%（氮）和22%（空气）。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 目前对ICP风洞中辐射传热的研究常常被忽略或采用简化的光学薄假设进行建模，并且非平衡辐射对等离子体动力学的影响仍然量化不足。需要系统地研究辐射冷却效应。

Method: 采用松散耦合的多物理场框架，将磁流体动力学等离子体框架与光谱辐射传输求解器自洽耦合，无需使用光学薄或经验模型。

Result: 结果表明，辐射损失呈现出强烈的压力依赖性：在低压下辐射可以忽略，但在高压下成为主要能量耗散途径。在大气压条件下，氮气和空气等离子体的辐射损失分别占输入功率的约32%和22%，导致核心等离子体温度显著降低。氮气等离子体的辐射损失始终高于空气等离子体。构建了辐射热损失相对于输入功率的压力-功率图，以量化综合操作效应并为设施运行和建模提供指导。

Conclusion: 研究发现，即使是高能条件下的Plasmatron X等离子体炬，在最高功率和压力下仍主要处于光学薄状态，表明光学薄近似可能适用于大多数运行条件。研究的框架为评估其他ICP设施中的辐射效应提供了可靠工具。

Abstract: High-power inductively coupled plasma (ICP) wind tunnels are widely used to reproduce high-enthalpy environments relevant to atmospheric entry and hypersonic testing. Despite their importance, radiative heat transfer in ICP facilities is commonly neglected or modeled using simplified optically thin assumptions, and the impact of non-equilibrium radiation on plasma dynamics remains poorly quantified. In this work, a loosely coupled, multi-physics framework is developed to systematically investigate radiative cooling effects in the 350 kW Plasmatron X facility at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. The approach self-consistently couples a magnetohydrodynamic plasma framework with a spectral radiative transport solver, eliminating the need for optically thin or empirical models. Simulations are performed for nitrogen and air plasmas over a wide range of operating pressures (1-101 kPa) and powers (100-350 kW). The results reveal a strong pressure dependence of radiative losses, with radiation contributing negligibly at low pressures, but becoming a dominant energy sink at elevated pressures. At atmospheric pressure, radiative losses account for up to approximately 32% and 22% of the input power for nitrogen and air plasmas, respectively, leading to substantial reductions in core plasma temperatures. Nitrogen plasmas consistently exhibit higher radiative losses than air as a result of increased concentrations of radiatively active species and higher electron number densities. Pressure-power maps of radiative heat loss relative to input power are constructed to quantify combined operating effects and to provide guidance for facility operation and modeling fidelity. Finally, an assessment of self-absorption demonstrates that the Plasmatron X torch operates predominantly in an optically thin regime, even at the highest power and pressure conditions considered.
