### [1] [The Memory Scaling of Reverse-Mode Differentiation in Particle Accelerator Simulations with Space Charge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27282)
*Arjun Dhamrait,Edoardo Zoni,Axel Huebl,Ji Qiang,Chad E. Mitchell,Ryan Roussel,Jan Kaiser,Chenran Xu,Jean-Luc Vay,Remi Lehe*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 通过实现支持反向模式微分的空间电荷模拟，研究发现内存使用随宏粒子数、网格单元数和空间电荷踢数线性增长，这有助于评估给定硬件内存条件下可微模拟的可行性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 随着粒子加速器可微模拟代码的发展，基于梯度的优化工作流提供了更精细的控制和更真实的设施建模能力，但在使用反向模式自动微分时，内存使用会持续增加，特别是在包含计算成本较高的空间电荷效应时，可能超出硬件内存限制。

Method: 在基于PyTorch的束流追踪代码Cheetah中实现空间电荷模拟，该代码支持反向模式微分，以此来研究可微模拟的内存需求。

Result: 反向模式微分的内存使用量与宏粒子数量、网格单元数以及模拟中涉及的空间电荷作用次数成线性增长关系。

Conclusion: 这些内存使用的标度规律可用于评估在给定硬件内存约束下，特定可微模拟是否可行。

Abstract: The recent development of differentiable simulation codes for particle accelerators has enabled gradient-based workflows that promise finer control and more realistic modeling of accelerator facilities. However, when using reverse-mode automatic differentiation, the memory usage continuously increases during the simulation, and can potentially exceed the available hardware memory -- especially when costly space charge computation is included. To study the memory requirements for differentiable simulations, we have implemented space charge in Cheetah, a PyTorch-based beam tracking code that supports reverse-mode differentiation. We find that the memory usage for reverse-mode differentiation grows linearly with the number of macroparticles and cells, and that it is proportional to the number of space charge kicks involved in the simulation. This general scaling can be used to evaluate whether a given differentiable simulation is feasible given hardware memory constraints.

### [2] [Enhancement of Ponderomotive End Plugs with Low-Mass Dopants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26472)
*Ian E. Ochs,Elijah J. Kolmes,Nathaniel J. Fisch*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 在ponderomotive端塞中使用掺杂剂可以逆转ambipolar电势，显著增强轴向端部约束性能


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统ponderomotive端塞产生排斥电势，但ambipolar电势会部分抵消该电势，研究者希望增强端塞约束

Method: 利用垂直于磁场极化的高频波在轴向端部产生排斥势垒，并通过掺杂调控ambipolar电势以增强势阱

Result: 掺杂能够逆转ambipolar电势的符号，使其从削弱转变为增强ponderomotive端塞约束

Conclusion: 掺杂可以显著增强端塞的ponderomotive约束，这是一种有前景的改善线性等离子体约束性能的方法

Abstract: During conventional confinement of a linear plasma using ponderomotive end plugs, a repulsive potential is created at the axial ends by employing a perpendicularly-polarized wave with a wave frequency greater than the ion gyrofrequency. This potential is then partially cancelled out by an ambipolar potential that arises to equilibrate electron and ion densities along the field line. However, recent work on centrifugally-confined plasmas has shown that the appropriate choice of a dopant in the end region can dramatically change the structure of the ambipolar potential. For the ponderomotive potential, this ambipolar shaping can be even more powerful, since a lightweight dopant can see a different sign of ponderomotive potential relative to the confined species. As a result, the dopant can reverse the sign of the ambipolar potential, causing it to dramatically enhance the end plug ponderomotive confinement.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索在直接驱动惯性约束聚变中，通过混合波长激光驱动平衡驱动效率与烧蚀稳定性，利用3ω光更深穿透特性改善性能，同时保留2ω操作的能量可及性优势。

Method: 使用FLASH辐射流体动力学代码进行一维模拟，扫描总激光强度（100-1600 TW/cm²）和3ω强度比例（0-100%），厚靶用于烧蚀压力标定，薄膜用于评估加速阶段和Rayleigh-Taylor不稳定性。

The total target-incident laser intensity is varied from 100 to $1600~\mathrm{TW}/\mathrm{cm}^{2}$, and the $3ω$ laser intensity fraction is scanned from 0 to 100\%. Thick-target simulations are used to determine quasi-steady ablation-pressure scalings, while thin-foil simulations are used to characterize the acceleration stage and to evaluate the linear ablative Rayleigh--Taylor instability (RTI) gain using a Takabe-type model. The simulations show that adding a $3ω$ component to a $2ω$-dominated drive increases the effective ablation pressure, enhances the ablation velocity, and reduces the maximum linear RTI gain. Within the present one-dimensional hydrodynamic model, the mixed drive also reduces the target-incident energy required to accelerate the foil to $300~\mathrm{km}/\mathrm{s}$, especially at high intensity. This improvement is attributed to the deeper penetration of $3ω$ light, which deposits energy closer to the dense ablation region and enhances conductive heat transport toward the ablation front. These results suggest that mixed-wavelength drive can recover much of the favorable hydrodynamic performance of $3ω$ irradiation while retaining part of the energy-accessibility advantage of $2ω$ operation, providing an additional design space of freedom for direct-drive target optimization.

### [4] [On Phase Transition of ITG Turbulence in the Dimits shift](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26765)
*L. N. Marquant,P. Morel,Ö. D. Gürcan*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 通过改变离子温度梯度研究湍流和纬向流主导态之间的转变，发现三种不同状态：高梯度下的湍流态、低梯度下的纬向流主导态（热输运大幅降低）、以及两者间歇切换的中间态。使用纬向流与总自由能比值作为序参数分类，观察到滞后现象：一旦形成纬向流，可在更高梯度下持续存在，湍流谱在不同状态下呈现不同特征。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究等离子体中湍流和纬向流之间的状态转变，探索不同温度梯度下系统的动力学行为，揭示纬向流形成后抑制湍流的记忆效应和滞后现象。

Method: 采用非线性回活动力学模拟，通过独立梯度扫描改变离子温度梯度，定义纬向流与总自由能比值作为序参数，分析从高梯度到低梯度再返回的渐变过程中系统响应。

Result: 识别出三个明显区域：高梯度湍流态、低梯度纬向流主导态（热输运减少）、中间间歇态。纬向流形成后可在更高梯度下持续，产生滞后回线。不同状态下自由能径向波数谱呈现不同特征：纬向流态集中最大径向尺度且谱斜率陡峭；湍流态谱更宽且有两个不同斜率段。

Conclusion: 等离子体湍流-纬向流转变存在滞后现象，纬向流的持续性导致系统对梯度变化的非对称响应。中间态的特征为两种状态的间歇切换，表明系统在临界区域存在复杂的动力学行为。

Abstract: The transition between turbulent and zonal flow dominated states is investigated by varying the ion temperature gradient in nonlinear gyrokinetic simulations. Independent gradient scans reveal three distinct regimes: a turbulent regime at high gradients, a zonal flow dominated regime with strongly reduced heat transport at low gradients, and an intermediate regime characterized by intermittent switching between these two states. These different regimes can be classified using an order parameter, defined as the fraction of the zonal to total free energy in the system. To assess the memory effects, the temperature gradient is first lowered gradually from high values that result in fully developed turbulence, to lower values in the Dimits shift region that form strong zonal flows, and then is slowly increased back. Once the zonal flows form, and efficiently suppress turbulence, they can persist at higher gradients, leading to an asymmetric response implying a hysteresis loop. It is observed that, in the zonal flow dominated state, the free energy is mostly condensated in the largest radial scale, with a steep slope of the $k_x$ spectrum, while in the turbulent state, it exhibits a wider spectrum with two distinct slopes.

### [5] [ITER ECE front-end design, alignment and in-situ calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27089)

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为ITER设计可靠的电子回旋辐射诊断系统，通过准光学方法实现对电子温度的绝对测量，提高空间分辨率和诊断功能，以支持等离子体物理研究和聚变反应堆运行。

Method: 基于场停概念的准光学系统设计，使用高斯光束传输准则对原位热校准源进行优化，并通过集成前端准光学系统和偏振分离单元、传输线、开关场等组件形成完整光路。

Result: 实现了基于场停概念的前端准光学系统设计，可定义高斯光束变化、设定ECE采样体积和空间分辨率。原位热校准源提供独立的绝对电子温度测量。倾斜ECE视图增强了对非热电子群体的灵敏度，并补充了对新古典撕裂模的诊断。

Abstract: The electron cyclotron emission (ECE) diagnostics suite at ITER utilizes a front-end quasi-optical (QO) system whose design is fundamentally constrained by a field-stop concept. The field-stop defines the Gaussian beam variation throughout the optical system and within the plasma, thereby setting the ECE sampling volume and spatial resolution. An in-situ hot calibration source, optimized using Gaussian beam transmission criteria, provides independent and absolute electron temperature measurements. The QO system extends beyond the front-end to include the polarization splitter unit (PSU), transmission lines, and switchyard, forming an integrated optical path to the ECE instruments. Misalignment between the front-end and PSU reduces the effective field-stop size, degrading spatial resolution and measurement fidelity. The oblique ECE view, a key feature of the ITER design, enhances sensitivity to non-thermal electron populations and complements the diagnosis of neoclassical tearing modes. Integrated QO design and plasma physics understanding are essential for reliable ITER ECE measurements.
