*Giuseppe Lerner*

Main category: physics.acc-ph

TL;DR: 本文探讨高能高强粒子加速器中束流损失机制及其对安全高效运行的影响，重点分析设备损坏、电子器件辐射效应和辐射防护风险。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 高能高强粒子加速器运行中不可避免的束流损失会带来设备损坏、辐射风险等重大挑战，需要系统研究以确保安全高效运行，特别是对于大型强子对撞机及未来加速器设施。

Method: 基于粒子-物质相互作用基础理论，综述高能高强粒子加速器中主要束流损失机制，分析其对设备安全和辐射防护的影响，并特别关注强子加速器，以大强子对撞机为主要案例，同时讨论未来环形对撞机和μ子对撞机等新设施。

Result: 系统总结了加速器中束流损失的主要机制及后果，为评估和缓解相关风险提供了理论框架，特别强调了大型强子对撞机运行中的实际挑战及辐射防护的重要性。

Conclusion: 高能高强加速器的安全高效运行需要全面理解束流损失机制，这对当前设施（如大型强子对撞机）和未来加速器设计都具有重要意义，特别是在辐射防护和设备保护方面。

Abstract: The operation of high-energy and high-intensity particle accelerators inevitably leads to the loss of a fraction of beam particles, either through controlled processes or accidental events. This article builds on a first lecture on particle-matter interactions to review the main beam loss mechanisms in high-energy and high-intensity accelerators and their implications for safe and efficient operation. It discusses the resulting risks of equipment and material damage, radiation effects on electronics, and radiation-protection hazards. The focus is on beam losses in hadron accelerators, with particular emphasis on the Large Hadron Collider at CERN, while also addressing proposed future facilities such as the Future Circular Collider and muon colliders.

### [4] [3D modelling of thermal loads during unmitigated vertical displacement events in ITER and JET](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22516)

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: ITER的破裂热负荷三维预测技术仍不完善，需要可靠的物理模型来评估钨第一壁的韧性和安全余量

Method: 结合MHD垂直位移事件模拟、三维场线追踪以及瞬态壁面热响应，在JET上使用铍壁进行验证后应用于ITER钨壁条件

Result: 方法成功重现JET的关键全局动力学和非对称电流特征，验证了熔融预测能力；ITER分析显示钨第一壁具备足够韧性抵抗破裂热负荷

Abstract: Predicting three-dimensional thermal loads during tokamak disruptions is essential for ITER yet remains weakly developed. The resulting analysis demonstrates the resilience of the ITER W first wall against these events and provides predictions for the energy deposition and current flow profiles. ### [5] [Scaling laws of multi-shock implosions toward the quasi-isentropic limit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22592)
*M. Murakami*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 本文提出用于自相似多重激波内爆的统一理论框架


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 开发一种能实现超高压缩同时抑制热力学熵增和Rayleigh-Taylor不稳定的新压缩方案

Method: 将经典Guderley模型扩展到N个叠加的球面汇聚冲击波，推导自相似解和最终密度标度律，并进行一维拉格朗日流体动力学模拟验证

Result: 累积压缩随堆叠冲击波数量增加而系统提升，熵产生被强烈抑制，N→∞时接近准等熵极限，并且大幅抑制了基于壳层内爆的Rayleigh-Taylor不稳定性

Conclusion: 该框架为惯性约束聚变和高能量密度系统提供了稳健的、几乎无失真的压缩途径，并连接了相似性理论与实际多重激波动力学

Abstract: We present a unified theoretical and numerical framework for self-similar multi-shock implosions achieving ultrahigh compression in a uniform solid spherical target. Extending the classical Guderley model to N stacked, spherically converging shocks, we derive selfsimilar solutions and the scaling law for the final density. One dimensional Lagrangian hydrodynamic simulations confirm this relation over a broad range of parameters, from the weakly to the strongly nonlinear regime. The results show that cumulative compression increases systematically with the number of stacked shocks while entropy generation is strongly suppressed, asymptotically approaching a quasi isentropic limit as N increases infinity. This volumetric scheme strongly suppresses the Rayleigh Taylor instability that plagues shell based implosions and thus provides a robust, largely instability-resistant compression pathway applicable to inertial confinement fusion and other high energy density systems. The framework bridges similarity theory with realistic multi-shock dynamics, guiding the design of advanced laser-driven compression schemes.

### [6] [Revisiting confinement scalings and fusion performance with a perspective optimized for extrapolation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22728)
*Jalal Butt,Geert Verdoolaege,Ghent University,Stanley M. Kaye,Egemen Kolemen*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究系统地寻找了最小复杂度聚变约束标度模型，发现以电流为主的关键参数支配聚变性能，暗示高场强金属壁托卡马克可能需要比预期更高的等离子体电流才能实现GW级聚变功率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 高温超导技术进步使更高环向磁场成为可能，重新开启了紧凑高场托卡马克反应堆的设计空间。基于最新的ITPA H模约束数据库更新，需要重新评估实验记录及其不确定性对聚变堆性能路径的启示。

Method: 采用外推导向的视角重新审视约束标度，系统搜寻能在方差捕获和外推稳健性间取得最优平衡的最小复杂度约束标度模型；构建跨装置的聚变功率经验标度作为直接性能衡量标准。

Result: 研究发现N=3到N=4阶的低阶模型最优，等离子体电流、装置尺寸、加热功率和拉长率是关键工程参数；金属壁会导致约束惩罚；聚变三重积近似比例于Ip²，聚变功率标度在多个装置最高性能放电中也呈现近二次方依赖关系。

Conclusion: 高场金属壁装置可能需要比基于IPB98(y,2)标准的预期更高的等离子体电流，实现GW级托卡马克性能可能需要Ip ≥ 20MA的运行条件。

Abstract: Recent advances in high-temperature-superconductor technology have made substantially higher toroidal magnetic fields technologically accessible, reopening the design space for compact, high-field tokamak reactors. Because reactor performance projections remain anchored to empirical confinement scalings, the recent update to the ITPA global H-mode confinement database raises an important question: what does the present experimental record and its uncertainty imply for the path to reactor-grade fusion performance? In this work, we revisit confinement extrapolation from an explicitly extrapolation-oriented perspective and, to complement its implications in terms of a direct reactor performance measure, present a cross-machine empirical scaling for fusion power. We systematically search for a minimally complex confinement scaling that optimizes the tradeoff between variance capture and extrapolative robustness. We find that low-order models centered near $N=3$ to $N=4$ optimize this tradeoff, with plasma current, machine size, heating power, and elongation emerging as the dominant engineering levers, together with an empirically inferred confinement penalty associated with metallic walls. Recast in reactor-performance terms, the results indicate that both the fusion triple product and fusion power are governed primarily by plasma current: the triple product scales approximately as $I_p^2$, and the empirical fusion power scaling exhibits a similarly near-quadratic dependence over a survey of the highest performing discharges across several machines. Projecting to reactors, these results suggest that high-field devices with metal walls may require higher plasma current than standard IPB98$(y,2)$-based expectations imply, and that gigawatt-class tokamak performance likely demands operation at $I_p \gtrsim 20\mathrm{MA}$.

</details>
